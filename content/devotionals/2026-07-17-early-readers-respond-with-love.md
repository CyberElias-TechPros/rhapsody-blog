Sometimes people may laugh at you,

ignore you, or say unkind things. In

those moments, you have a choice. You

can react in anger, or you can respond

the way Jesus taught.

When Jesus was nailed to the cross,

He did something unexpected. Instead

of speaking harshly, He prayed for the

people who hurt Him: “Father, forgive

them…” (Luke 23:34). He showed that

love is stronger than hate.

When you choose to forgive, stay

calm, and speak kindly, you show that

God’s love lives in you. Love changes

situations. It also changes hearts. And

when you respond with love, you shine Scan tolike Jesus.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you that your love fills my heart. I express it through my words, thoughts, and actions today and always, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
