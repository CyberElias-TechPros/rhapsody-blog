God’s Word is full of treasures that can help you live a great life. However, you can only enjoy these treasures if you take the time to study and meditate on God’s Word for yourself.

Timothy is a great example of someone who gave attention to God’s Word. As a young child, he learned the Scriptures from his mother and grandmother. God’s Word helped him grow into a strong and wise young man. Later on, he became a great helper to Apostle Paul and a leader in the church (2 Timothy 3:15).

You can be like Timothy too! So, start today. Set time every day to study God’s Word. The more of God’s Word in you, the more you’ll grow in wisdom, faith, and strength.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for giving me your Word to build me up and nourish me for the life of greatness you’ve planned for me. My mind is renewed right now by the power of your Word, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
