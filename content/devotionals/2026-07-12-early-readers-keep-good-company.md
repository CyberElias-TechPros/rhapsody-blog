As God’s child, the friends you choose

are important. God cares about the

people you spend time with, because

He wants you to grow strong and live

an excellent life. That’s why the Bible

warns us about bad company: “…bad

company corrupts good behavior” (1

Corinthians 15:33).

Look at Daniel. Even though he lived

among people who didn’t honor God,

he chose friends who loved God and

made wise decisions (Daniel 1:8–17).

Because of that, he had good company

to pray with.

So, choose friends who love God,

encourage you to do what is right, and get children’sgrow in His Word. Good friends help other languages!you shine brighter for Jesus every day!

## Confession

I’m guided and led in the right path by God’s Word, and as such, I only keep friends that love the Lord God and follow His Words.
