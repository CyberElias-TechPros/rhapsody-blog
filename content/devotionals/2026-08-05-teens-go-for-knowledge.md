Every day, satellites orbiting the Earth transmit huge amounts of data that help scientists predict storms, guide aircraft, and monitor the planet. Without this information, even the most advanced technology would struggle to work effectively. In many ways, access to the right knowledge determines whether systems succeed or fail. The same principle applies to life.

The knowledge of the Word of God is vital to living a successful and victorious life. Unfortunately, some people inadvertently ignore knowledge, and they’re the ones who are called “fools.” If you were to ask some teenagers why things aren’t working out for them, they’d have all sorts of reasons: “Oh, my teachers are mean,” or “I’m from a disadvantaged home”, and so on. If you were to ask them, “When last did you study your Bible, or listen to a message?” you’re sure to find out that such folks aren’t consistent in taking in God’s Word. Though they might not know it, they have been ignoring knowledge.

Any person who ignores knowledge is setting up himself for the hard life and that’s why the Lord said, “My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge...” (Hosea 4:6). The reason for the hard life many are facing isn’t the lack of prayer or power, but the lack of the knowledge of God’s word, which is revelation knowledge. In your life, don’t ignore knowledge. If you’re that way, God won’t give you more of it. Jesus said, “Do not give what is holy to the dogs; nor cast your pearls before swine” (Matthew 7:6); in other words, He will not give precious revelations to the man who despises knowledge.

Go for the knowledge of God’s Word with all your heart. If you find that you spend all your free time on social media or gaming at the expense of God’s Word, then you’re ignoring knowledge. The cost of getting a digital message or a Christian book that’ll change your life is a little compared to the everlasting impact it will make in your life. Invest in obtaining materials that have the potential to transform your life forever. Always remember that the knowledge of God’s Word is the key.

## Prayer

Dear Father, my heart goes after your Word. I thank you because I know that as I receive more of your Word, I grow in grace and in the knowledge of Jesus Christ. My heart is ever open and receptive to your Word, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Today, decide to invest in a resource that’ll build your knowledge of God’s Word. It could be a book, an audio or video message by Pastor Chris, or a conference.
