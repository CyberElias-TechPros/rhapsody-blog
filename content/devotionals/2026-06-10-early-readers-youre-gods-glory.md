The Bible tells us that Jesus shows us exactly what God is like. He is the exact picture of who God is (Hebrews 1:3). And here’s something amazing, God created us to look like Him too! Because as Jesus is, so are we in this world (1 John 4:17).

Jesus said, “...The glory You gave Me, I have given them...” (John 17:22). That means God’s glory is in you too; therefore, you’re not ordinary. God chose you to shine His light and show His goodness everywhere you go (1 Peter 2:9).

Therefore, every time you study God’s Word, it’s like looking into a mirror. It shows you who you really are; someone filled with God’s glory. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for your glory in my life and for making me a shining light in my school, family, and community to bless lives, in Jesus’ Name. Amen. 1515
