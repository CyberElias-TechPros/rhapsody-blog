The Scriptures contain several accounts of men and women who experienced divine visitations. Sometimes it was a visit by an angel; at other times, it was the Lord Himself. The Bible tells us about Abraham, who, on a certain day, sat in his tent door in the heat of the day and saw three men passing by. By faith, he perceived they weren’t ordinary men and called out to them to stop over for some refreshments (Genesis 18:1-5). It turned out that one of the three men was the Lord Himself, while the other two were angels. But guess what? It was during that encounter that the Lord told Abraham exactly when Sarah would conceive and give birth (Genesis 18:10).

When Jacob was at a crossroads in his life, it took a divine visitation from the Lord for him to experience the turnaround he had long desired. He was alone one day when an angel came to him and wrestled with him all night long (Genesis 32:24). At the break of dawn, he realised the angel wasn’t an ordinary angel but the Spirit of God Himself, and pleaded with Him to bless him. That was the day the Lord changed his name from “Jacob”, which hitherto meant “usurper”’ or “swindler”, to “Israel,” which means “a prince of power with God.” That divine visitation changed his destiny and set him on the path of greatness.

We’re in the last days, and God wants to visit you and touch your life in a special way. Divine visitations weren’t reserved only for folks in the Old Testament; you can expect to be visited by God as well. A divine encounter with the Lord will definitely change your life forever. He can visit you in your room, at school, on your way home, or even while you sleep! But you can be sure that when He does, your life won’t remain the same. Glory to God!

## Speak

Precious Holy Spirit, I’m sensitive and alert in my spirit to recognise moments of divine visitations so that I’ll receive every blessing that you desire to impart into my life. I recognise that this is my season for divine visitations, and I welcome all such visitations, in the Name of the Lord Jesus. Amen.

## Act

Today, be intentionally kind and open to everyone you meet. You never know when you might be entertaining an angel.
