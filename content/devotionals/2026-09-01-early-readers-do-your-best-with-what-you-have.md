Jesus once told a parable about servants

who were given talents (Matthew 25:14–30).

Each servant received talents according to

his ability. Some received more, and some

received less, but their master wanted the

same thing from all of them—faithfulness.

The servants who used what they were

given and made it grow were praised. But

one servant hid his talent and did nothing

with it. He gave excuses and was unfaithful.

God has given you something special too.

The Lord expects you to use what He’s given

you well. For example, whatever you learn

from God’s Word, practice it and share it

with others.

Always be faithful and give your best to books!the Lord in everything you do.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for helping me to be faithful. I give my best in everything I do, and I serve you with all my heart, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
