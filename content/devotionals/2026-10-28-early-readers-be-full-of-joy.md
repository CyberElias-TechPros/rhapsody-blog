You’re meant to be full of joy at all times. As God’s child, joy is part of your life in Christ—it’s already in you. Even when things don’t go the way you expect, don’t give in to sadness or anger; choose joy (James 1:2).

Think about Paul and Silas. They were in prison, yet they sang and praised God because their joy didn’t depend on their situation (Acts 16:25–26).

So let your joy show! Smile, sing, and thank God every day. The more you express joy, the happier you become.

## Confession

No matter what happens, I’m full of joy. I’m happy, and I live in God’s goodness every day. Hallelujah!
