The Holy Spirit is your best friend and guide. He helps you know what to do, where to go, and what to say. When you listen to Him, you’ll always make the right choices.

He may speak to your heart quietly or give you a gentle thought about something you should or shouldn’t do. When He does, obey Him quickly. The more you follow His leading, the stronger and wiser you’ll become.

So, talk to the Holy Spirit every day. Ask Him to guide you, and you’ll always walk in success and peace.

## Confession

The Holy Spirit lives in me and leads me every day. I follow His voice and walk in wisdom and victory!
