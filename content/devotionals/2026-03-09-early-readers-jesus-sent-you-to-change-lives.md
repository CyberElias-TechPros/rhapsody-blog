After Jesus rose from the dead, He gave us a special mission—to tell the whole world about Him. That means every Christian, no matter how young, is a messenger of God’s love.

When you share the Gospel of the Lord Jesus with a friend, invite someone to church, or pray for others, you’re helping to change lives! You’re part of God’s big plan to fill the earth with His glory.

Never think you’re too small to make a difference. God’s Spirit in you gives you boldness and wisdom to speak His Word with power.

## Confession

I’m sent by Jesus to share His love and truth. I’m bold to tell others about Him everywhere I go.
