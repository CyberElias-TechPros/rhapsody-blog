Notice that the scripture above doesn’t only speak of receiving righteousness; it also speaks of receiving abundance of grace. Righteousness is a settled gift imparted to you at the New Birth. Grace, however, is God's limitless supply for your life, and He wants you to take full advantage of it. That's why He says, "...be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus" (2 Timothy 2:1).

Don’t limit God’s grace. Grace is available to every Christian, and you can receive as much of it as you can take a hold of. The Greek word is “Lambano”; it means to take hold of something and make it yours. God isn't limiting His grace, so don't limit your reception of it. Keep receiving more and more grace for greater strength, greater wisdom, greater effectiveness, and greater victories in your walk with Him.

Sadly, some approach God with hesitation. They say, "The Lord has already done so much for me; I shouldn't ask Him for too much." Others even feel apologetic whenever they pray for greater opportunities, bigger dreams, or increased resources. But that's not the language of faith, nor is it the mindset of grace.

God has never asked us to lower our expectations. Rather, He invites us to ask, receive, and walk in the fullness of His provisions. The Bible says, “But he giveth more grace…” (James 4:6). He said in Hebrews 4:16, “Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need.” Then in John 16:24, the Lord Jesus said, “…ask, and ye shall receive, that your joy may be full.”

Never think your requests are too great for God or that your dreams exceed His ability. He delights in your faith. He’s the one who said, "Ask of me, and I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance, and the uttermost parts of the earth for thy possession" (Psalm 2:8). He has more than enough for every one of His children. Therefore, refuse to think small.

Don't limit God's grace by limiting your faith. Keep receiving from His inexhaustible supply, confident that His grace is available to you in abundance for every assignment and every opportunity He's given you.

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for your abundant grace at work in my life. I boldly receive all that you have made available to me in Christ. I refuse to limit my faith or my expectations but continually take full advantage of your grace, walking in the fullness of your wisdom, strength, provision and victory, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
