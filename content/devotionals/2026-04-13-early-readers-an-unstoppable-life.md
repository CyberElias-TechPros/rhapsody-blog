The moment you were born again, God gave you the greatest gift—eternal life! This life is called Zoe, God’s very own life. And because you have this life in you, you’re unstoppable! Nothing can defeat you or keep you down.

Adam’s disobedience brought death into the world, but Jesus came and changed everything. His obedience and sacrifice brought eternal life to everyone who believes in Him (Romans 5:17–19). Now, you’re not ordinary—you have God’s life in you!

So rejoice! Eternal life is working in you right now. It makes you bold, strong, victorious, and truly unstoppable.

## Confession

I have Zoe, the life of God, in me; therefore, I’m victorious, unstoppable, and indestructible. Hallelujah!
