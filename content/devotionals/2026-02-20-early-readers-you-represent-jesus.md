An ambassador is someone who represents his country in a foreign land. You’re God’s ambassador here on earth! That means when people see you, they should see Jesus’ love, kindness, and peace.

You carry His message to the world. When you speak God’s Word, pray for others, or share His love, you’re doing what Jesus would do.

So, be proud of who you are. Everywhere you go, represent Jesus with your words, actions, and smile!

## Confession

I’m God’s ambassador! I shine His light and share His love everywhere I go. Hallelujah!
