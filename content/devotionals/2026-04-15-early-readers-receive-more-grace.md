God doesn’t want you to have just a little grace—He wants His grace to keep increasing in your life! The more you know Him through His Word, the more His grace multiplies in everything you do.

Think about Peter. He was once an ordinary fisherman, but as he followed Jesus and listened to His teachings, something happened—he grew in grace! Soon, he became bold and full of God’s power, preaching the Gospel and healing the sick. God’s grace changed him from ordinary to extraordinary!

This is what grace does for you, too. As you spend time reading your Bible, meditating on God’s Word, and speaking it every day, His grace grows stronger in you—helping you shine, succeed, and live the glorious life He planned for you.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for your grace at work in my life. As I study and meditate on your Word, your grace increases in me every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
