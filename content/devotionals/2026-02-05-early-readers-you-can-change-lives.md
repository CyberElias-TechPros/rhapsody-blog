When Jesus said, “Go and make disciples of all nations,” He gave us a very special job—to teach and train others in the ways of God’s Kingdom. That means you’re not just any child; you’re a leader for Jesus!

The Holy Spirit in you makes you strong, wise, and full of boldness to show others how to live for God. You’re God’s champion, sent to change lives and reveal His love and power to the world. Hallelujah!

Every time you share the Gospel, pray for someone, or show kindness, you’re revealing God’s love. Even the angels in heaven rejoice when you do these things. So, don’t keep quiet about Jesus. Let your words and actions show His love everywhere you go!

## Confession

I’m a champion for Jesus! His Spirit in me makes me bold to share His love and show His power everywhere I go. Glory to God!
