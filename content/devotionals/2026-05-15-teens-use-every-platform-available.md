In this era of unprecedented connectivity, the world has become a global village. From the palm of your hand, you can reach someone across the oceans, share a message, and spark a transformation. Imagine the Apostle Paul’s heart for evangelism in today’s context! He was relentless in his pursuit to reach every soul for Christ, adapting to every city, culture, and circumstance without compromising the Gospel. Today, we have tools that Paul could only have dreamed of — technology, social media, podcasts, blogs, and more — all at our fingertips.

Beloved, the world is more accessible than ever before. These platforms are not mere trends or distractions; they’re divine appointments and strategic tools for soul winning. The Apostle Paul said in 1 Corinthians 9:22 NKJV, “I have become all things to all men, that I might by all means save some.” This is our mandate too! Your voice can now travel faster than your feet ever could. A single post, a shared Scripture, or a prophetic word sent through your phone can ignite faith in a stranger’s heart miles away.

Don’t underestimate the power of your digital ministry. Whether it’s sending out digital copies of Rhapsody of Realities TeeVo, posting a devotional on your timeline, or sharing a quick teaching clip, the Holy Spirit will breathe life into your efforts. Souls are being drawn closer to Christ through your obedience, even when you don’t see the full impact.

As we stand in these last days of the Church age, let us be intentional, creative, and consistent. Use every platform God places in your hands to preach the full Gospel. Let your gifts, talents, and influence become the channels through which many are saved. The harvest is ripe—go forth and reap!

## Speak

I maximize every opportunity and platform to preach the Gospel. I am wise, creative and effective in reaching people for Christ. Multitudes are being saved daily through me. Hallelujah.

## Act

Today, make a conscious decision to use your phone or social media to share at least one Scripture, devotional thought, or gospel message, trusting the Holy Spirit to touch a life through you.
