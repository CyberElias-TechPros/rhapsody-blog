Being content means not feeling sad or jealous because someone else has something you don’t. It means trusting that God has given you everything you need.

In 1 Corinthians 3:21, the Bible says, “… all things are yours.” Which means in Christ, you’re richly blessed and never empty. Hallelujah!

Peter showed this when a man asked him for money. Peter said he didn’t have silver or gold, but he had something greater—Jesus! And through the Name of Jesus, the man was healed (Acts 3:1–10).

You also carry something precious in you. So, be thankful, joyful, and condent. With Jesus, you have more than enough.

## Confession

I have all that I need to live a beautiful, excellent, and glorious life. Therefore, I’m joyful and full of thanksgiving always! Hallelujah!
