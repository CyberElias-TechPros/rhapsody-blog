Askilled architect never begins construction without first seeing the building in his mind. Before the first brick is laid, the vision is clear—every line, every level, every purpose carefully imagined. In the same way, God has designed the human spirit to see before it experiences. When you pray in tongues, you’re not merely uttering sounds; you’re engaging a divine system for creating realities.

Did you know that you can actually create visions while speaking in tongues? This is one reason you must let the Holy Spirit guide your mind in prayer. There's a difference between a spiritual vision from God that He shows you through your spirit and a spiritual vision that your spirit has the capacity to create. These are two different things.

Many times, we wait only for the visions that God gives, but He doesn’t grant those kinds of visions every day because they are specific to certain kinds of guidance. However, through the new birth, God has lifted you into His class of being. We’re associates of the God-kind, partakers of the divine nature (2 Peter 1:4). And as God is, a creator, so are you. This means you can actually use your mind to create visions for what you want to see in your schoolwork, relationships, ministry, and future. So when you speak in tongues, one of two things should be expected: you can create a vision with your mind, your spirit-led imagination, or the Holy Spirit Himself reveals something to you.

So, if there are circumstances you desire to create or change, whether in your church, your city, your life, or even in a nation, in your academics, character, and personal growth, speak in tongues. And through the Spirit, create the vision. Then discern by the Spirit if that vision aligns with the will of God. If it does, step into it and bring it to pass.

For example, maybe you’re praying for your city because you want to see a mighty move of God and the expansion of God’s Kingdom there. Or perhaps you’re praying about your exams, your career path, or your future. As you pray, use your imaginative power to create the visions of your desire.

## Speak

As I speak in other tongues, I create visions and realities that are consistent with the perfect will of God. My spirit is synchronised with the Holy Spirit, and I function in higher realms of creativity, revelation and influence. I chart the course of my life, my education, relationships, and future, and my world in alignment with God’s plans and purposes. I walk in perpetual victory and continual advancement by the Spirit. Amen.

## Act

Take 5–10 minutes today to pray in tongues. Pay attention to the pictures or ideas that come to your heart. Write down the ones that align with God’s Word and act on them in faith.
