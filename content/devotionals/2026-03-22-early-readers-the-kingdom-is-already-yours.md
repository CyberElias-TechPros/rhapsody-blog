When you were born again, God made you a part of His Kingdom. That means you now belong to a place where Jesus is Lord and where peace, joy, and love rule! You’re not waiting to enter God’s Kingdom someday—you’re already in it.

The Bible says that God has delivered us from darkness and brought us into the Kingdom of His dear Son (Colossians 1:13). You’re not a stranger in this Kingdom; you’re a full citizen with special rights and blessings.

The Kingdom of God isn’t far away—it’s in you! That’s why you can walk in victory, talk with confidence, and share God’s love with others. God is happy for you to have His Kingdom because He wants you to live in joy and greatness every day.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for bringing me into your Kingdom and filling my heart with joy and peace. I live every day with confidence, knowing your Kingdom is in me, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.29
