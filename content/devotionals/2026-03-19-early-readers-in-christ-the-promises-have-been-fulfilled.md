Every promise God made in the Bible has already come true in Christ Jesus! That means you don’t have to wait or wonder when God will do something—He already has. When you received Jesus, you received everything God promised: His love, peace, joy, health, and blessings.

The Bible says God’s divine power has given us all things that we need for life and godliness (2 Peter 1:3). So, instead of asking God to do what He’s already done, start thanking Him and walking in those blessings.

You’re not trying to be blessed—you already are! You belong to Jesus, and all things are yours. So, talk like it, act like it, and rejoice because God’s promises have been fulfilled in your life!

## Prayer

Dear gracious Father, thank you for fulfilling all your promises in Christ. I walk in your blessings and enjoy everything you’ve given me—peace, joy, health, and success—every day, 26in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

Romans 8:32; Galatians 3:14
