The Bible says you’re a priest. This means you belong to God and have a special role in serving Him. You don’t have to wait until you’re older—you can serve God right now.

As God’s child, you can pray for others, share God’s Word, and praise Him with your words. Always remember that God has made us kings and priests to Him (Revelation 1:6). This means you can help others know Him and show His love every day.

So, pray, speak His Word, and praise Him always. You’re God’s representative in your world!

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for choosing me and making me a royal priest. I show your love and speak your Word everywhere I go, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
