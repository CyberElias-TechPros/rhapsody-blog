You’re God’s child, and you have His nature now. This means His love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, and goodness are already inside you.

So you don’t have to say, “I can’t love people” or “I don’t have patience.” God has already put these good things in you. Rather, you can say, “I’m full of love and peace. God’s nature is in me.”

The more you believe and say what God’s Word says about you, the more those good things show in the way you talk, act, and treat others.

Remember, you’re not trying to get God’s nature—you already have it! Allow His light to radiate from within you daily.

## Confession

Thank you, precious Father, for putting your nature in me. I’m full of love, peace, gentleness, goodness, and faith. Glory to God!
