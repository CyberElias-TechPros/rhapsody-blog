God wants His Word to be seen in your life. The Bible speaks of the manifestation of the truth, meaning showing God’s truth in the way you live. It’s more than just saying the right things; it’s living the Word every day.

When you speak kindly, show love, forgive others, and act on what the Bible says, you’re expressing God’s Word. Your life becomes like a letter that people can read, showing them God’s goodness. That’s what Christianity is about—living in the Word and letting it shine through you everywhere you go.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for your Word that lives in me. My life shows your truth, love, and goodness everywhere I go, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
