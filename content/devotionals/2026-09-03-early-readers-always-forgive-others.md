One day, Peter asked Jesus how many times he should forgive someone who wronged him (Matthew 18:21–22). Jesus answered, “Seventy times seven.” That doesn’t mean you should start counting! It means you should always forgive.

Forgiveness is something you do all the time. As God’s child, you can choose to forgive quickly and not stay angry.

The Bible says we shouldn’t let the sun go down while we’re still angry (Ephesians 4:26). That means don’t stay upset for long. Even if someone makes the same mistake again, choose to forgive them again.

Jesus forgave us, so we also forgive others (Colossians 3:13). Let His love in your heart help you forgive freely and gladly.

## Confession

God’s love is in my heart, and it helps me forgive others easily. I show kindness and love every day. Hallelujah!
