God wants you to enjoy your life and stay joyful every day, no matter what happens around you. His Word shows us simple steps to live happily. Today’s memory scripture teaches us to watch the words we speak, choose what is right, and look for peace in every situation.

A great example is Joseph. Even when his brothers were unkind to him, Joseph didn’t speak ill of them or fight back. Rather, he stayed close to God, chose to do good, and kept a peaceful heart. And God turned everything around for his good (Genesis 45:4-8).

Just like Joseph, your words and actions matter. Speak kindly, do what’s right, and keep peace with others. As you do God’s Word every day, your life will be full of joy, strength, and beautiful blessings!

## Confession

I live a beautiful, exciting, joyful, and wonderful life! And as I follow God’s Word, His glory is seen in me everywhere I go. Amen.
