Sometimes, some bad stuff happens, and people don’t know that the devil is behind it. But here’s the good news: the Lord has given us the ability to know when he’s the cause; and more so, He has given us the power to deal with it: cast out the cause of the problem— the devil!

As a child of God, exercise your authority in Christ Jesus. Use the Name of Jesus against any activity of the devil in your home, school, community or anywhere around you. When you command the devil to leave, he’ll obey!

Therefore, don’t be quiet when you see things that aren’t in line with what God’s Word says, whether it has to do with you or others. Speak boldly in Jesus’ Name, and take charge to bring about a change!

## Prayer

In the Name of Jesus, I have power over the devil. He has no place in my life, my home, school, or in the lives of my friends. I live in victory every day. Amen!
