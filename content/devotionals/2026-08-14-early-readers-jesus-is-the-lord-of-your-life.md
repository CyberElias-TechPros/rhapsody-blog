When you receive Jesus, He becomes the Lord of your life. This means you choose to follow Him, and live the way He teaches you.

Jesus guides you through His Word, the Bible. His Word shows you what is right and helps you make good choices every day. When you listen to God’s Word and obey it, you’re showing that Jesus is truly the Lord of your life. His Word will help you grow strong in faith and live a life that pleases God.

Always remember to listen to His Word, and follow it with joy.

## Prayer

Gracious heavenly Father, thank you for Jesus, who is the Lord of my life. Help me always listen to your Word, and follow it with joy every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
