The Name of Jesus is powerful! When you use it, things and situations change for the best. Sickness leaves, fears disappear, and miracles happen.

Peter and John once met a man who couldn’t walk. Peter said, “...In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk” and immediately the man jumped up, completely healed (Acts 3:6).

When you pray or command anything in Jesus’ Name, you’re using His power and authority. So, use His Name with faith and confidence—it still works today!

## Confession

I live victoriously and do great things through the power in the Name of Jesus.
