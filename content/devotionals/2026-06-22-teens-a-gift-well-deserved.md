There’s something profoundly beautiful about the righteousness we have in Christ Jesus: even though it was given to us as a gift, it is a gift well deserved. Someone might say, “But I thought it was an unmerited favour?” Not exactly; righteousness wasn’t given to us as an unmerited favour but as a well-deserved gift of grace, and I’ll explain.

First, understand that Christianity didn’t begin before the Cross, on the Cross, or even from the Cross; it began after the resurrection. What does this mean? When God raised Jesus from the dead, He raised Him with the righteousness of God. And the Bible says we were raised together with Him. When the Lord Jesus was raised from the dead, He was born again. This means that when you gave your life to Christ, you didn’t just join a church; you received a new life and a new identity.

Your old nature was crucified with Him. Everything about you that was condemned or condemnable was nailed to the Cross with Him. Romans 6:4 says, “Therefore we were buried with Him through baptism into death, that just as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life.” So, when Jesus was raised from the dead, we were born into a newness of life; we were born with His righteousness. It wasn’t unmerited!

So, you're not trying to “be good enough” for God. You’re already accepted in Christ. Even when you make mistakes, God doesn’t reject you. We deserved what we got because we’re born of Him. We belong to Him. Let this understanding guide your walk with God! This is the very message He sent us to preach and teach. He wants the world to know that they’ve been made righteous in Christ Jesus.

It’s a gift purchased by the blood of Jesus Christ. Hallelujah! This means you can be confident in school, online, and among your friends, knowing who you are in Christ. You don’t have to follow wrong pressure just to fit in. Praise God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, I thank you for granting me the understanding and revelation of my righteousness in Christ Jesus. Help me remember who I am in You when I’m tempted or feeling insecure.I walk boldly in the consciousness that I was raised together with Christ, justified, perfected, and born righteous. I reign in life, expressing your nature and fulfilling your will with boldness and joy, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Reject old labels of sin or inadequacy; confess and live according to your new creation identity.
