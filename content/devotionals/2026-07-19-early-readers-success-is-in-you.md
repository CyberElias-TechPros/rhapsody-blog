True success doesn’t start with money, school, or where you live; it begins in your heart. The Bible says to guard your heart carefully, because everything you do ows from it (Proverbs 4:23).

God has placed good things inside you. And as you study His Word, it strengthens your spirit and helps you think wisely. That means your success does not depend on what is happening around you. Even when things change, what God has put inside you will show forth.

Jesus said a good person brings good things out of a good heart (Matthew 12:35). So, ll your heart with God’s Word each day. As you do, His wisdom and joy will shine through your life and produce good results.

## Confession

God’s Word is in my heart and in my spirit. Therefore, no matter what happens around me, I only bring out good things. Hallelujah!
