When Jesus was on the earth, He exercised authority over the devil. Whenever He spoke, the devil left, and people were healed. The good news is that when you were born again, Jesus gave you authority in His Name. The Bible says, “…these signs will follow those who believe: In My Name they will cast out demons…” (Mark 16:17). That means you can speak in the Name of Jesus with boldness. Glory to God.

So, if there’s something you don’t want, don’t stay quiet; declare what you want in the Name of Jesus, and stand on God’s Word. Soon, you’ll see the result you desire.

## Prayer

Thank you, precious Father, for giving me authority over the devil. I walk in power today and rebuke the activities of the devil in my school, community, and family, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
