When God blessed Abraham, He made a promise that would reach every nation on earth. That promise was about something very special—the gift of the Holy Spirit!

When you received Jesus as Lord, that promise became yours too. The Holy Spirit came to live in you. He’s not just a power; He’s a Person—God’s presence in you! He helps you understand the Bible, teaches you what to say, and gives you strength to do what’s right.

The Bible says this promise is for everyone who believes in Jesus (Acts 2:39). That means the Holy Spirit is God’s precious gift to you. So, every day, thank Him for being with you, talk to Him, and let Him guide you in all you do.

## Prayer

Thank you, dear gracious Father, for giving me the Holy Spirit to live in me. He teaches, guides, and strengthens me every day. I walk in His power, wisdom, and joy always, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
