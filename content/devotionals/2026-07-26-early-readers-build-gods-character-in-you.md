When you say, “I am God’s child,” or “I am the righteousness of God in Christ,” you’re not pretending. You’re agreeing with what God has already said about you in His Word.

The Bible says in Hebrews 13:5–6, “… For He Himself has said...so we may boldly say…” This means God speaks rst, and then we boldly say the same thing. When you keep saying what God says about you, His Word begins to shape the way you think and act.

And as you keep confessing God’s Word, your words and actions will begin to reect Him every day.

## Prayer

I thank you, precious Father, for giving me your Word and showing me the way to live and enjoy a glorious life, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
