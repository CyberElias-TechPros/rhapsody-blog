When you’re baptized in water, it’s a way of showing everyone that you believe in Jesus! The water doesn’t wash away sin—only Jesus’ blood did that when He died for you.

Going under the water shows that Jesus died and was buried. Coming out of the water shows that He rose again! Baptism is your way of saying: “I believe in what Jesus did for me, and I now live for Him.”

So, when you’re baptized, do it with joy and faith, knowing it’s a special sign that you belong to Jesus.

## Confession

Thank you, precious Lord Jesus, for saving me. I believe in your death, burial, and resurrection, and my life shows your love and victory every day. Amen.
