The blessing of Abraham isn’t just a nice Bible story—it’s the very foundation of the Gospel! When the Lord said to Abraham, “I will bless you and make your name great… and in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed.” (Genesis 12:2–3 NKJV), He wasn’t just talking about material things. He was revealing His plan to pour out His love, grace, and kindness on all humanity through faith in Jesus Christ.

The Apostle Paul explains that this same blessing has come to us through Christ: “That the blessing of Abraham might come upon the Gentiles in Christ Jesus, that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith” (Galatians 3:14 NKJV). This means if you’re born again, you’re not trying to get blessed—you already are! You’re Abraham’s seed, the heir of God’s promises, and a carrier of divine favour.

When you received Christ, you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise. That seal marks you as God’s own and guarantees your access to all the blessings and benefits of the Abrahamic covenant. So refuse to live small. You carry the same blessing that made Abraham great, the same power that caused Isaac to prosper in famine, and the same grace that distinguished Joseph in a foreign land. You’re an answer to the needs of your generation!

Never forget—you’re the evidence that God’s promise to Abraham is alive today. Live boldly, talk boldly, and bless your world with the life of God in you. You are the expression of the blessing! Hallelujah!

## Speak

I’m the seed of Abraham, blessed and highly favoured in Christ Jesus. The blessing of Abraham is working in me—in my spirit, soul and body. I have access to all that God has made available in Christ. I walk in divine health, prosperity and victory today, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Today speak words of favour, help someone around you, and let God’s grace find expression through you everywhere you go!
