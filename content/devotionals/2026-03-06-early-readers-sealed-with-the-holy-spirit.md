The very moment you were born again, something wonderful happened—you were sealed with the Holy Spirit! This means you belong to God completely. You’re His special treasure, marked and protected by His Spirit.

A seal shows ownership and authority. Long ago, kings used their seals to show that a letter or command came from them. In the same way, God gave you His Holy Spirit to show that you’re His child and that heaven backs you up!

The Holy Spirit is God’s promise and gift to you. He helps you make right choices, teaches you God’s Word, and fills your heart with peace and joy. You never have to feel unsure or afraid, because the Spirit in you is proof that God is with you always.

## Prayer

Dear precious Father, thank you for sealing me with the Holy Spirit. I know I belong to you, and I’m guided, protected, and strengthened by your Spirit every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.11
