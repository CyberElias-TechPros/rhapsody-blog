Have you ever felt worried when something didn’t go the way you planned? Jesus understands, and He loves you very much, and has a better plan for you. That’s why He tells us to be “of good cheer” because He has already won the victory for us (John 16:33).

Remember Joseph in the Bible? Even when he was treated unfairly and put in prison, he trusted God and refused to give up (Genesis 37–50). God was with him through it all, and in the end, everything turned out for his good.

So, when things don’t seem right, don’t lose your joy. Stay cheerful and trust God. You’re a victor! Your future is bright, joyful, and full of hope.

## Confession

Greater is He who is in me than he who is in the world! Therefore, I live victoriously and reign in life above sickness, the devil, and disease. Hallelujah!
