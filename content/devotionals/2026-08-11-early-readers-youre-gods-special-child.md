Did you know that you’re very special to God? The Bible says you’re part of a chosen generation and God’s own special people. This means you belong to Him, and He loves you dearly.

God called you out of darkness into His wonderful light so you can show His goodness to others. Your words, actions, and kindness can help people see how wonderful God is. When you live the way God teaches you in His Word, you shine brightly for Him.

Always remember you’re not ordinary. You’re God’s special child, chosen to show His love and goodness to the world.

## Prayer

Dear loving God, thank you for choosing me as your special child. Help me shine your light and show your goodness everywhere I go, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
