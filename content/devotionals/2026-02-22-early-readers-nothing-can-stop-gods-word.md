People may try to stop the Gospel, but they can’t! The Word of God is unstoppable—it keeps spreading and changing lives everywhere.

Even when Paul was in prison, he kept preaching, writing letters, and encouraging the churches. The more people tried to stop him, the more the Gospel spread!

It’s the same today. No law, threat, or challenge can silence God’s Word. You’re part of that unstoppable Church—so keep shining your light and speaking God’s truth boldly!

## Confession

Nothing can stop the Gospel! I’m bold to share God’s Word everywhere, and it keeps changing lives through me.
