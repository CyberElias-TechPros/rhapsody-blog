Many young people hear adults say, “God works in mysterious ways,” and start thinking God is unpredictable or hard to understand, like someone who changes rules without warning. But God isn’t that way; He’s not mysterious at all. Jesus showed us clearly what the Father is really like.

Jesus proved that God isn’t mysterious when He said, “He who has seen Me has seen the Father.” In other words, if you want to know what the Father is like, just look at Jesus. The Bible describes Jesus as the brightness of God’s glory, and the express image of His person (Hebrews 1:3), and Jesus wasn’t mysterious at all!

To be mysterious is to be strange and unpredictable, and Jesus wasn’t that way. He was, and still is, the perfect representation of the Father’s personality. He’s the Word of God made flesh. Thus, if you want to know more about the Father, then dig into the Word of God! Instead of guessing what God is like based on random opinions, social media quotes, or what someone angrily says during a crisis, study the Word daily, and you’ll become better acquainted with the Father’s personality. As you receive His Word into your Spirit and meditate on it, His character will be revealed to you and through you.

Make up your mind to know the Lord; He’s a loving, caring, thoughtful, patient, and merciful Father! A loving person can never be mysterious. Contrary to the perception of many, God isn’t the one responsible for the things that cause grief, turmoil, and panic in the world; those are the acts of the devil. According to the Bible, only good and perfect things come from God (James 1:17).

So, don’t be deceived by ignorant folks who attribute every disaster and chaos in the world to God. He’s a God of love who is more than willing to reveal Himself to His kids. He doesn’t want anyone in the dark regarding His personality; that’s why He gave us His Word and sent the Holy Spirit to show us who He is. Praise God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for revealing your personality to me in the Person of your Son, Jesus Christ! I rejoice at knowing what a loving God you are indeed! As I study your Word to know you more, I thank you for flooding my spirit with the full and exact knowledge of who you are, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
