One of the most beautiful realities of the Christian life is the assurance that God is at work in you. Every day, as you rise and step into your assignments, you must be conscious of this truth: God is at work in me! He isn’t watching from afar; He is actively involved, faithfully perfecting the good work He began in you.

The Bible says in Philippians 2:13 NKJV, “For it is God who works in you both to will and to do for His good pleasure.” This is absolute truth. It’s God Himself working in you, making you willing and giving you the ability to fulfil His divine purpose. You’re His living tabernacle, His outstretched arm, His operational headquarters on the earth. How could you fail when His power and wisdom are at work in you?

In Judges 6:14 NKJV, the Lord said to Gideon, “… Go in this might of yours, and you shall save Israel from the hand of the Midianites. Have I not sent you?” What he had was sufficient because the presence of God was with him. The same is true of you. What you have is more than enough. Christ in you is enough! God at work in you is enough!

You can win souls in multitudes, you can change your world, and you can accomplish the extraordinary. The Spirit of God is working mightily in you, and His glory is revealed through you. You’re a burning and shining light, drawing men and women into the Kingdom. Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, your power is at work in me, causing me to accomplish great things for the Kingdom. I am a burning and a shining light, a soul winner, and a beacon of your glory. Your Spirit is mighty in me, and through me, many are drawn into the Kingdom. Thank you for the unstoppable spread of the Gospel in the nations of the world, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

As you step out today, go with the consciousness that God is at work in you.
