God’s Word teaches us that faith is not just in your heart; it comes out of your mouth too! That’s what today’s memory verse is saying.

When you believe that Jesus is Lord in your heart, that’s wonderful. But God also wants you to say it. Saying it out loud shows your faith. It’s like a seed: if you plant it in your heart and say it, it grows strong.

In Romans 10:8, the Bible says the Word of faith is in your mouth and in your heart. That means your faith is alive when you believe it and say it! So, don’t keep quiet. Say it, and watch God’s Word work in your life!

## Confession

I’m a child of God! I declare that Jesus is Lord of my life. Therefore, I live in divine health, I succeed in everything I do, and I tell of His goodness to everyone. Amen!
