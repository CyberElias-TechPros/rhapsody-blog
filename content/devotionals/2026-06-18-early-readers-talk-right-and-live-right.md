Did you know your words have the power to make a difference? The Bible teaches us that our words can bring life or trouble. When we speak God’s Word, we speak life, joy, and victory.

Remember how God created the world? He spoke, and it happened! The amazing news is that He has given you the ability to use your words to recreate your world too.

When you fill your heart with God’s Word and speak it in faith, it’ll help you live right and therefore produce all you have spoken. So, choose your words carefully. Speak what God says about you, and your life will show His goodness every day.

## Confession

I speak faith-filled words every day. And with my faith-filled words, I create whatsoever I want. Hallelujah! 2525
