God wants His Word written in your heart, not just in a book. How do you do this? By reading, meditating, and speaking it often. The more you do this, the more His Word will produce what it talks about in your life.

God told Joshua to keep His Word in his mouth and heart all the time (Joshua 1:8). That was the secret to his success as he led Israel through the desert after Moses died. Like Joshua, speak words of faith, strength, and victory. This is how you win every day!

## Confession

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for filling my heart with your Word. I choose to speak words of faith, power, and victory every day. Hallelujah!
