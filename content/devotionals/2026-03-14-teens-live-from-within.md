A fascinating truth about the eagle is that it never flaps its wings endlessly in flight. Instead, it soars, riding on the currents of the wind. Its strength is not in constant striving but in aligning with the unseen forces that carry it higher. That’s how God designed us to live—not by mere struggle on the outside, but by drawing strength from within, where His Spirit dwells.

Ecclesiastes 3:11 NKJV tells us something remarkable: “He has made everything beautiful in its time. Also, He has put eternity in their hearts...” What a glorious thought! The world is in your heart. Eternity is in your heart. Imagine that! No wonder Jesus said, “A good man out of the good treasure of his heart brings forth good things…” (Matthew 12:35 NKJV).

You carry divine treasures within you. Not only are the world and eternity in your heart, but the very Spirit of God resides there (1 Corinthians 3:16). That’s why the Christian life is lived from the inside out. Paul, writing by the Spirit, tells us, “…though our outward man is perishing, yet the inward man is being renewed day by day” (2 Corinthians 4:16 NKJV).

The forces that determine your success, health, and victory are not external—they’re in your inward man. Proverbs 4:23 exhorts us to guard our hearts diligently, for out of them flow the issues of life. When your spirit is trained to listen to God, you’ll always know what to do, where to go, and how to respond. Living from your spirit means speaking from your spirit. Declare the Word boldly, pray in tongues, and meditate on the Scriptures. Learn to pause and check within before making decisions. As you do, you’ll discover that your life aligns perfectly with God’s will.

Today, refuse to be driven by circumstances or emotions. Instead, be led by the Spirit within you. That’s the secret to consistent victory.

## Speak

With my spirit, I’ve encircled and taken possession of all things; I possess the blessings of Abraham. Life, immortality and eternity are in my spirit. I live from within, guided by divine wisdom and empowered for a life of success and impact as the Spirit of God guides me into all truth and causes me to walk in victory today.

## Act

Before leaving the house today, spend time to pray in the Holy ghost and at different intervals during the day.
