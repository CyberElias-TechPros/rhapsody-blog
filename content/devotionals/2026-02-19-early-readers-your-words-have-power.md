The words you speak are powerful. They can bring life, joy, and health—or they can bring sadness and fear. That’s why it’s important to always speak the right words—God’s Word!

When you say, “I’m strong,” “I’m wise,” or “I can do all things through Christ,” you’re filling your world with power and victory.

So, watch what you say. Use your words to build, heal, and bless others. Speak faith, and you’ll see great things happen!

## Confession

I speak faith-filled words that bring life, health, and victory. My words change things for good!
