When you received Jesus, something wonderful happened—you were born again by the Word of God.

This means God’s life and Word are in you. Because you’re born of the Word, you can live like God’s child and show His goodness to others. Just like Jesus lived the Word when He was on earth, you also express God’s Word through your thoughts, words, and actions.

Every day, you can let God’s Word guide what you say and do. Remember, you’re born of the Word, and your life shines with God’s truth and blessing.

## Confession

I’m born of the Word of God. His Word lives in me, and my life shows His goodness every day. Hallelujah!
