Did you know God already planned a wonderful life for you? Yes, He did! As God’s child, you’re not an accident. Before you were born, He had a special plan for you—a plan filled with joy, victory, and good things.

In Luke 1:13-17, the Bible talks about John the Baptist. Before he was born, an angel of God told his father, Zechariah, what he would become. God had a plan for John’s life, and John grew up to fulfill it.

So, how do you walk in God’s special plan for you? It’s through His Word. As you study God’s Word, pray, and listen to the Holy Spirit, He’ll guide and lead you in victory, success, peace, and prosperity!

## Confession

Dear precious Father, I thank you for the paths of greatness you’ve prepared for me. I’m living the good life that you planned for me, manifesting your glory. Hallelujah!
