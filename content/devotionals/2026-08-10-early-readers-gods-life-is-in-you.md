When you accept Jesus into your life, a great thing happens; God’s life comes into you.

The Bible says we become partakers of the divine nature. This means the life of God is working in you to help you live a victorious life. Because God’s life is in you, you can be strong, joyful, and full of courage. Remember Timothy in the Bible? He was timid, but Paul reminded him that the Spirit within him made him strong and courageous (2 Timothy 1:6-7).

God’s life changed everything. The same Spirit is in you today, helping you live with strength, courage, and joy each day.

## Confession

The life of God is in me. I’m strong, bold, and full of joy because I have God’s divine nature. Hallelujah!
