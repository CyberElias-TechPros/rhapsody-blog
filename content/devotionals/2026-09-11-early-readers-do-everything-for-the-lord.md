Did you know that God cares about everything you do? He even knows your thoughts, and He loves you very much.

The Bible tells us to do everything as if we’re doing it for the Lord. This means you can be honest, kind, and hardworking, even when no one is watching.

When you choose to do what is right, you make the Lord happy. His Spirit in you helps you live the right way every day.

The Bible says that when you know what is right, you should do it (James 4:17). So, always choose to do the right thing, because you’re God’s child.

## Prayer

Dear faithful Father, thank you for helping me do what is right. I’m honest, kind, and sincere in all I do, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
