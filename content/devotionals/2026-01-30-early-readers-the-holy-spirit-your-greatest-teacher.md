Do you know you have a special teacher? Yes, you do! He’s the Holy Spirit, whom the Father sent to help you with all things. He teaches you God’s Word and shows you the right things to do.

Before Jesus ascended to heaven, He told His disciples that the Holy Spirit would come to guide them into all truth (John 16:13). And guess what? The same Holy Spirit lives in you now! He helps you understand the Bible and helps you make the best choices in life.

So, don’t ignore the leading of the Holy Spirit on anything, no matter how small it seems. Remember, He’s your best Teacher, and you can always trust Him to lead you on the right path.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for your Spirit who lives in me, teaching, guiding, and directing me every day. guides me. Amen!39
