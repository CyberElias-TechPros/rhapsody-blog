To prophesy means to speak words from God that bring encouragement, strength, and life. When you speak God’s Word, you build others up and help their faith grow.

For example, you can say, “The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want” (Psalm 23:1). When you speak like this, you remind yourself and others that God takes care of you.

The Bible tells us to desire to prophesy so we can bless others (1 Corinthians 14:1). So, speak words of faith over your life— about your health, your academics, and your future. Through God’s Word, you can bring joy and encouragement to everyone around you.

## Prayer

Thank you, faithful Father, for your Word in my heart. I speak words that bring life, strength, and encouragement, and I grow in wisdom every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
