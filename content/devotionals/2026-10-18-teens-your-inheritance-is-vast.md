The world is full of treasures, yet many go through life without ever knowing those treasures belong to them. It’s like having access to something valuable but living as if you’re broke because you don’t know what’s yours. As a child of God, you’re not just an observer; you’re an heir! Every resource, every hidden wealth, every unclaimed blessing on the earth is yours in Christ. God said through Isaiah, “I will give you the treasures of darkness and hidden riches of secret places” (Isaiah 45:3).

The riches of this world belong to God, and as His child, everything He owns is at your disposal. The Scripture reminds us, “And if you are Christ’s, then you are Abraham’s seed, and heirs according to the promise” (Galatians 3:29). Your inheritance is vast—both the treasures already revealed and the hidden riches waiting to be discovered. That includes ideas, opportunities, connections, and solutions others haven’t even thought of yet.

The Lord Jesus Himself demonstrated this. He instructed Peter to catch a fish, and inside its mouth was a coin to pay the temple tax. This shows that God’s provision is always accessible when we move in obedience and faith. Long before you were born, God deposited reserves of wealth and blessing for you: ”unsearchable riches” (Ephesians 3:8). These are inexhaustible treasures, waiting for you to access them with the wisdom and revelation of the Holy Spirit.

The Bible says all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden in Christ (Colossians 2:3). Thus, as you constantly study the Word and fellowship with the Spirit, you’ll receive insight into reality; the Spirit of God will help you discover the treasures of darkness and hidden riches of secret places. Glory to God forever!

## Prayer

Father, I thank you for opening the eyes of my spirit to discover the treasures and inestimable riches that you’ve laid up for me in life. I declare that this is my set time to enjoy the treasures and hidden riches in secret places, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Declare today: “I’m an heir of God! Every treasure, and every good thing belongs to me. I walk in the revelation of His riches, and I lack nothing!”
