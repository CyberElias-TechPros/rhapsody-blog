You might be wondering why bad things still happen in the world. Satan is the one who brought sin and trouble into the world. He is the source of fear, pain, and confusion.

But the good news is that Jesus has already defeated him, and has given us victory over him and all his works.

When Adam disobeyed God, he handed his authority over to Satan. But God already had an amazing plan. He sent Jesus, who defeated Satan completely through His death, burial, and resurrection.

Now, because we are in Christ, we live in His victory. Satan has no power over God’s children. Therefore, you should live boldly, knowing that Jesus has already won—and you win with Him every day!

## Confession

Thank you, precious Father, for sending Jesus to take my place. Through His death, burial, and resurrection, I have complete authority over Satan, and I walk in victory every day. Hallelujah!1111
