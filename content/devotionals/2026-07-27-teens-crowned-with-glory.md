Romans 3:23 says, ”for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.” In other words, man became separated from the glory life due to Adam’s transgression. But as a result of the death, burial and resurrection of Jesus Christ, we’re no longer short of the glory of God. You were brought into the life of glory the moment you were born again.

I want you to note the tenses in our opening scripture as we read it this time in the Weymouth New Testament Translation: “and those whom He has pre-destined He also has called; and those whom He has called He has also declared free from guilt; and those whom He has declared free from guilt He has also crowned with glory.” Here, we identify four vital realities concerning the new creation, which are the result of salvation as consummated by Christ Jesus.

Firstly, you’ve been pre-destined; that means you’re not a chance occurrence; you aren’t an ‘accident’ or a mistake, no matter what anyone says. God knew you before you came. Secondly, you’ve been called; that means elected, chosen and appointed by God unto the good life. Thirdly, you’ve been justified; that means declared not guilty; as far as God is concerned, there are no charges against you. Then fourthly, you’ve been crowned with glory; that means brought into a life of beauty, excellence, success and prosperity.

These aren’t things that’ll happen when you get to heaven; they’ve already taken place. You’ve been pre-destined, called, justified and crowned with glory. You’re no longer “basic” or “average”—you’ve been glorified. Little wonder the remarkable words of Jesus in John 17:22: “And the glory which You gave Me I have given them.” You’ve been brought into the glory-life. You were born to show forth the glory of God and display the wonderful deeds and perfections of Him who has called you out of darkness.

Yes, sin separated humanity from glory, but Jesus ended that story. Through His resurrection, He brought you into a life of glory. There’s glory in your mind, your academics, and your creativity. God wants you to see it, believe it, and live like it.

## Prayer

Dear Lord Jesus, your glory is upon my life, and I manifest that glory everywhere I go. I thank you for transferring me from the domain of darkness into your glorious Kingdom, and I declare that by the help of the Holy Spirit, I’m displaying your wonderful deeds and perfections. Amen.

## Act

Walk confidently today. Keep your head up, speak boldly, and carry yourself with the quiet assurance that God has crowned you with His glory.
