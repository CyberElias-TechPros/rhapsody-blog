In 2 Timothy 4:7, the Apostle Paul said, “I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.” He wasn’t talking about a physical fight or athletic races. He was speaking about a faith fight—choosing to trust God and believe His Word, no matter what happens.

The Bible also tells us that faith is like a shield that blocks all the fiery darts of the devil (Ephesians 6:16). This means that when challenges come, you don’t have to be afraid. You can win by using your faith—by speaking God’s Word and holding on to your faith-filled confessions.

So, never give up. Keep trusting the Lord. And just like Paul, you’ll be able to boldly say, “I have won!”

## Confession

I am who God says I am. I have what He says I have, and I can do what He says I can do. Therefore, I stay strong in faith because my victory is sure in Christ. Hallelujah!
