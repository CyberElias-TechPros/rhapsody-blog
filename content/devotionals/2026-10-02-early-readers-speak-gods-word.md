One day, Jesus spoke to a storm and said, “...Peace, be still...” and the storm stopped (Mark 4:39). That shows how powerful His words are.

The good news is that when you were born again, you were born through God’s Word (1 Peter 1:23). Jesus also said that if His Word remains in you, “…you will ask what you desire, and it shall be done for you” (John 15:7). That means when you speak God’s Word, it carries His power.

You can make great things happen by speaking God’s Word. So, when you face challenges, don’t stay quiet. Speak His Word with faith, and you’ll experience peace, victory, and joy in your life.

## Confession

I speak God’s Word with faith, and it works in my life. I enjoy peace, victory, and joy every day. Hallelujah!
