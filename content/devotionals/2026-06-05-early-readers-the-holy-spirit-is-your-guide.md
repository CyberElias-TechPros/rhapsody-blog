A GPS device is a tool that helps people

find their way when traveling. It shows the

right direction, so they don’t get lost. The

Holy Spirit is our guide; He guides us in the

right direction, and He is better than the best

GPS device.

Philip was led by the Holy Spirit to meet

a man from Ethiopia, who was reading the

Scriptures but didn’t understand them. He

obeyed, explained God’s Word, and the

man believed in Jesus and was baptized

(Acts 8:29–38).

Just like Phillip, the Holy Spirit guides you,

too. He’s always speaking to you. So, the

more attention you give to the study of God’s devotionals inWord and praying in tongues, the louder

and clearer you’ll hear Him every time He is

leading on anything.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for the gift of the Holy Spirit, to lead, instruct, and guide me in all things, in Jesus’ Name. Amen. 1010
