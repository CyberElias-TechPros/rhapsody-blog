As God’s child, God has already declared, “You’re forgiven and free from sin!”

This is called justification. It means God sees you as clean, right, and perfect in His eyes. Jesus took your place so you could take His place—standing before God as His special, beloved child.

Before Paul met Jesus, he made wrong choices and even hurt God’s people. But when he met Jesus, his whole life changed! Paul became a transformed person and began preaching boldly about Jesus everywhere (Acts 9:1–22). Paul didn’t earn this new life—God gave it to him as a gift because of what Jesus did.

Now, because of Jesus, you also have a brand new place with God—a place filled with His glory, power, and victory. So, go through your day knowing you stand with Jesus in a place of strength, honour, and greatness. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for giving me a special place with Jesus—full of your glory, victory, and blessings. I reign, rule, and walk in your power today and every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
