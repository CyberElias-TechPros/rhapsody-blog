When Jesus died on the cross, He no longer needed to offer sacrifices. His one sacrifice was enough to take away sins forever. Through that single, perfect offering, you were made perfect in God’s sight.

The Bible tells us in 2 Corinthians 5:21 that Jesus—who never sinned—became sin for us, so that we might become the righteousness of God in Him. This means that when God looks at you, He doesn’t see your mistakes or shortcomings; He sees His own righteousness in you. How amazing is that?

So, boldly agree with God’s Word and say: “As Jesus is, so am I in this world” (1 John 4:17).

You’re forever perfected. Glory to God!

## Confession

I am the righteousness of God in Christ Jesus. In Him, I live as a winner and reign in life. My life continually displays His righteousness and brings glory to His Name. Hallelujah!
