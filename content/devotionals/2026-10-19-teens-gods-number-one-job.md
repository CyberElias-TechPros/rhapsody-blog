Soul winning is God’s number-one job. It’s so important to Him that He sent Jesus, His only Son, into the world, not to be the president of a nation, a senator or an engineer, but a soul winner. No wonder the Lord Jesus Himself also took soul winning very seriously during His earthly ministry. Even when He was physically tired and needed rest, the opportunity to share the Gospel with the Samaritan woman was enough to make Him forget His hunger and tiredness. He said to His disciples,“... My food is to do the will of Him who sent Me, and to finish His work” (John 4:34).

We need to realise as God’s people that we may not always have the opportunity to win souls, and that’s why Jesus said, “I must work the works of Him who sent Me while it is day; the night is coming when no one can work” (John 9:4). He knew He had a time limit in the earth; so He maximised it by being an effective soul winner. This is the same thing He expects from us; we ought to recognise that the responsibility of telling others about the Gospel of Christ’s salvation has been entrusted to us (2 Corinthians 5:18), and we must do the work of Him that sent us while it’s day.

You’re young, and that’s your advantage. You have energy, influence, and opportunities to reach your peers, friends, and even strangers with the Gospel. The responsibility God has given you is serious: to share His love now, while the time is right. Don’t wait for the “perfect moment” or think you’re too young. God can use your social media posts, your school friendships, your conversations, and even your small acts of kindness to bring someone to Christ. This is the day of salvation; this is the season of the end-time harvest.

## Prayer

Dear Lord, thank you for the privilege of being a vital labourer in the end-time harvest. I can see that the fields are white with the harvest; therefore, I commit myself to pulling men and women out of darkness into the kingdom of light. By your Spirit, I’m guided to seize today’s opportunity to impact and win souls into the Kingdom, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Before the day ends, look for an opportunity to share the Gospel with someone. It could be a friend, a family member, or even a stranger. It doesn’t have to be a long sermon; just a simple testimony of what He’s done for you.
