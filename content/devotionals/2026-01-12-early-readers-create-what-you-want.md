God created the whole world with Words. He spoke, and everything came into being. Guess what? You can do the same because you were made like Him!

When things don’t seem to go right, that’s not the time to cry or complain. Rather, speak God’s Word! If you feel any symptoms in your body, say, “I’m strong and healthy in Jesus’ Name!” Refuse to speak fear or doubt. Say only what God has said about you.

That’s what faith does! God wants you to say what you want to see. Just like He spoke light, life, and beauty into the world, you can speak peace into your home, joy into your day, and success into everything you do! That’s how to live by faith—and create what you want.

## Confession

The Holy Spirit makes my words powerful and full of life! With my words, I create a beautiful life filled with peace, joy, and God’s glory every day. Amen.
