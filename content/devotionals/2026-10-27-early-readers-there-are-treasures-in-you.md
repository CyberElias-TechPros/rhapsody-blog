Did you know there are treasures inside you? Yes! As God’s child, He has placed good things in your spirit—wisdom, strength, and the ability to do great things. Jesus said that, “…out of the good treasure of his heart brings forth good things...” (Matthew 12:35). This means you already have what you need to succeed.

How do you bring these good treasures out? By studying, believing, and speaking God’s Word. When you say what God says about you, those good things begin to show in your life.

So always declare, “I’m strong, I’m wise, and I’m blessed!”

## Confession

I have treasures in me. I’m full of wisdom and strength. God’s life and glory are at work in me every day. Glory to God!
