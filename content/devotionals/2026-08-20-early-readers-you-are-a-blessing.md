As God’s child, all things are yours. So, there’s no need to worry about anything. Remember, Abraham was a blessing and because of him, his cousin, Lot, was blessed too (Genesis 13:5-6). When God wants to bless someone, He sends you to them because He has blessed you with every blessing, so you can be a blessing to them.

If your friend or anyone around you feel pains in their body, they can get healed as you pray and lay hands on them because God lives in you.

Make up your mind to be a blessing to everyone you meet.

## Prayer

Thank you, precious Father, for making me a blessing to my world. Through me, others will know your love, grace and glory, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
