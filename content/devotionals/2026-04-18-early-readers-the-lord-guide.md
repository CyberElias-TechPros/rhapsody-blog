God loves you so much that He never leaves you to figure things out on your own. Today’s memory verse tells us that the Lord Himself will show you the right path and teach you what to do. When you trust His Word and listen to the Holy Spirit, you’ll always make wise and excellent decisions.

David understood this well. He said, “He leads me in the paths of righteousness…” (Psalm 23:3). Even when life seemed uncertain, David trusted God completely— and God guided him every step of the way.

So, don’t ever worry about the future or feel confused about what to do. Trust the Lord to direct you. His grace, mercy, and wisdom have gone ahead of you, to help you win every day. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for your love and mercy. I trust your Word, and I know you’re always with me. Thank you for leading me on the right path every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
