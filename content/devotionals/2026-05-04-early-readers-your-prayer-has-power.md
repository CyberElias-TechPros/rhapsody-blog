Prayer is not something we do only when we’re in trouble. Prayer is powerful! When you pray, you’re taking action because God listens to you and responds.

The Bible tells us about Elijah. He prayed to God, and it did not rain for three and a half years. Later, he prayed again, and God sent rain (James 5:17–18). Elijah was not special because he was perfect—his prayers worked because he trusted God and believed His Word.

In the same way, when you pray for your family, your friends, your school, or yourself, God hears you. Pray with faith, speak in the Name of Jesus, and know that God answers your prayers. Your prayer truly has power!

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for hearing me when I pray. As I pray in the Name of Jesus, your power works in my life, my family, my school, and my world. Amen.
