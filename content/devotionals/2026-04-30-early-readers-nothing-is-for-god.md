Sometimes at school you may meet a subject, project, or equation that feels really tough. You’d try your best, but the answer still doesn’t come. When that happens, remember this powerful truth: nothing is ever too hard for God!

In today’s memory verse, God calls Himself “the Lord of all flesh.” That means He’s the God of everyone and everything. No problem is too big, too complicated, or too impossible for Him. Even when you don’t know what to do, God already has the answer!

So, never say, “This is too difficult,” or “I don’t think God can fix this.” Instead, declare with confidence: “Nothing is too hard for my God!” He’ll help you, guide you, and turn every situation around for your good. Hallelujah!
