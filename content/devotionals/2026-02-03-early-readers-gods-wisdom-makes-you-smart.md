It’s great to be smart, but being wise is even better!

You become smart by learning facts and studying at school, but real wisdom comes from God. His wisdom helps you know what to do, when to do it, and how to do it right (Colossians 1:9).

When you’re born again, Jesus becomes your wisdom (1 Corinthians 1:30). That means you don’t just think with your mind—you see things with your spirit, the way God sees them.

As you study and meditate on God’s Word every day, you’ll grow wiser and stronger. God’s wisdom in you brings success, peace, and victory!

## Confession

I walk in God’s wisdom. The Holy Spirit helps me understand things, so I always know what to do. Hallelujah!
