The moment you were born again, the Lord put everything you need for a great life inside you. That means the strength, wisdom, joy, and success you require are already in you. Isn’t that awesome?

The Bible says, “A good man out of the good treasure of his heart brings forth good… For out of the abundance of the heart his mouth speaks” (Luke 6:45). God has put great treasures in your heart, and He wants you to bring them out.

How do you bring them out? By the words you speak! Speak God’s Word every day, and believe it in your heart. As you do, the good things God put in you will begin to show in your life.

## Confession

I thank you, precious Father, for giving me all that I need to live a beautiful, glorious, and successful life. Therefore, I win always and manifest your glory in all I do. Glory to God!2121
