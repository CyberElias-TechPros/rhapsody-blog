God has given you His Word to speak things into existence by faith. The Bible shows us that God created the world by His Word, and His Word is powerful and true.

The Bible tells us about Abraham. He was very old and had no children, but God said he would be the father of many nations. Abraham believed what God said and did not doubt. He trusted God, and the promise came to pass (Romans 4:20–21).

You can do the same. When you need help, courage, or anything, nd what God says in His Word and speak it in faith. Trust Him and agree with His Word. God is faithful, and He always brings His Word to pass!

## Prayer

Thank you, Lord, for your Word in my heart. Therefore, I declare favour, divine health, success, and victory in my life, in my academics, and in my family, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.9
