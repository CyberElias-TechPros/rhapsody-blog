Our opening scripture lets us know that Jesus is the embodiment of all wisdom and knowledge. In other words, not only is He the creator of all things, but He also knows all things. There’s absolutely no topic or subject that He’s deficient in. He knows everything about God, heaven, angels, man and the devil. He also knows everything about your school subjects, your career path, coding, content creation, science projects, music production, business ideas and things beyond your imagination. He can teach you about them better than any professor.

He’s the fountain of all knowledge and can teach you anything. He can give you a word of knowledge, and that knowledge will be what you need to change your world for the better. The world is looking for solutions, and God wants to give His kids the solutions—even students like you. He can give you a word of knowledge about your career. He can give you the medical solution to cancer, HIV/AIDS and other medically incurable ailments. All you need is a word of knowledge from Him, which is only a part or a little piece of everything He knows.

You can receive a word of knowledge from the Lord about your health, job, business, family, academics and finances from time to time. What you need is to recognise that Jesus is knowledge personified, and then trust Him to enrich you in everything, including all utterance and in all knowledge (1 Corinthians 1:5).

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, I thank you that in everything I am enriched by you, in all utterance and in all knowledge. Thank you for granting me revelation knowledge and knowledge of everyday events. The eyes of my understanding are enlightened, and my heart is flooded with divine knowledge that I may know you more. Amen.

## Act

Speak in tongues for 10 minutes and receive the wisdom of God about a subject or situation that’s of concern to you or a loved one.
