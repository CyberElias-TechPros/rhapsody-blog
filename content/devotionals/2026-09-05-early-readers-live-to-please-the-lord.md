Enoch was someone very special in the Bible. In Hebrews 11:5, we learn that before God took him to heaven, he had a testimony that he pleased God. That means he lived every day to please the Lord. What a beautiful thing to be known for!

How did Enoch do this? He walked by faith. The Bible says that without faith, it is impossible to please God (Hebrews 11:6). Faith means trusting what God says and choosing to act on it.

As God’s child, you can live this way too. Be a doer of His Word (James 1:22). When you act on God’s Word every day, you’re pleasing the Lord.

Jesus said, “If you love Me, keep My commandments” (John 14:15). So, choose to do what God’s Word says, and your life will make the Lord happy.

## Prayer

Dear merciful Father, thank you for helping me live by faith. I do your Word every day, and my life brings you joy, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
