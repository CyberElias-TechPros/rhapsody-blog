As God’s child, God wants you to be someone

who thinks about others, not just yourself. The

Bible says in 1 Timothy 2:1-4 that we should

pray for everyone, our friends, family and even

our leaders.

Nehemiah did this when he helped rebuild

Jerusalem’s walls. He didn’t think only about his

own safety; he told the people, “Fight for your

brothers, your sons, your daughters, your wives,

and your homes.” He wanted everyone to be

protected (Nehemiah 4:14).

Don’t only pray for your own needs. Pray for

the sick, the poor, and people who need help.

Jesus cares about everyone (Hebrews 4:15).

That is what champions do: they think beyond children’s devotionalsthemselves.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for loving everyone and helping me to be kind, caring, and one who thinks beyond himself, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
