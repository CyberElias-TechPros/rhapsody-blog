Sometimes you may feel like there isn’t enough time to finish your homework or complete a task. But God says He can restore lost time (Joel 2:25). That means He can make up for anything that seems wasted. So, don’t panic or complain about time.

When people panic, they act in fear. But fear isn’t from God. The Bible tells us how Elijah outran horses because God helped him (1 Kings 18:46). Philip was also taken by the Spirit to another city (Acts 8:39–40). Isn’t that wonderful?

Always remember, you’re not ordinary. God lives in you. So, speak faith-filled words and always declare to yourself, “I have enough time to do all that God wants me to do.” Hallelujah!

## Confession

I have enough time to do all that God wants me to do; therefore, I refuse to panic or fear. Hallelujah!
