God’s Word is very powerful. The Bible says it can build you up, and make you strong (Acts 20:32).

When you read, listen to, and think about God’s Word, it helps your faith grow. It also reminds you of the wonderful blessings God has prepared for His children. The more you learn God’s Word, the more confident and joyful you become. Just like healthy food helps your body grow strong, God’s Word helps your spirit grow stronger every day.

So, take time to study the Bible and listen carefully when it is taught. God’s Word will guide you and help you live a beautiful life.

## Confession

God’s Word is working in me. It builds me up, makes me strong, and guides me every day. Hallelujah!
