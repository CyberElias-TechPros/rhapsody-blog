When God arises in a person’s life, all enemies are scattered, and all that hate Him flee in terror! But how does God arise in your life? Is it just by praying, “O God, arise; defeat my enemies”? What you do matters. Firstly, notice the words “Let God arise”; that’s another way of saying, “Allow God to arise.” It’s your choice to let Him move in your life. Since God and His Word are one: “In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God” (John 1:1), giving His Word first place in your life lets Him arise.

God doesn’t need to part the heavens to work in you; He already lives inside you. As you meditate on His Word and speak in tongues, His Word stirs in your spirit. That’s God arising in you! Then speak that Word as a prophecy. No enemy can resist it. When life throws challenges—like school stress, peer pressure, or feeling left out—don’t just feel stuck. Stir God’s Word in your spirit and speak it out. Say, “I’ve got the power of God in me! I’m stronger than any challenge! I’m set up for success, happiness, and peace!” Doing this makes fear, doubt, and negativity back off.

Keep declaring: “I have the life of God in me! I can never be poor! Prosperity, health, and goodness are my birthright!” As you do, poverty, sickness, lack, and failure will flee. Your faith in God through His Word will rise like a giant and defeat every enemy in your life.

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for your power that resides in me, which I consciously activate as I meditate and speak the word of faith. Your rhema-word of faith is always in my heart and in my mouth, and as I speak it forth, it becomes the sword of the Spirit that cuts the enemy down, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

If a challenge shows up today, don’t panic. Open your mouth and declare the Word of God.
