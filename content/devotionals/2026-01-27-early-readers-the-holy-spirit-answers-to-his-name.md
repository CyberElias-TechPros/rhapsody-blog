Did you know the Holy Spirit has a name? Yes, He does! “Holy Spirit” isn’t His name—it’s who He is! It’s like calling someone “human being” instead of his real name.

The Holy Spirit answers to the Name of Jesus. That’s why the Bible never tells us to pray to the Holy Spirit or ask Him to do something. Rather, we’re instructed to pray in the Name of Jesus, and the Holy Spirit will get to work. He’s the One who makes the power and presence of Jesus real in our lives.

So, whenever you call on the Name of Jesus, know that the Holy Spirit is at work, bringing His power and glory into the situation to cause a change. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for the power in the Name of Jesus. As I use His Name, I see the works of the Holy Spirit in all that concerns me, in Jesus’ Name. 36Amen.
