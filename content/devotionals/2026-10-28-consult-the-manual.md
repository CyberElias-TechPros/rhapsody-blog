Every manufacturer provides instructions for getting the best out of a product because the maker knows exactly how it was designed to function. In the same way, God has given you His Word so you can get the very best out of the life He's given you. He doesn’t want you to rely on guesswork or the opinions of others to know how to live the transcendent life He’s given you. Rather, He wants you to consult the manual: His Word.

His Word unveils His purpose for your life. His Word reveals who you are, what He created you for, and how to live the life He designed for you. The Bible says all Scripture is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, and for instruction in righteousness. It's the instruction manual for the life of righteousness.

As you study and live by God's Word, it trains you, corrects you, equips you, and thoroughly furnishes you for every good work. Through the Word, you're continually guided to walk in God's perfect will and fulfil His purpose for your life. Never navigate life by human reasoning or the opinions of the world when the Manufacturer has already given you His instructions.

His Word is dependable because it comes from the One who designed you. Make it your daily guide. As you live by it, you'll walk in His wisdom, fulfil His purpose, and live in the fullness of the life and blessings He planned for you before the world began.

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for giving me your Word as the instruction manual for my life. As I study and live by it, I walk in your wisdom, fulfil your purpose, and experience the fullness of the life and blessings you've prepared for me. Your Word guides every decision I make and continually produces excellence and victory in me, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
