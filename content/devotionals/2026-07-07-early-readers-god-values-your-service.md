Everything you do for God is important to Him. When you help in church, pray, sing, give, or show kindness, God sees it; and He is pleased. The Bible says, “For we are God’s fellow workers…” (1 Corinthians 3:9). That means you’re working with Him!

You may sometimes think what you do is small, but to God, it matters very much. Your words, actions, and choices can show His love to others every day. That’s why God wants you to serve Him with joy, excellence, and a good heart.

God has a beautiful plan for your life, and serving Him is part of that plan. So do your best with joy and a willing heart. Your service is precious to Him, and He delights in you!

## Prayer

Thank you, Lord, for the opportunity to serve you. I am faithful in all that I’m asked to do, and my service brings praise and glory to you, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
