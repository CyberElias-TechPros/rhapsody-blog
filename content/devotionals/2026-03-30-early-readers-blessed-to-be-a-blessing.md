When God blessed Abraham, He didn’t just say, “I’ll give you blessings.” He said, “You will be a blessing!” That means Abraham became the expression of God’s goodness. Everywhere he went, people were blessed because of him.

The Bible says that if you belong to Christ, you’re Abraham’s seed and an heir of the same promise (Galatians 3:29). That means you’re not waiting to be blessed—you’re already blessed! The blessing of God is in you, and it makes everything you do succeed.

Just like Joseph in the Bible, who was blessed even as a slave, you can bring blessings everywhere you go—at home, in school, and among your friends. God’s power in you causes others to see His goodness through your life!
