A fragrance has the power to change the atmosphere of a room. A single drop of perfume can alter the environment, leaving a lingering presence that can’t be ignored. That’s how your life is—you carry with you something far greater than a fragrance: the very essence of God’s blessing.

Galatians 3:29 NKJV declares, “And if you are Christ’s, then you are Abraham’s seed, and heirs according to the promise.” This isn’t just a scripture to admire; it’s your divine identity. The blessing of God is not simply what you have— it’s who you are. God didn’t only say to Abraham, “I will bless you”; He also declared, “You shall be a blessing.” That means Abraham became the embodiment of the blessing, and in Christ, you have inherited that same blessing.

You’re not chasing blessings; you’re the carrier of the blessing, and this blessing is not limited to material possessions. It’s a spiritual force that empowers everything you touch to prosper. It makes you fruitful, productive, and influential. Like Joseph, who prospered even in slavery and prison because the blessing was upon him (Genesis 39:2), you too are unstoppable. The blessing doesn’t recognise limitations—it manifests results regardless of circumstances.

You’re the seed of Abraham, called to be a blessing to your world. You’re not ordinary. The forces of heaven are at work in and through you. Be conscious of this truth. Declare it daily; walk in it. Let it reflect in your words, actions and expectations. You’re the expression of the blessing!

## Speak

I’m the seed of Abraham. I’m an heir of God and a joint heir with Christ. I’m blessed and I am a blessing. The power of the blessing is working in and through me. Everywhere I go, I bring favour, increase and divine influence. I am the expression of God’s goodness in the earth. Hallelujah!

## Act

Declare the confessions in the speak section to yourself at different intervals today.
