The Kingdom of God works by faith. Just like gravity makes things fall to the ground, faith helps us receive from the unseen, spiritual world. We believe what God says even before we see it.

God has already given us many blessings: health, help, angels, and more. Faith helps us see these truths with our hearts and bring them into our everyday lives with our words.

So, choose to live by faith. Think God’s thoughts, speak God’s Word, and act like it’s true—because it is!

## Confession

I live by the law of faith. I see with my spirit, speak God’s Word, and walk in His blessings every day.
