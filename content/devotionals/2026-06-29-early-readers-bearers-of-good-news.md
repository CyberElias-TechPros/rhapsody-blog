God has trusted you with a very special task! When you were born again, He gave you a message to share—the Good News about His love and salvation.

Jesus told His disciples to go into the world and tell others about Him. This important job was not given to angels—it was given to us, God’s children. God wants people to hear about Him through you.

You might think, “I’m too young,” but you can still help others know God. You can be kind, share Bible stories, and tell your friends that Jesus loves them too.

So, be glad you’re among those God has chosen to share the Good News and help others know Jesus, too.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for choosing me to share your love with others, and as I do, my hearers believe and are saved, in Jesus’ Name. Amen. 3838
