Going to church is very important! It’s not just a place to sing and clap; it’s where you learn God’s Word, grow in faith, and meet other Christians who love Jesus too.

The Bible says we shouldn’t stop meeting together, because when we gather, we strengthen one another. Even though we can watch services online, it’s also good to be in God’s house with other Christians.

So, be excited to go to church! Go with your Bible, sing with joy, and listen to the Word with an open heart.

## Confession

I love being in church. I grow stronger in God’s Word and help others know Jesus better. Hallelujah!
