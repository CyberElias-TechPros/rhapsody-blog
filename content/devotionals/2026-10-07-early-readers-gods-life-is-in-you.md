As God’s child, His life is in you. That means you’re not helpless—God’s power is at work in you to protect and strengthen you.

Jesus said that even if His children drink anything deadly, it’ll not harm them (Mark 16:17–18). This shows that God’s power is greater than anything harmful.

The apostle Paul showed this when a poisonous snake bit him. People expected him to fall sick, but he shook it off and was unharmed (Acts 28:3–6).

So, don’t be afraid. The Holy Spirit is with you, and within you. He protects you and helps you live strong, healthy, and full of life.

## Confession

The life of God is in me; therefore, no sickness, disease, or pain can overcome me. I’m healthy, strong, and full of life. Hallelujah!
