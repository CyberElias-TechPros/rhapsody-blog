Psychologists have identified a common trait in people who thrive despite difficult circumstances. It’s called “cognitive reframing”—the ability to find humour or perspective in hard situations. They aren’t pretending problems don’t exist; they’ve simply learnt that how you interpret an event shapes how that event affects you.

The Bible reveals an even more powerful truth: when you see your circumstances through the eyes of God’s Word, you can respond with joy instead of fear. That’s why Proverbs 17:22 declares that “A merry heart does good, like medicine, but a broken spirit dries the bones.” Why wouldn’t you laugh when you know that you’re more than a conqueror? Why shouldn’t you laugh when you know that all things work together for your good?

Refuse to allow circumstances dictate your happiness. It doesn’t matter what comes against you in life; learn to laugh your way to victory. Unsatisfactory grades, drama with friends, online pressure, family issues—none of these get to decide your joy. The opening scripture shows us that God laughs at the ranting of His enemies. Like your Heavenly Father, learn to laugh the enemy to scorn when he starts throwing his tantrums at you. When tough times come, that’s the time to laugh, for there’s absolutely nothing the enemy could do to harm you.

No wonder the Apostle James admonished that you “… count it all joy when you fall into various trials” (James 1:2). If you’ve been facing trying times, I recommend to you “Laughing sessions.” Sit all by yourself and meditate on the victorious life Christ has brought you into, and begin to laugh! At first, you might even look ridiculous to yourself, but keep at it. It won’t be long before you’ll find yourself declaring the Word of God that’ll change everything. Glory, Hallelujah!

## Speak

I’m laughing my way through life, from victory to victory and from glory to glory! I laugh at the devil and challenging circumstances because I know I can never be disadvantaged. I’m soaring high on the wings of the Spirit. My mouth is filled today with laughter and my tongue with the songs of triumph, in Jesus’ mighty Name. Amen.

## Act

Have a laughing session today.
