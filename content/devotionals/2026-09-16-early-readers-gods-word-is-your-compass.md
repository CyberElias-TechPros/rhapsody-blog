Have you ever used a map or asked someone for directions when you didn’t know the correct direction? Without help, you might feel lost. God’s Word guides your life in the same way. It shows you the right choices to make, and keeps you on the right path.

Jesus said, “…I am the light of the world. He who follows Me shall not walk in darkness…” (John 8:12). You follow Jesus by obeying and doing His Word. When you study and meditate on God’s Word, it helps you understand His plan for your life.

So, keep God’s Word in your heart, and speak it often. Let it guide you in all things; this will help you make wise decisions always.

## Prayer

Dear precious Father, thank you for your Word in my life. It’s a lamp to guide my steps and my path. With all my heart, I trust and follow your Word for my life, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.23
