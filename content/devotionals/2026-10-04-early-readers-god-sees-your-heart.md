As God’s child, what is in your heart matters to God. That is why the Bible tells us to fill our minds with things that are true, pure, and good (Philippians 4:8).

In the Bible, Cain became angry and jealous of his brother, Abel. Instead of choosing what was right, he allowed those wrong thoughts to grow in his heart. Even when God corrected him, he refused to listen, and it led him to do something very wrong (Genesis 4:3–8).

This teaches us an important lesson. Fill your heart with good thoughts, and choose what pleases God every day.

## Prayer

Thank you, precious Father, for your Word. My heart and mind are fixed on your Word, and I choose what pleases you every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
