Romans 8:29 (NLT) says, “For God knew his people in advance, and he chose them to become like his Son….” Predestination is at work in you. God has a divine plan for your life, and He has set events and their sequence in motion in line with His divine plan.

Think about John the Baptist. His entire calling was to reveal Jesus to Israel. Yet God didn’t simply tell him who the Messiah was. Instead, John had to wait for the sign: “…The man on whom you see the Spirit come down and remain is the one who will baptize with the Holy Spirit.” (John 1:33 NIV). When Jesus finally stood before him, the moment unfolded exactly as God planned (John 1:29–34).

Or consider Samuel’s mission to anoint Israel’s new king (1 Samuel 16). He thought Eliab, Jesse’s tall and impressive son, was the one. But God corrected him: “People look at the outward appearance, but the Lord looks at the heart.” 1 Samuel 16:7 (NIV). One by one, Jesse’s sons were rejected until David—the forgotten shepherd boy—was brought in. Then the Lord said, “Rise and anoint him; this is the one.” 1 Samuel 16:12 (NIV). Step by step, God’s plan came into focus.

In the same way, God has a plan for your life, and it’s a good one. He has already set the path and the sequence

1010 of events that will shape you into who He has called you to be. You don’t have to panic or force things to happen. What matters is that you stay yielded to Him, trusting His timing.

Ephesians 2:10 (AMP) says, “For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus… so that we would walk in them [living the good life which He prearranged and made ready for us].” This year, walk boldly in the path God has marked out for you. It’s already prepared, and it leads to His glory in your life.

## Prayer

Dear Father, I trust you wholeheartedly, knowing that you have predestined my life for your glory. My life is a testimony of your faithfulness, and I yield myself to your perfect will, ever advancing and fulfilling all you have called me to do, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
