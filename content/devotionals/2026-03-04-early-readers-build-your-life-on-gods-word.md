Jesus said the person who hears His words and does them is like a wise builder. Storms may come, but that house stands strong.

Feelings and trends change, but God’s Word never fails. When you build your life on the Word of God as contained in the Bible—not just one verse, but the message of Jesus—you become steady and strong.

Choose the solid foundation. Believe the Word, speak the Word, and do the Word.

## Confession

God’s Word is my foundation. I hear it, speak it, and do it—so my life stays strong in every season.
