The Holy Spirit lives inside you to guide, strengthen, and help you every day. And God wants you to stay full of Him—overflowing with His joy, power, and wisdom.

How? By praying in tongues, singing spiritual songs, and thanking God always. When you do this, your heart stays full of joy and your life shines brightly for Jesus.

Don’t let sadness or fear fill your heart. Stay full of the Holy Spirit and be joyful always!

## Confession

I’m full of the Holy Spirit and joy every day. I walk in God’s power, surrounded by His peace, and overflowing with His love.
