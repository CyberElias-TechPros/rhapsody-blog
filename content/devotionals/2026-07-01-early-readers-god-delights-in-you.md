Have you ever wondered how God feels about you? The Bible says, “For the Lord takes pleasure in His people…” (Psalm 149:4). That means God delights in you and is pleased with you as His child.

He is not far away or angry with you. Even when you make mistakes, He still loves you and cares for you nonetheless. When you remember this, your heart will be full of peace instead of fear.

Therefore, live each day with condence. You’re precious to God, deeply loved, and safely held in His care.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for loving me in a special way and for making me your favourite. I’m blessed and shown favour everywhere I go, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
