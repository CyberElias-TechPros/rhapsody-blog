Imagine you had a wealthy relative you’d never met. You spent years writing letters, asking them to please send you some money, hoping one day they might answer. Then, one day, a lawyer showed up at your door and informed you that the relative had passed away years ago, and you were the sole heir to the entire fortune. You were already rich and didn’t know it. That’s the moment you start living in your inheritance!

Isn’t it amazing to know that the fullness of God’s blessings already belongs to you? Not “when I’m older,” not “after I’ve figured my life out.” Right now. Somebody said, “I’ve been praying to God to bless me.” But you see, that’s actually not the best way to think about it. God has already blessed you way beyond what you can imagine! He’s already given you ALL spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ Jesus, not just a few.

The Bible says you were called to inherit a blessing (1 Peter 3:9); you were born into God’s blessing because you’re part of Abraham’s family. God told Abraham, “I will make you a great nation; I will bless you and make your name great; and you shall be a blessing” (Genesis 12:2). That promise wasn’t just for Abraham; it’s for you too (Galatians 3:16). So, not only are you blessed, you’re meant to be a blessing to others.

In your school, your friend group, your online space, you carry something valuable. Decide today to live like the person God created you to be. You’re full of His blessings. There’s nothing in this world or heaven that you need to beg for, because everything belongs to you (1 Corinthians 3:21). Your life should overflow with good things so that others who come in contact with you can partake of the goodness of God in your life. Praise God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for blessing me so much that I can actually be a blessing to everyone else. My life is packed with good things, with no empty spaces, and anyone I hang out with or talk to gets to experience your goodness just by being around me. Hallelujah!

## Act

Since you’re already blessed, look for an opportunity to be a blessing to someone today. It could be a kind word, a small gift, or offering your time.
