As God’s child, you’ve been given everything you need for a beautiful life. The Bible tells us that we’re God’s children and that we’re joint-heirs with Christ (Romans 8:17). That means we’re in God’s rich Kingdom. That’s why Jesus said to seek God’s Kingdom first, and all other things will be added to you (Matthew 6:33).

Jesus also said, “…it is your Father’s good pleasure to give you the kingdom” (Luke 12:32). And the beautiful thing is: He has already given it to you! So, instead of being worried, say with faith:

## Confession

The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not lack! I’m in Christ, and a joint-heir with Him; therefore, all things are mine. Blessed be God!
