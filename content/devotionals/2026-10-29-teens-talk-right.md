A GPS navigation system needs a destination to guide you. It only responds to where you tell it to go. If you input the wrong address, it confidently guides you to the wrong place. If you input your correct destination, it recalibrates every turn to get you there. Your words function like entering destinations into the GPS of your life. They determine which direction your energy, focus, and opportunities flow.

As a teenager, you’re building your world every day with your thoughts, your choices, and especially your words. When you constantly say, “I’m not good enough,” “I can’t do this,” or “Things never work out for me,” you’re forming a language of limitation. And that language will influence how you see yourself and what you expect from life. But as a child of God, you’ve been called to speak differently. This goes beyond positive thinking or motivational talk. It means speaking truth—and God’s Word is truth (John 17:17).

When you consistently speak the truths of God’s Word, those words shape your mindset, align your spirit, and produce results consistent with that divine reality. It’s not just the vocabulary that matters; it’s the system of thought behind it. For example, some say, “When we started worshipping, the presence of God came down.” It sounds spiritual, but it’s inaccurate. You carry His presence. He doesn’t come and go. When Christians gather, His presence is manifested—not imported.

Train yourself to speak in agreement with New Testament revelation. Let your words align with who you are in Christ—God’s dwelling place, full of His glory and power. As your language changes, your thinking will change. As your thinking changes, your experiences will follow. Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for the revelation of your Word that enlightens my spirit. My words are full of grace and power, producing in me victory, excellence and glory. I speak always in line with your truth, expressing your divine thoughts through my words, and I’m continually transformed from glory to glory by the power of your Word, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Today, pay attention to your words—replace every negative or limiting statement with what God says about you.
