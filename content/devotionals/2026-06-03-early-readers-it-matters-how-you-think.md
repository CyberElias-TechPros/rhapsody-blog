God cares about how you think. Your thoughts are important because they can bring joy and peace, or sadness and fear.

An example is King David in the Bible. When he saw Goliath, everyone else thought, “This giant is too big.” But David thought differently. He believed God was with him, and that right thinking gave him the victory (1 Samuel 17).

Your thoughts can help you grow. That’s why God wants you to fill your heart with His Word. When you think about what is true, good, and right, your life will go in the right direction.

So today, choose to think God’s thoughts as you meditate on His Word, and you’ll live a beautiful life every day!

## Confession

God’s Word is in my heart, guiding my thoughts, words, and actions. I think excellent and glorious thoughts always. Hallelujah!
