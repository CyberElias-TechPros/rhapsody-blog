God created boys and girls carefully and beautifully (Psalm 139:14). Each of us is made exactly the way He planned, and He loves all His children deeply.

Being “one in Christ” does not mean boys and girls are the same. It means we share the same love, the same blessings, and the same opportunity to serve God. When you pray or worship, God does not look at whether you are a boy or a girl. He looks at your heart— your faith and love for Him (John 4:23).

Remember, as God’s children, we belong to the same family and have the same life in Christ.
