When the morning sun rises after a long night, its golden rays don’t just light up the sky; they transform the atmosphere, dispelling shadows and announcing a brand-new day. In the same way, the revelation of Christ in you lights up your life. It changes everything—bringing glory, beauty, and divine possibilities into your world.

The greatest revelation of the New Testament isn’t only that Jesus died for us but that He rose again and now lives in us! This was the mystery hidden for ages but is now unveiled: “Christ in you, the hope of glory” (Colossians 1:27 NKJV). Notice, it doesn’t say the promise of glory, but the hope of glory—a present-hour reality. The glory is here. The glory is now.

Many quote, “God will not share His glory with another,” and that’s true when it comes to idols. But with us, He went beyond sharing—He imparted His very glory! The Lord Jesus prayed, “And the glory which You gave Me I have given them, that they may be one just as We are one” (John 17:22 NKJV). Child of God, when you received Christ, you received glory. Shame and reproach were terminated. You were ushered into the realm of excellence, beauty, and victory.

Romans 3:23 declares that all had sinned and fallen short of the glory of God. But that was before Christ. Now, with Christ in you, you’re not falling short—you’re full of His presence, power, and righteousness. You’re a God-carrier, the living temple of the Holy Spirit. Everywhere you go, divinity shows up in you and through you. That’s why you can never be disadvantaged—the One who created the universe lives in you in His fullness.

More so, you’re the glory of God. In Ephesians 1:17 NKJV, He’s called, “…the Father of glory….” And if He is the Father of glory, then you—His child—are the glory. This is your identity. See yourself in that glory. Walk in it. Talk it. Live it. The glory life is yours—today and always!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for making me the effulgence of your glory, the expression of your righteousness, and the dispenser of your grace. Thank you for making my life a manifestation of your beauty, presence, power, and excellence. I see myself as your glory, and live the glorious life in Christ, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
