Your words have power! When you speak with faith, great things happen. That’s why it’s important to always speak God’s Word—not your fears or feelings.

When you say, “I’m strong,” even when you feel weak, you’re using your faith. When you say, “God supplies all my needs,” you’re declaring His truth—and it changes everything!

So, don’t speak doubt or fear. Keep your words full of faith and life every day!

## Confession

I speak God’s Word always. My words bring healing, joy, and victory to my world. Hallelujah!
