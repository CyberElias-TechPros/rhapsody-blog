Have you ever wanted to pray but didn’t know what to say? Don’t worry—the Holy Spirit helps you pray. The Bible says He helps us when we don’t know what to say or how to pray as we should (Romans 8:26). Glory to God!

Sometimes, you may want to pray for your friends who feel sad, sick, or too weak to pray for themselves. At such times, the Holy Spirit guides your heart and helps you pray the right way.

If you’re praying for a friend to be saved or healed, remember that the Holy Spirit is working with you, and in you. You’re not alone. He gives you wisdom and boldness as you pray in tongues.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for the Holy Spirit who helps me pray. As I pray, He guides me to say the right things, and helps me bring blessings to 26others, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
