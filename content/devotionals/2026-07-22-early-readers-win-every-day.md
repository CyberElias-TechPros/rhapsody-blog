God wants you to live every day as a winner! That means no matter what happens, you can overcome challenges because God has already given you victory through Jesus.

So, how can you win every day? Through God’s Word and using your faith. Jesus said even a tiny bit of faith can achieve amazing things (Matthew 17:20). But faith isn’t just believing in your heart—you must also act on God’s Word. It’s like having a treasure map: you need to follow the steps to nd the treasure.

Therefore, trust God, do what His Word says, and watch yourself win every day!

## Confession

I win and reign in life with my faith and God’s Word. Nothing is too difficult for me, because I can do all things through Christ. Hallelujah!
