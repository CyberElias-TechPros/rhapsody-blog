Do you know what it means when Jesus says He is the First and the Last?First and the Last? It means Jesus was there before anything began, and He will still be there when everything ends. He is the greatest and the mightiest of all.

The Bible tells us that Jesus died but is now alive forever; no one else is like Him. Because Jesus lives forever, He is always with you, and, in you through the Holy Spirit.

So, you don’t have to worry about anything – even the future – because Jesus is there for you and with you. Remember, He knows the end from the beginning (Isaiah 46:9-10). Therefore, He has everything under control! Hallelujah!

## Confession

Dear Lord Jesus, I’m full of joy because I know you are in me and always with me. Thank you for guiding me and perfecting all that concerns me. Hallelujah! 3737
