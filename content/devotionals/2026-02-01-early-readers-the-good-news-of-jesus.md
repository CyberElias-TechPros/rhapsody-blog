When Adam disobeyed God, sin came into the world, and everyone was born under its power. But the good news—the beauty of the Gospel—is that Jesus came and changed everything!

Through His obedience, Jesus took away sin and gave us righteousness instead (Romans 5:19). Now, everyone who believes in Jesus is made the righteousness of God. Isn’t that wonderful?

This is the Gospel we preach. We don’t try to convince people that they’re sinners. Rather, we show them that the solution to sin, poverty, sickness, and all other work of darkness is salvation in Christ Jesus. Jesus has brought life, righteousness, and victory to everyone who believes in Him.

## Confession

I have life and righteousness in Christ! I share this good news with others and help them see God’s love for them. Hallelujah!
