Happy New Year! Today isn’t just the first day of a new calendar; it’s the beginning of a new chapter in your journey with God. God has brought you into 2026 for a reason, and He wants you to grow, make progress, and change levels. No matter what last year looked like, this year offers a fresh opportunity to rise—spiritually, mentally, and in every area of your life. But change doesn’t happen automatically. You have to be intentional about it.

Today, as you start the year, take time to reflect: Where am I now? What areas of my life need to go to the next level? Be honest with yourself. Whether it’s your prayer life, school performance, habits, confidence, relationships, or mindset—identify what needs to grow.

Next, visualise where you want to be. Set clear and specific goals. What does your next level look like? Is it reading more books this year? Passing that exam? Starting a small business? Could you be more consistent in your walk with God? Picture it. Write it down.

Then, ask yourself: What actions must I take to get there? Every level change requires effort. You can’t expect a new result from an old routine. Do you need to wake up earlier? Start a Bible plan? Find a mentor? Study smarter? Whatever it is, commit to doing it.

And please keep in mind the most important part: involve God. Pray about your plans and let the Holy Spirit lead you. He knows the best way forward and will give you the strength, wisdom, and grace to make progress.

This year, don’t let life happen to you—happen. Take responsibility. Take action. Plant the right seeds and trust God for a supernatural harvest. While others are just making wishes, you’ll be living in results. 2026 is your year to grow, thrive, and change levels.

## Speak

Blessed Father, I refuse to remain at the same level. I take action, planting the right seeds into my heart, my mind, and the realm of the spirit. By the power of your Word and the guidance of the Holy Spirit, I move forward and upward, changing levels and fulfilling my destiny. Thank you for your divine power at work in me.

## Act

Today, identify specific areas of your life that need growth, and set clear goals for each one as you begin this new year.
