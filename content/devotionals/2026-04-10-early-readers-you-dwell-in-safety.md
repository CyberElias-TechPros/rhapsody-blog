Do you remember Noah? God told him to build a big ark because a great flood was coming. Noah believed God and obeyed Him. He even warned the people around him (Hebrews 11:7), but they didn’t believe. When the flood came, only Noah and his family were safe— inside the ark God told him to build.

In the same way, because you believe in Jesus and are now in Christ, you live in God’s safety every day. Our memory verse says that anyone who believes in Jesus will not perish. That means when you were born again, God brought you into a special place of protection.

The Bible says you’re a new creation in Christ (2 Corinthians 5:17). And being in Christ is the safest place in the whole world! It’s a place of protection, joy, grace, prosperity, and excellence. Glory to God! So say it boldly: “I’m in Christ; therefore, I’m safe forever!” Walk with joy and confidence, knowing that God loves you and watches over you always.

## Confession

Dear loving Father, I live in safety, free from anything that can harm or hurt. I walk daily in your blessings, protection, joy, and peace. Hallelujah!
