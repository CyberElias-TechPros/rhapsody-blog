Think about how differently things worked just thirty years ago. If you wanted music, you drove to a store, bought a CD, went home, and played it. If you wanted a movie, you waited for it to air on television or rented it from a video store. Today, you pull out your phone and stream anything instantly. The waiting period disappeared. In the same way, God designed your spiritual life to operate on instant access, where harvest doesn’t require waiting for the “right season” because every season is harvest season!

The Lord didn’t say in our opening verse, “While the earth remains, seed time and harvest time...shall not cease.” Sadly, that’s how some folks read the scripture above in their minds. Someone may ask, “But what’s the difference?” The difference between the two statements is irreconcilable. To say “seed time and harvest time” suggests that there’s a definite time of sowing and a definite time of harvest. But the idea given to us in the Scripture, when it says “seedtime and harvest”, is that harvest is anytime. In other words, you might as well consider today the set time for your harvest!

What the Lord is saying to us here is that though there must be a time of sowing (seedtime), there’s no fixed time for your harvest; you can call it forth anytime! Don’t forget that as a child of God, you play by a different set of rules. So, your harvest ought to come in supernatural ways. For example, the Bible says that Isaac sowed during the time of famine (a time when it was naturally impossible for a harvest), yet he received in the same year a hundredfold(Genesis 26:12-13).

Others sowed beside Isaac and got no crop, yet Isaac reaped a miraculous harvest at a time when the natural conditions favourable for harvest were absent. That’s how it ought to be in your life. It’s the set time for your harvest. As Abraham’s seed, your harvest is now; your miracle is now. Go ahead and sow your seed, and the Lord will bless and multiply it back to you. Whether it’s money-seed, seeds of prayer, or seeds of faith-confessions—this is the set time for your harvest! Begin to relate to your harvest by faith.

## Speak

I declare that for every seed I have sown, my harvest comes speedily in the Name of Jesus. None of my seeds shall fail to produce the expected harvest. Always, my harvest is supernatural in relation to my seed. I receive even now a multiplied harvest of all my seeds sown, in the Name of Jesus. Amen.

## Act

Think of a seed you have sown—whether financial, time, or prayer. Today, speak to it. Say, “I call forth my supernatural harvest now! It comes speedily, in Jesus’ Name. I receive it by faith.”
