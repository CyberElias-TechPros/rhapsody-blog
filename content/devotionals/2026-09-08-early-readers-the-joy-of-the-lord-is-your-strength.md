God is kind and gracious! In the Bible, the Israelites once felt very sad because they hadn’t done what the Lord told them to do. But God didn’t want them to remain sad. He told them to rejoice and celebrate, because “the joy of the Lord is your strength” (Nehemiah 8:10).

This shows how powerful joy is. I’m talking about the joy that comes from the Holy Spirit inside you. This joy doesn’t come from what someone does for you; it comes from knowing Jesus.

The Bible says God gives us joy that is “full of glory” (1 Peter 1:8). This joy can be seen in your smile, your laughter, and the way you live.

No matter what’s happening around you, choose joy. Jesus said His joy is in you (John 15:11). So, smile, rejoice, and celebrate God’s joy every day!

## Confession

I’m full of joy because the joy of the Lord is my strength. No matter what happens, my joy remains strong within me. Hallelujah!
