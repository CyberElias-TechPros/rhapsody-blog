Have you ever considered what it truly means to be an heir? In this world, an heir isn’t just someone who waits in the shadows to receive an inheritance; an heir lives in the reality of possession and authority. Imagine a young prince or princess who doesn’t just dream of the palace but walks confidently within its walls, knowing it’s theirs. That is exactly who you are in Christ!

God’s purpose for saving us was never limited to rescuing us from sin alone. No! He designed a greater plan—to bring us into the glorious inheritance promised to Abraham, a blessing so vast and so abundant that it defies human comprehension. The Word declares in Romans 8:17 NKJV that we are “…heirs of God and joint heirs with Christ….” This means everything that belongs to Jesus belongs to you—fully and freely.

What belongs to Christ? The Bible says, “The earth is the Lord’s, and all its fullness, the world and those who dwell therein.” (Psalm 24:1 NKJV). The Apostle Paul reveals that Christ’s riches are unsearchable, inexhaustible, and boundless (Ephesians 3:8). This inheritance includes wisdom, peace, authority, and every blessing from the Father.

But here is the good news: you’re not waiting for this inheritance in some distant future. Ephesians 1:11 declares you have already obtained it. The Holy Spirit is your deposit, your guarantee that all these riches are yours now (Ephesians 1:14). Paul confirms, “…all things are yours….” (1 Corinthians 3:21 NKJV)—everything in heaven and earth is under your ownership through Christ.

So, refuse to live small. Think big! Speak the language of an heir. Live boldly, knowing that the divine power of God has given you all things that pertain to life and godliness (2 Peter 1:3). Your inheritance is not a promise, it’s a possession. Walk in that truth today and always!

## Speak

I’ve obtained an inheritance in Christ; all things are mine. The blessing of Abraham is working in me. I walk in dominion, fruitfulness and divine favour. I live in the reality of my inheritance in Christ today, hallelujah!

## Act

Declare out loud today, “I am an heir of God, and I walk in the reality of all the blessings and authority that belong to Christ as my inheritance!”
