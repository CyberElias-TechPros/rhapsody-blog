Imagine a world where everyone asked, “How can I make a difference today?” What a beautiful place it would be! Often, we think success is about what we can gain, but true success is measured by what we give. It’s about leaving the world better than we found it. The world needs visionaries: young people who will step out from the crowd, find a need, and reach out to meet that need. That’s one of the principles of success—when you’re challenged to make things better than the way you met them.

Instead of being the one asking for help and waiting for others to give to you, you should become the helper! Become the one to meet other people’s needs. Be the blesser and the giver, for that’s your calling. The Bible shows us that Joseph stood out in his day and saved the entire nation of Egypt from starvation during a time of famine (Genesis 41:55). Think about your school, your group of friends, or your community; maybe someone is being bullied, struggling with school, or just needs a friend; you don’t have to solve the world’s problems first; you can start small.

This is your set time to make a difference in your world. The world ought to be a better place because you came. And you know what? It begins with being passionate about a God-idea and having the courage to implement that idea once you receive it from God. It doesn’t matter how small the idea may seem; once you have it, run with it. Before long, God will expand your vision and increase your inspiration! He’ll grant you the motivation and the ability to conceive and execute more ideas that’ll impact the world and make it a better place. Wake up to that calling today. Praise God!

## Prayer

Dear Heavenly Father, I thank you for giving me the inspiration, motivation and ability to conceive and execute divine ideas that’ll make the world a better place. I recognise that this is my season and set time to make a difference in the world; therefore, by the power of the Spirit, I’m impacting the world with the investment of my personality! Thank you for making me a blesser and a giver, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Look around your immediate environment: your home, your school, your neighbourhood; identify one problem, big or small, and take a step towards solving it. Offer help, share an idea, or lend a hand.
