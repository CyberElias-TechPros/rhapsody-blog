Epaphras wasn’t a pastor or prophet, and he didn’t have some fancy title in his church. But he was a man known to love to pray for others. The Bible says he prayed fervently— that means he prayed with great passion and never gave up!

You can be like Epaphras too. Pray for your friends, family, and the people in your church. Ask God to make them strong, full of faith, and joyful in serving Him.

When you pray this way, you’re helping other Christians grow so they can fulfill God’s plans for their lives.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for teaching me to pray for others. I pray that my friends and church family grow stronger in their faith and love for you every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
