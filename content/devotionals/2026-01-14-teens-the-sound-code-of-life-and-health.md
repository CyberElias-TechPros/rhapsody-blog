In our last study, we saw how sound codes carry divine energy and meaning. Today, let’s take it a step further—into your very body. Years ago, a groundbreaking experiment showed the effect of words on water. Water placed in jars was exposed to different words. When kind and uplifting words like “You are beautiful, you are wonderful” were spoken, the water crystals formed into stunning, harmonious patterns. But when harsh words like “You’re ugly, useless” were directed at other jars, the crystals became deformed and chaotic.

This experiment reveals a significant lesson. Recall that the human body is over seventy per cent water, which accounts for more than two-thirds of its composition. Therefore, the words you speak to yourself and your body have a tangible effect. This isn’t just science; it’s a spiritual law. The Bible already revealed it long ago: “Death and life are in the power of the tongue” (Proverbs 18:21 NKJV).

When you speak negatively to yourself—saying things like, “This headache is killing me” or “I’m always sick”—you are literally coding your body with destruction. Those words send sound codes into the water molecules in your system, disfiguring them and, over time, manifesting as sickness, weakness, or inexplicable conditions.

However, the opposite is also true! When you speak words of life, health, and strength, those same crystals align beautifully, carrying the energy of life throughout your body. You can renew your health, strengthen your body, and invigorate your mind by the consistent release of faith-filled sound codes.

This is why you must be mindful of your speech; the crystals in your body respond to words, and your speech has the potential to heal, transform and invigorate your physical being. Take this seriously.

## Speak

My speech aligns with God’s Word, bringing life, health, strength, and peace to my body and mind. I speak words of faith, love, and hope, and my body responds to these words, functioning in divine order and perfection to the glory of God. Every cell, tissue, and organ in me resonates with the sound codes of life, producing vitality, energy, and wholeness. I live in perpetual health, soundness, and strength, for the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus is at work in me mightily.

## Act

Speak life-filled words over your body today; look in the mirror and boldly declare health and strength, in every part of you.
