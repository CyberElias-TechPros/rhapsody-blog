God has given His angels charge over you to keep you in all your ways (Psalm 91:11). Be conscious of the presence of God’s angels with you. We’re in a day where there’s increased angelic activity upon the earth because the second coming of the Master is at hand. His work of salvation must be carried out with more urgency and fervency than ever before.

I remember the story of a young man who was crippled from birth. One day, while passing by a crusade venue, he heard the message of Jesus Christ being preached and decided to stop and listen. He was deeply touched by the message and prayed earnestly that God would cleanse him of his sins and purify his heart. As he was praying, he began to feel as though someone was lifting him. Before long, he felt so high up and thought some friends had been kind enough to lift him. When he opened his eyes to see who they were, he saw no one. He looked again and discovered he was standing on his own feet! God had sent His angels to pick him up! Glory to God!

As a teenager, take advantage of the ministry of angels in your everyday life, even in your academics. As you learn to act on the Word of God and confess it, you’ll surely experience increased angelic ministry in your life. Never hesitate to deploy your angels whenever you need a miracle; it’s their ministry to see to it that the Word of God that you speak— your faith-filled confessions—come to pass.

## Prayer

I thank you, Lord, for giving your angels charge over me to keep me in all my ways. I rejoice to know that angels are ministering spirits sent to help me accomplish divine tasks; therefore, I recognise their presence with me, and I pray that all over the world, your children will be sensitive to the ministry of angels in their lives as they work to establish your will in the earth, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Today, when you face a need—whether it’s for protection, provision, or direction—verbally deploy your angels. Say, “In the Name of Jesus, ministering spirits, I dispatch you now; go ahead of me today to prepare my way, and bring me the right opportunities.”
