Have you ever swapped seats with a friend? That’s a little like what Jesus did for you—only what the Lord Jesus did was much greater!

The Bible says He took your place so you could take His place in righteousness. Long before it happened, the prophet Isaiah saw it in a vision: the Son of God would be wounded for our sins, beaten so we could be whole, and by His stripes we are healed (Isaiah 53:4–6).

When He came, everything happened just as God planned. He was judged, beaten, and killed—all for you. His sacrifice made you right with God! Now, you live sin-free and enjoy God’s blessings every day through His resurrection. Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for giving me a special place in Christ, a place of joy, victory, and blessing. I live every day knowing you’re with me, and I reign over challenges, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
