In ancient cities, a watchman on the wall was vital. His duty was to stay alert, to guard, and to warn. If he forgot his role, the entire city was at risk. Likewise, when we forget our spiritual priorities, we leave room for distractions and worldly influences to creep in.

The early Church spread with astonishing speed; in just three centuries, Christianity had permeated the known world. By 324 AD, persecution was abolished under Emperor Constantine, and within a few short years, Christianity became the official religion of the empire. Yet, in the midst of that success, urgency waned. The Church thought, “Everyone’s religious now; everything is fine.” But Jesus didn’t come to start a religion; He came to save men’s souls.

The children of Israel made the same mistake. God gave them rest and peace in the land He promised (Joshua 21:43-45). Yet, He warned them not to mingle with other nations or serve their gods (Joshua 23:7-8). Sadly, over time, they forgot and turned to idols. For young people today, the “distractions” may not be pagan idols but things like endless scrolling on social media, chasing popularity, gaming marathons, or relationships that weaken your fire for God. Before long, fellowship fades, church attendance drops, and soul winning gets pushed aside.

But hear this: you’re first and foremost a Christian! Your identity in Christ must be evident in all you do. Don’t let social media or the glitter of trends pull you away from your devotion to the Lord. Keep your fellowship strong, your priorities right, and your passion for soul winning alive. Let nothing distract you. Keep your eyes on the Master’s call, for that’s where true fulfilment lies.

## Speak

What a joy to preach the glorious Gospel! The expansion of God’s Kingdom in the earth is my top priority, and I declare that nothing will distract me from channelling my energies towards turning men from darkness to light, and from the power of Satan unto God, making them citizens of God’s eternal Kingdom. Amen.

Look out for an opportunity to share the message of God’s love with someone today.
