The message of Christ’s salvation is God’s answer to the darkness and confusion in the world. It’s our responsibility as young people to make sure everyone hears it before Jesus comes back. The Lord said the Gospel would be preached in all the world for a witness, and then the end would come. This means the timeline of history was so designed that the Church should finish the work. You’re part of the generation chosen to wrap up the ages. Don’t just watch from the sidelines. Participate actively through intercessory prayer, strategic involvement in Gospel initiatives, financial partnership, and bold witnessing. The Spirit of God has equipped you to reach your world.

Acts 1:8 declares, “But you shall receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you; and you shall be witnesses to Me in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth.” Remember, this mission is urgent, and the Spirit has chosen you to be part of it. The mandate and urgency of the Spirit is clear: God wants all men to be saved and to come unto the knowledge of the truth that there’s only one God and one mediator between God and mankind, Christ Jesus, who gave Himself as a ransom for all humanity (1 Timothy 2:5–6).

He’s the answer to every problem, the solution to the pain, misery, decay, and disease in the world. He’s the life-giver. He’s the true God and eternal life: “And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us an understanding, that we may know Him who is true; and we are in Him who is true, in His Son Jesus Christ. This is the true God and eternal life” (1 John 5:20). Even as teens, we can make this real—through our words, our actions, and sharing Jesus with the people around us.

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for committing to me the responsibility of the Gospel. Help me, even as a teenager, to reach my friends, schoolmates, and community with the message of Christ’s love, power, and righteousness. I’m empowered by your Spirit to reach my world with the message of Christ’s love, power, and righteousness. Through us, your grace and truth are made known everywhere and prevail in every nation, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Stop saying "I’m not ready." God already made you an able minister. Speak to one person today, trusting His sufficiency.
