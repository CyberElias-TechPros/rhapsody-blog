As God’s child, your life isn’t supposed to be full of defeat or fear—God wants you to live as a winner every day! When you were born again, He gave you a brand-new life in Christ, and that life is full of victory. Glory to God!

So, when you face challenges, God’s Word tells you to “...count it all joy...” (James 1:2), because nothing can truly defeat a child of God.

Always choose faith over fear, courage over worry, and joy over sadness (Isaiah 61:3). The Bible says, “...When you walk through the fire, you shall not be burned...” (Isaiah 43:2).

And Jesus said, “...be of good cheer; I have overcome the world!” (John 16:33). This means that with Jesus in your life, you always win. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Thank you, precious Father, for the victorious life you’ve given me. I live above sickness, fear, and failure. I walk in constant triumph, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
