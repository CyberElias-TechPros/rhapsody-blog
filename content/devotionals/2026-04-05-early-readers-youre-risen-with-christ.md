The Bible says you sit with Jesus in a special place of power and glory. That’s a place of joy, victory, and authority—far above sickness, fear, and defeat.

You may be wondering, “How did that happen?” Here’s the answer: When Jesus suffered, was crucified, and died on the Cross, He did it for everyone. He was the sacrifice for the sins of the whole world— including yours. So, you were in Him when He died. And when God raised Him from the dead, He raised you with Him.

That means the moment you were born again and received eternal life, you were lifted into this place of authority. You’re no longer down, defeated, or trying to climb up. God Himself has already placed you in a throne-room position with Christ.

So live boldly! Walk in victory every day! You’re seated with Jesus in power and glory. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for this glorious life I have, where I dwell in health, prosperity, and victory every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
