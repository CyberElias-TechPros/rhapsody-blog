David was a young boy when he stood before the giant Goliath, yet he was not afraid (1 Samuel 17). While others were frightened, David trusted God. He believed that God was greater than any giant. David loved God deeply and wanted His Name to be honoured; that was what made him different.

You’re God’s child, and that makes you different too. Whether at school, at home, or with friends, choose to think and act as God’s child.

So, don’t be afraid to stand out. Love God boldly and live for Him. As you do, your life will show His glory everywhere you go.

## Confession

I am standing strong for the Lord; therefore, I’m a burning and shining light for Him! Hallelujah!
