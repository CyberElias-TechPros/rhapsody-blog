Going to church is very important. It helps us learn about God, grow in faith, and be close to Him. God wants us to meet with other children of His, encourage one another, and be part of His family.

A good example are the early Christians in the Bible. Daily, they came together to pray, sing, and learn from God’s Word. They didn’t stay home; they wanted to grow stronger in their faith, and help others do the same (Acts 2:42-47).

Church is where you’re trained to live God’s way and to do His will. When you go to church, you get to hear God’s Word, meet leaders who can guide you, and make friends who love God too. So, make church attendance a priority for the rest of this year.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for making me part of your family. I love your Word and choose to live by it every day. Your Word guides me, and my life brings glory to you, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.1717
