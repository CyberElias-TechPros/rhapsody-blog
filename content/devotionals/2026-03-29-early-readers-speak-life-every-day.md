Your words are powerful! When you speak, things happen—so use your words to bring life, joy, and peace.

Don’t speak fear or complain. Rather, say what God says. Speak blessings over your family, your school, and your friends.

God has made your mouth a fountain of life. Keep speaking life every day, and you’ll see good things happen all around you!

## Confession

My words bring life and blessing everywhere I go. I speak faith, love, and victory every day!
