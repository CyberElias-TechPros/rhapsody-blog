As God’s child, your life should not only be about personal plans, games, or toys. Living for Jesus and helping others know Him should be the most important thing in your life. This is your special assignment.

The Bible tells us about Paul, who felt it was his duty to preach the Gospel everywhere he went. He lived to make Jesus known everywhere he went (1 Corinthians 9:16). Just like Paul, you’re God’s representative in your world. He trusts you to share His message of love, forgiveness, and grace with others around you.

So, be kind, be bold, and use every means to tell others about Jesus, so they can have hope and live for God too.

## Prayer

Thank you, heavenly Father, for making me your representative in my school, neighborhood, and community. Through me, others will know your love, kindness, and grace, in Jesus’ Name. Amen. 1212
