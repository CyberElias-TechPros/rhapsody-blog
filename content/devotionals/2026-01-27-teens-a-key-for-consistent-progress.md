Praying in the Spirit is not a casual exercise; it is a vital spiritual practice that sustains and strengthens you. God designed prayer—especially praying in tongues— as a divine means of keeping your spirit healthy, vibrant, and strong. When you pray in tongues, you edify yourself. It’s like charging a battery until it is full. Your spirit is energised, fortified, and refreshed, enabling you to function effectively in the things of God. No wonder Paul declared in Romans 1:9 NKJV, “For God is my witness, whom I serve with my spirit in the gospel of His Son....” We serve God with our spirit, and for that spirit to remain effective, it must continually be built up.

The Bible shows us that the human spirit can either be weak or strong. To strengthen your spirit, you must give yourself to praying in the Holy Ghost. As you do, you rise higher and higher like an edifice, strong and unshakeable. Paul understood this deeply. He prayed that the Ephesian church be strengthened with might through God’s Spirit in their inner man (Ephesians 3:16). The word “might” here refers to miracle-working ability infused into your spirit by the Holy Ghost. That was Paul’s secret to his continuous growth, victories, and extraordinary accomplishments. He could boldly say, “I thank my God I speak with tongues more than you all” (1 Corinthians 14:18 NKJV).

Paul’s consistency in praying in tongues was key to his continuous progress, victories and extraordinary accomplishments. It wasn’t something he did occasionally; it was a lifestyle. If you desire consistent growth, success, and advancement, then praying in the Spirit must be something you do consistently. It’ll help you make consistent progress as you experience continuous increase in grace and victory after victory. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear Father, I thank you for the blessing of praying in the Spirit through which I build up and strengthen my spirit to serve you effectively. Even now, my spirit is attuned to your guidance and direction, and I walk in perpetual victory, progress and ever- increasing grace, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Take several 15-minute breaks today to speak in tongues.
