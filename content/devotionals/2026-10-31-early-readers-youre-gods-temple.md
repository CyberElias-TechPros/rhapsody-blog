As God’s child, you’re always in His presence because the Holy Spirit lives in you. You don’t have to wait until you’re in church to enjoy Him—you carry His presence everywhere you go. That’s why you can be joyful, calm, and confident every day.

When you were born again, God made your heart His home. Jesus said the Holy Spirit would live in you forever (John 14:16–17). This means you’re never alone—God is with you at home, in school, and everywhere.

So always remember who you are. Live with joy, boldness, and peace every day.

## Prayer

Thank you, heavenly Father, for making me your home. You’re alive and at work in me. Everywhere I go, I carry your presence, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
