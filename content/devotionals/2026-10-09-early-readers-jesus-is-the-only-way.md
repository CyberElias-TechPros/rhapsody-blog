The Bible teaches that Jesus is the only way to God. This means we receive salvation and eternal life through Him alone. There’s no other name by which we can be saved (Acts 4:12).

Jesus came to give us eternal life—the God-kind of life. He said that those who believe in Him will never perish (John 10:28). That is why it’s important to tell others about Jesus.

In the Bible, Peter boldly preached about Jesus, and many people believed and were saved (Acts 2:38–41). You can do the same. Share the good news and help others come into God’s family.

## Prayer

I refuse to be silent about God’s love. I’ll share the good news of Jesus with boldness, helping many come into God’s family, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
