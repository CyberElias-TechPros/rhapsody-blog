The Apostle Paul told Timothy, “… be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus” (2 Timothy 2:1). That means Timothy didn’t have to wait for it. As God’s child, you can do the same!

You can take grace to be wise, do well in school, be kind, excellent, and become everything God wants you to be. Think about filling a cup with water—you can keep pouring until it’s full. That’s how grace works too! You can take as much as you need.

The Lord isn’t holding anything back. So, take all the grace you need to do great things, and live your best life in Christ! Hallelujah!

## Confession

Dear loving Father, I thank you for your grace that’s working in me. Today, I receive more grace to grow, shine, and be the best in all I do. Hallelujah!
