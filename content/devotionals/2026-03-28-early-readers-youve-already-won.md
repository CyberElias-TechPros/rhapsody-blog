As a child of God, you’re not trying to win—you’ve already won! When you were born again, God made you a winner for life.

This means no challenge, no sickness, and no trouble can defeat you. You’ve got the victory through Jesus Christ!

When things get hard, remind yourself: I’m born of God, and I’ve overcome the world! That’s your winning confession every day!

## Confession

I’m born of God, and I’ve already won! No challenge can defeat me because Jesus lives in me.
