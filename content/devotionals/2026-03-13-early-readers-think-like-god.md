When you were born again, your spirit became new, but your mind needs to be renewed with God’s Word. That means learning to think like God—seeing things the way He does.

For example, when others say, “It’s too hard,” you say, “I can do all things through Christ!” When others fear, you declare, “The Lord is my helper!”

The more you study God’s Word, the more your thoughts, words, and actions will look like God’s. That’s how you live a winning life!

## Confession

God’s Word fills my mind and shapes my thoughts. Therefore, I think faith, speak faith, and live in victory.
