God has given you many wonderful blessings:

peace, joy, victory, and His Word. But God doesn’t

want you to only know about them. He wants you

to enjoy them every day.

In the Bible, there was a man named Caleb.

God promised him a place called Hebron. Even

though giants were living there, Caleb wasn’t

afraid. He trusted God and said, “...the Lord will

be with me...” And He did! Caleb received what

God had promised him (Joshua 14:12-14).

We shouldn’t let fear, doubt, or problems stop

us from enjoying what God has already given us

in Christ. When we trust God, speak His Word,

and stay strong in faith, we can enjoy all His Scan toblessings.

God is with you, and He helps you every step of

the way!

## Confession

Dear faithful Father, thank you for giving me a special place in your family and for the wonderful blessings in Christ. I enjoy all the blessings you give to me every day. Hallelujah!21
