Long ago, God’s prophets talked about how the Son of God would suffer and then be glorified. Jesus already suffered, so now is the time of glory! Hallelujah! He took away sin, sickness, and pain so you can live the beautiful life God planned for you.

The Bible says Jesus became poor so you could be rich (2 Corinthians 8:9), and He took your sin so you could be made right with God (2 Corinthians 5:21). That means you don’t have to suffer for what Jesus already paid for. You’ve been called to glory and excellence (2 Peter 1:3).

So, walk boldly in health, joy, and success everyday—because that’s your inheritance in Christ! Everything changed when Jesus rose again, and now you live in victory!

## Prayer

Dear precious Father, thank you for the glorious life I have in Christ. I live in health, joy, and victory every day, displaying your love and grace to my world, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
