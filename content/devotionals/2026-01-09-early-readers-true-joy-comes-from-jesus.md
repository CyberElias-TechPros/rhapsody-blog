People have created many amazing things—airplanes, phones, toys, and games. You might think all these things should make everyone happy. However, even with all this, some people still don’t feel joyful or complete. That’s because true joy doesn’t come from things—it comes from Jesus!

It’s the same with some children. They may have many toys, nice clothes, or gadgets, yet they still feel sad or empty inside. Only Jesus can fill our hearts with real joy and peace that never ends.

So, don’t look for happiness in things around you. Look to Jesus. When you walk with Him and let His love fill your heart, you’ll always have joy, peace, and purpose in life. Hallelujah!

## Confession

Dear Lord Jesus, thank you for being my joy and peace. My heart is full of joy because I have you, and in you I live every day with purpose and gladness. Hallelujah!
