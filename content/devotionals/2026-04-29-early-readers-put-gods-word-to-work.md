God’s Word is powerful—but it only works for you when you use it! It’s not enough to just read it or hear it at church. You must believe it, speak it, and act on it with confidence. Peter and John showed us exactly how this works. One day, they met a man who had never walked before. He asked them for money, but Peter had something far better to give. He boldly said, “...In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk.” Peter reached out, took the man’s hand, and immediately the man’s feet and legs became strong. He jumped up and began walking, leaping, and praising God! (Acts 3:1–8).

Peter didn’t just know the Word—he did the Word. And because he acted on it, a miracle happened!

You can do the same. When you face a challenge, don’t stay quiet—use God’s Word. Believe it, speak it, act on it. That’s how you enjoy all the blessings God has already given you.

## Confession

God’s Word is in my heart and in my mouth. As I act on it, it produces blessings and miracles in and around me. Glory to God!
