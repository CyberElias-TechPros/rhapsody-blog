Engineers have long dreamed of creating a truly universal remote control that can operate every device regardless of brand, model, or age. The challenge is that different systems use different frequencies, protocols, and codes. Yet, in the realm of the spirit, there exists a “Master Code” that works on every problem, whether it’s physical, emotional, relational, or financial—the Name of Jesus!

The Bible tells us that Peter and John encountered a man who was lame from birth at the gate of the temple called Beautiful. Yet, when Peter said to the man, “In the Name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk,” life came into those dead limbs, and the man leapt and walked! If Peter had shouted, “In the name of Caesar, rise up and walk!” nothing would have happened. If he had yelled as loud as he could, “In the name of Pontius Pilate, rise up and walk,” nothing would have happened either. It had to be “In the Name of Jesus,” for that’s the Name that’s above every name; that’s the Name that unlocks every door! Hallelujah!

No matter the critical situation in which you’ve found yourself, you can use the Name of Jesus to effect a change. Maybe it feels like failing an important exam, being left out by your friends, battling anxiety before a presentation,

1010 or dealing with online bullying. It may be impossible for you on your own to get out of that negative situation, just like the crippled man at the gate couldn’t walk by his own ability, but through the power in the Name of Jesus, you can walk out of any trouble. All you need to do is call that Name in faith, and you’ll walk out of fear, anxiety, depression, and failure.

The Name of Jesus is the Name you can trust; it’s the name that never fails! By the power of that name, I prophesy to you today: you’re walking out of that difficulty. If you’ve been sick, I pronounce you healed! If you couldn’t walk, begin to walk! I declare success, promotion, increase, and prosperity for you, in the mighty Name of Jesus! Whatever you couldn’t do before, do it now, in the all-powerful Name of Jesus! Glory to God!

## Prayer

Precious Heavenly Father, I thank you for the wonderful Name of Jesus, the Name that’s above every name, and to which all of creation responds. Thank you for giving me the right to use that name to effect positive changes in my life and in the lives of my loved ones. I know that I can never be disadvantaged in this life because I live by the power of that Name! Amen.
