Jesus is more than a great teacher or a good man.

The Bible teaches that all the fullness of God lives in Him. This means when we see Jesus, we see what God is like. Jesus showed God’s love, kindness, wisdom, and power everywhere He went. He helped people, healed the sick, and taught them about God’s Kingdom. That’s why Jesus said, “...He who has seen Me has seen the Father...” (John 14:9). When you learn about Jesus in the Bible, you’re learning about God Himself.

The more you know Jesus, the more you understand God’s love and goodness.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for showing us who you are through Jesus. As I learn about Him in your Word, I grow to know and love you more every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
