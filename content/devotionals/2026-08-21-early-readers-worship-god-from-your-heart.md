God loves it when we worship Him sincerely. True worship does not come from showing off or following empty traditions. It comes from a heart that loves God.

When you pray, sing, and thank God sincerely, you’re offering true worship. You don’t need special objects or actions to worship God, because He lives in you through His Spirit. Your songs, prayers, and words of praise that come from your heart are precious to Him.

Always worship God with love, joy, and sincerity, knowing that He listens and delights in the praise of His children.

## Confession

I worship God from my heart. My songs, prayers, and praise are full of love for Him every day. Hallelujah!
