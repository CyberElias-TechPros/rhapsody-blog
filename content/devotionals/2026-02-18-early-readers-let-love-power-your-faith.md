Faith is powerful! But did you know that love is what makes it work? When you walk in love—being kind, forgiving, and patient—your faith becomes strong and unstoppable!

That’s why it’s so important to love others, even when it’s not easy. Love is the way of Jesus, and it’s the secret behind a strong life of faith.

So, keep your heart full of love every day. Speak kindly, forgive quickly, and let love power your faith!

## Confession

I walk in love every day. My love makes my faith strong, and through faith, I change my world.
