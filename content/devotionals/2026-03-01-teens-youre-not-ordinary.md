A small flame in the middle of a vast, dark auditorium may seem insignificant at first glance. Yet, as your eyes adjust, you realise something profound: that one flame is enough to conquer the shadows. It changes the atmosphere, shifting the focus from darkness to light. Beloved, that is exactly how the Spirit of God describes you.

Jesus declared, “You are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hidden” (Matthew 5:14 NKJV). You’re not an accident in this world. You were born with divine intent—to radiate God’s glory, inspire hope, and manifest His goodness. Your presence in any environment should bring illumination, direction, and life.

Think of Jesus Himself. His public ministry was brief— just over three years—but the impact is eternal. What was His secret? Acts 10:38 NKJV reveals it: “How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power….” That same anointing is upon you today through the indwelling Spirit. You’re not ordinary; you’re God’s answer to your generation.

The Apostle Paul reminds us, “For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power and of love and of a sound mind” (2 Timothy 1:7 NKJV). Fear, timidity, or failure cannot define you because the Spirit within you produces boldness, wisdom, and excellence. In fact, the Bible declares in 1 John 2:20 KJV, “But ye have an unction from the Holy One, and ye know all things.” That means you have divine insight, supernatural ability, and the wisdom to influence your world.

Therefore, never see yourself as ordinary. Carry this consciousness everywhere: you’re anointed to make a difference! Like that candle in the auditorium, your light can’t be hidden. Shine in your family, your school, your community, and your nation. Let men see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven.

## Speak

I’m the light of the world. I’m anointed by God, filled with His Spirit, and positioned to shine. I’m not ordinary; I’m empowered with divine wisdom, insight, and excellence. I do not have the Spirit of fear but of power, love and a sound mind. I influence my world with the glory of God, and my light can never be hidden. Today and always, I shine brightly to the glory of my Father in heaven. Amen.

## Act

Today, step out with confidence and look for one way to shine your light—whether by sharing encouragement, helping someone in need, or boldly expressing your faith.
