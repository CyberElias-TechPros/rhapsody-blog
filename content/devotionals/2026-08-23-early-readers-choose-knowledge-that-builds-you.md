Not all knowledge is helpful, so it’s important to choose the right kind. God wants you to fill your heart and mind with knowledge that builds you up.

The best knowledge comes from God’s Word because it teaches you how to live, think, and make wise decisions. When you study the Bible and listen to godly teaching, your faith becomes stronger.

This kind of knowledge helps you grow into the wonderful person God created you to be. Always choose to learn things that encourage your faith and help you become wiser, stronger, and more excellent every day.

## Confession

God’s Word fills my mind with wisdom and understanding. I grow stronger and wiser every day as I learn and follow His truth. Hallelujah!
