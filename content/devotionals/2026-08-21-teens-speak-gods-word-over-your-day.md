There’s a beautiful confidence that fills your heart when you realise your life isn’t a product of chance. Imagine a master conductor leading an orchestra—every instrument comes in at the right time, every note perfectly arranged. In the same way, your life in Christ is divinely orchestrated. Nothing about you is accidental; nothing around you is random.

As Christians, we don’t operate by chance, nor do we go anywhere or meet anyone by accident. That means even at school, practice, or hanging out with friends, it’s not random or meaningless. If you don’t understand this truth, you won’t be able to control what happens to you and take possession of what belongs to you. But when you know how God works, and you discover your position in Christ, you’ll realise nothing happens to you by accident, chance or coincidence.

The Spirit of God brings divine opportunities, but it’s your responsibility to recognise and maximise each one of them. Sometimes those opportunities look like a new friendship, a chance to lead a project, helping someone who feels left out, or making the right choice when others don’t. This is the reason you must learn to declare God’s Word into your day every morning, before you go out.

Speak peace and productivity to your day, and the Spirit of God will lead you to the right places at the right time! He can guide your conversations, your decisions, and even your reactions. This is how to operate at the level of the supernatural, where you know nothing will ever happen to you by chance, for God isn’t a God of chance. I’m sure you understand now why you need to arm yourself with God’s Word.

Tell yourself, “Today, I’m going to walk into divine opportunities. I’m going to put the divinity of God in me to work.” Say it confidently, even if you feel nervous or pressured. That means in every situation you find yourself, you’re going to recognise the presence of God, and claim for yourself everything good and godly, and refuse to give attention to anything contrary. Even when distractions or peer pressure show up, stay focused on who you are in Christ and choose what honours Him.

## Speak

I’m not a child of chance. God has prearranged my life for good. I walk those paths that have been laid out for me before the foundations of the world, and I prosper in all my ways. Glory to God!

## Act

Before you leave your room, speak aloud God’s Word over your day. Declare that He’ll guide your steps, open doors, and help you make the right choices in every situation.
