Some have recited our opening verse countless times without realizing it’s from the Scriptures. To them, it’s merely a closing prayer at the end of a service. Yet, in that scripture, we’ve been blessed with three indispensable provisions for life’s journey. Any Christian, anywhere, regardless of city or nation, who walks by these three important keys will live victoriously in every circumstance, irrespective of the challenges they may face.

The first one is the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ. What does this mean? The Bible says in John 1:17, “For the law was given by Moses, but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.” This means you couldn’t really know God until Jesus came. The prophets of the Old Testament brought God’s message but they didn’t express the real character of our heavenly Father.

Study the Old Testament; you’d find the Law and the judgments that made the people fear God. But the Lord Jesus came and expressed the love of God. He said, “…he that hath seen me hath seen the Father...” (John 14:9). To know God, you must know Jesus. He is the One who expressed God’s character: His person and His reality. Grace and truth (reality) came by Jesus Christ.

The second thing is the love of God. God wants us to walk in the light of His love. Understand that all love is not the same. This isn’t about a mother’s love or a friend’s love. It’s a different kind of love, love that knows no bounds. Love that’s beyond this world. Love that’s liquid and flows eternally.

The third one, the communion of the Holy Spirit, is so important because the Father and the Lord Jesus are in heaven, but the Holy Spirit is here with us. He’s in you and will always be. That fellowship is your greatest blessing and advantage in life.

God’s desire is that you walk in the light of these three amazing blessings; they are to be your daily realities. When you understand them and walk in their light, it won’t matter where you are, where you’re placed, or what comes against you, you’ll always rise above and remain victorious. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear Father, I walk daily in the fullness of your blessings, guided by your grace, rooted in your love, and sustained by unbroken fellowship with the Holy Spirit. My life is victorious, excellent and ever triumphant, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
