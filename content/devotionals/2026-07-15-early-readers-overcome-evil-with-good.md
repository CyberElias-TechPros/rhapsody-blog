Kindness is powerful. When someone is unkind to you, it may feel natural to respond the same way. But God teaches us a higher way. The Bible says we should overcome evil with good (Romans 12:21).

Think about what happens when you turn on a light in a dark room; darkness disappears immediately! In the same way, love and kindness are stronger than unkindness. Jesus showed us this by loving people, even when they treated Him badly.

When you choose to forgive, help, and speak kindly, you shine God’s light in a dark world. Your good actions can help others see God’s love. So always choose good, no matter what anyone does to you. You’re God’s shining light, and light always wins.

## Confession

God is in me, and His love is shed abroad in my heart. Therefore, I’m full of love towards everyone around me, and His love is seen and heard in my words and 22actions always. Amen.
