Thanksgiving is more than giving offerings in church or talking about what God has done sometimes. It should be your way of life as a child of God. The Lord wants you to thank Him every day, not only when something special happens.

A thankful heart is always joyful and ready to receive more from the Lord.

Jesus lived this way. Before raising Lazarus from the dead, He first gave thanks to God (John 11:41). When He fed five thousand people with five loaves and two fishes, He also gave thanks before the miracle happened (John 6:11). Jesus showed us that thanksgiving comes before great results.

So learn to always thank the Lord. Make praise and thanksgiving your daily habit, and you will live in joy and see His goodness in your life.

## Prayer

Glorious heavenly Father, thank you for your love and kindness to me every day. My heart is always full of praise, and thanksgiving, in Jesus’ Name. 14Amen.
