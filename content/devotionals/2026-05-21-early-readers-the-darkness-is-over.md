The Bible tells us that people who once walked in darkness have now seen a great light. That light is Jesus. When you received Jesus into your heart, His light came into your life. Darkness can mean fear, sickness, sin, or sadness. But because you’re in Christ, darkness has no power over you anymore. You live in God’s light every day.

The Bible says God’s light shines in our hearts, and nothing can stop it. So when things feel difficult at school, at home, or anywhere else, remember this truth: the light of Jesus lives in you, and it drives away darkness.

So say it boldly and joyfully: “The darkness is over! God’s light shines in me forever!” Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for Jesus, the light of the world. Thank you for bringing light and life into my heart. Help me share this light with others, in Jesus’ 28Name. Amen.
