The Gospel of Jesus Christ is not a fable or fairy tale; it’s real. The Lord Jesus truly came. He truly died, was buried, rose again, and ascended into Heaven. These aren’t symbolic acts; they’re historical and spiritual truths with eternal significance. Many people today, especially young people, think Christianity is just another religion or moral idea, but they’re completely wrong.

Christianity is the life of God manifested in human flesh. We serve and preach a living Christ, One who can be known, experienced, and proven. He’s not just someone we read about in the Bible; He’s someone we can know personally. The Holy Spirit was sent to help us demonstrate the reality of the Gospel. He confirms the Word, the Gospel we preach, with signs, miracles, and wonders.

Never allow doubt or unbelief to dilute your faith. Don’t let social media, peer pressure, or negative comments make you question what you believe. The proof of our message is the supernatural life we live. Every time you pray for someone, share Jesus with a friend, or stand for what is right, you’re proving that the Lord Jesus is alive and the Gospel is the power of God unto salvation to everyone who believes (Romans 1:16).

The world doesn’t need another argument about religion; they need proof. And you’re the proof and proof producer that the Gospel is real, the Lord Jesus is real, and that eternal life is available today only in Him and through Him. Hallelujah!

## Speak

Blessed Father, how glorious it is to be a witness of the resurrected Christ; my life is proof that Jesus is alive. Help me to boldly live for you in my school, among my friends, and everywhere I go. Through me, the world experiences the reality of the Gospel. Thank you for causing me to walk in the supernatural, manifesting the glory and power of the Gospel as I lead many to righteousness, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Share the Word of God with someone today; it doesn’t matter where you are—in a bus, in your community—let them know about Christ.
