God’s Word is like a mirror. When you study it, He shows you who you truly are in Christ. The Bible says, “There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus” (Romans 8:1). That means God is not angry with you. He loves you completely.

If you make a mistake, don’t despair or think you’re not good enough. God calls you His beloved child, and His Spirit lives in you. He wants you to see yourself the way He sees you—loved, holy, and made right through Jesus.

So, the more you look into His Word, you’ll see the wonderful person God made you to be: special, royal, beloved, and righteous.

## Confession

I know who I am: I’m God’s child. I’m special and loved. I have God’s nature in me. Hallelujah!
