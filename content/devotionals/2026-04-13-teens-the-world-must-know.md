The greatest truth in all the world is this: Jesus Christ is alive! This is not a story from history or a doctrine preserved through time. It’s the very pulse of our faith, the power of the Gospel, and the living reality of Christianity. The same Jesus who walked the streets of Galilee, who healed the sick and raised the dead, is still doing the same works through us—His Church—today.

When I began holding crusades many years ago, my passion was to present this living Christ to the people. Every handbill declared it boldly: “Presenting Jesus Christ Alive!” It wasn’t a slogan; it was a revelation. I knew that Jesus was not confined to the pages of Scripture or the memories of history—He was, and still is, the same yesterday, today, and forever!

In one of our early camp meetings, the Spirit of God gave us the theme: “Beyond the Written Pages.” How powerful! The Bible isn’t just a record of what God once did; it’s the revelation of what He’s still doing today through His Spirit. The Word has leapt off the pages and lives in us— hallelujah!

Christianity is not about following a historical figure; it’s the manifestation of the living Christ in and through you. This is our calling—to take this message to the nations, to let the world know: Jesus is alive and His life is in us!

So, wherever you are, whatever you do—present Him alive! Let His love, His power, and His glory flow through you. The world must know: Jesus Christ is alive today! Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for the consistency of your vision and the revelation of Jesus Christ alive today. I’m taking the message beyond borders, presenting the living Christ to my world and beyond. The Church is marching forward, expanding and spreading the message of your love, power and grace through the nooks and crannies of the earth, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Boldly share the message of Christ with someone today.
