God wants us to pray for our leaders and for our country. When we do, peace fills our cities and nations, and people can hear the Gospel freely.

Instead of complaining about what’s wrong in the world, pray! Ask God to guide your leaders, give them wisdom, and help them make good decisions that bless everyone.

You have power through prayer. So, every time you pray for others, remember—you’re helping to bring peace and godliness into the world.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for making me a helper through prayer. I pray for my leaders, my nation, and my city, that your peace and love will fill every home, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
