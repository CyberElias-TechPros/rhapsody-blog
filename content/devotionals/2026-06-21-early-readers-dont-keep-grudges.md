God wants His children to walk in love and be at peace with everyone. When you keep grudges in your heart, it can stop the flow of God’s blessings in your life. In James 5:16, the Bible teaches us to forgive, apologize when we’re wrong, and pray for one another.

Joseph is a wonderful example in the Bible. Even though his brothers treated him badly, he chose to forgive them and show them kindness (Genesis 50:20– 21). Because he forgave, God used him to save others.

Even when you feel you’re right, God wants you to choose peace and continue to walk in love.

## Prayer

Thank you, precious Father, for giving me the Holy Spirit. He helps me stay calm and display good attitudes. I show love, kindness, and forgive others easily because your love is in 2828 me, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
