An ambassador represents their country in another place. In the same way, you represent Jesus here on earth (2 Corinthians 5:20). This means you show God’s love, truth, and power everywhere you go.

When you speak God’s Word and pray, heaven responds. Jesus said that what you declare in line with God’s will is established (Matthew 18:18). That means your words matter.

So, think and act like God’s ambassador. Let your words, actions, and choices show His light and love to others every day.

## Prayer

Thank you, precious Father, for making me your representative. Through me, your wisdom, power, and love are seen everywhere I go, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
