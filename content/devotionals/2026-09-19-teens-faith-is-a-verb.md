Faith, the Bible says, is the substance of what you hoped for, and the title-deed of unseen realities. In other words, faith means that the things you were hoping for are now your present-hour possession. This is where the demonstration of faith comes to play. If it’s true that what you hoped for is now yours, then you ought to act that way. For example, you might have prayed to God for something concerning yourself, believing He’d do it.

If you truly believe that God heard your prayer, then you shouldn’t come back tomorrow and pray about the same thing again. You should now begin to act like someone who has received from God: that’s how to put your faith to work. That’s what Marcus’ Dad is saying in the opening conversation.

When Peter and John met the crippled man at the gate of the temple called Beautiful, Peter said to the man, “Silver and gold I do not have, but what I do have I give you: In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk” (Acts 3:6). Peter then grabbed the man by the hand, and lifted him. The Bible says, “So he, leaping up, stood and walked and entered the temple with them—walking, leaping, and praising God” (Acts 3:8).

The man didn’t try to test whether he could stand up; he jumped up! That’s a demonstration of faith. He believed he’d been healed, and he immediately acted that way, and he began to walk instantly. Understand that faith is active and not passive; faith acts now because it’s taken possession. That’s why the Bible says, ”… faith by itself, if it does not have works, is dead” (James 2:17). That means faith without corresponding actions is dead. The faith that works is when you believe that what God says are yours are yours, and then you act accordingly.

## Speak

My faith is producing results; I don’t waver or stagger at the Word of God through unbelief. I believe that God is who He says He is, and that I have what He says I have, and I can do what He says I can do. My faith is the victory that overcomes the world. Hallelujah!

## Act

Take one physical step today that proves you believe your prayer is already answered. If you’ve prayed for a new opportunity, clear your schedule for it or prepare the “outfit” you’ll wear for that success.
