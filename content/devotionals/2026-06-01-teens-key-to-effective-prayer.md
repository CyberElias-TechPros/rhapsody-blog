You can sit in class, eyes on the board, appearing attentive—yet your mind is miles away. Then the lesson ends, and you suddenly realize you didn’t absorb a single thing. Sometimes, that’s how people pray. Their spirit is talking to God, but their mind is busy somewhere else. Meanwhile, praying in tongues is one of the most powerful spiritual exercises you can engage in. It’s the language of the Spirit through which you utter divine mysteries. When you pray in tongues, your spirit communicates directly with God, bypassing the limitations of the natural mind.

However, your prayer becomes far more impactful and effective when your mind is engaged as you speak in tongues. Just like when you’re studying, listening in class, or playing a game, you need to stay focused. Fix your attention on what you’re praying about. There may be the tendency for your mind to wander as you pray, maybe thinking about your phone, music, friends, or what you’ll do next, but refuse the distractions and deliberately keep your mind focused on the Spirit.

As you do, He’ll flood your mind with visions, thoughts, and ideas. These may be revelations from God or creative insights that He inspires within you. He enables you to see possibilities, create godly imaginations, and bring divine realities into manifestation. You may suddenly receive ideas for your schoolwork, your future, or how to help others. As He grants you visions, respond accordingly; that’s how you partner with Him to bring Heaven’s plans to pass on earth.

The Christian life is a walk in the Spirit. The Spirit-filled life is a life of rest, joy, and victory. He keeps you strong, refreshed and full of faith, even in school, at home, and among your friends. So, even in prayer, depend on His guidance, follow His instructions, and yield to His influence. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear Father, as I pray in the Spirit, you help me understand your plans for my life and guide my daily choices, and I speak and act in ways that bring your will to pass in my school, home, and friendships. Thank you for helping my heart and mind stay connected to you, causing me to make wise decisions, walk in confidence, and live for your glory, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
