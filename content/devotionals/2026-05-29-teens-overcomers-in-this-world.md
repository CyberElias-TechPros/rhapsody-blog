What does it truly mean to be “in the world but not of the world”? The Lord Jesus Himself declared this powerful truth, reminding us that as Christians, we are handpicked by God, set apart, and called to live by divine principles that transcend earthly limitations. We’re not ordinary; we’re children of the Kingdom—destined to rule and reign over the very systems and circumstances that others see as obstacles.

The world may be full of trials and tribulations, but listen closely to the words of our Lord Jesus in John 16:33 NKJV: “These things I have spoken to you, that in Me you may have peace. In the world you will have tribulation; but be of good cheer, I have overcome the world.” He (Jesus) overcame the world—not for Himself alone, but for YOU. When you accepted Him, you stepped into His victory, and that victory is your inheritance.

This means you’re superior to the forces of darkness, the corrupting influences of this age, and every challenge that stands as an obstacle in your path. You’re royalty! You belong to a higher realm, a realm of dominion and excellence. While others complain, you bring solutions. While the crowd demands change, you effect change through your faith-filled words and prayers.

Remember, you have a Father greater than all, and His Spirit lives within you. You represent His excellence and righteousness in a dark world. As 1 Peter 2:9 NKJV reminds us, “But you are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, His own special people, that you may proclaim the praises of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light.” Walk in this truth today!

Hear me, you are Heaven’s best—chosen, royal, and supremely blessed. Live as the overcomer you are. The world is yours to rule!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for choosing me out of the world and setting me apart for your glory. I’m conscious of my divine origin and heavenly calling. I function from the realm of glory, shining your light and dispensing your grace to my world. I live as royalty, revealing the beauty and excellence of the Kingdom, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Declare with faith today: “I am more than a conqueror through Christ; I walk in victory, ruling and reigning over every circumstance in Jesus’ Name!”
