When Jesus was born, King Herod wanted to harm Him. But God sent an angel to Joseph in a dream and told him, “...Arise, take the young Child and His mother, flee to Egypt, and stay there...” (Matthew 2:13-14). Joseph obeyed God’s instruction, and that’s how Jesus was protected from Herod’s evil plan.

Also, in Exodus 12, God told the Israelites to mark their doors with the blood of a lamb and stay inside. Everyone who obeyed was protected, including the “mixed multitude” who followed God’s instruction.

God protects us the same way today. Your safety is found in listening to His Word and doing what He says. So, learn to obey instructions because your protection and progress are in them.

## Confession

I am in Christ, and He is in me; therefore, no harm can come near me, because God’s Word is my life, and I walk in His care. Hallelujah! 1414
