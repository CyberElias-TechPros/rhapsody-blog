In our opening scripture, the Apostle Paul reveals a profound spiritual principle: in the Kingdom of God, faith is not always one-directional; there’s such a thing as mutual faith, faith responding to faith, faith working together to produce results.

Paul, though an apostle with extraordinary grace, didn’t see himself as the only one with faith or the only one bringing the “supply.” He recognised that as he ministered to the saints in Rome, there would also be a supply coming from them to him. It reminds of what the Bible says: the body grows by that which every joint supplies (Ephesians 4:16).

Every member has a supply, something to contribute. And when those supplies come together, the Body is strengthened. This is what mutual faith produces: shared participation in spiritual realities. When faith responds to faith, something happens in the realm of the Spirit. There’s an activation, a release, an unhindered flow of the Spirit of God. Ministry becomes more effective because of the unity and synchrony of our mutual faith. This is why your response matters. Remember, faith is the response of the human spirit to the Word of God.

When the Word is being taught, respond; engage your spirit. The Bible says, “For unto us was the gospel preached… but the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard it” (Hebrews 4:2). The Word must be met with faith to produce results. Paul says, “...that I may be comforted together with you by the mutual faith both of you and me” (Romans 1:12).

That word “comforted” means strengthened, encouraged, built up. Both the one ministering and the one receiving are edified. Now, there’s something else that comes out of this—unity. When there’s a flow of the Spirit, especially through signs and wonders, it produces a certain kind of unity among God’s people.

Those who walk in the miraculous understand it’s not by human ability. They recognise their dependence on God. As a result, they learn to honour the grace of God in others. This is why those who function in the Spirit walk in greater unity. They’re not quick to criticise or tear others down. They recognise the same hand of God at work. Mutual faith, therefore, is not just about receiving; it’s about alignment, honour, and unity in the Spirit.

## Prayer

Dear Father, I thank you for the spirit of faith at work in me. I respond actively to your Word and to the ministration of the Spirit. I honour the grace of God in others and walk in unity, humility and power. My faith is effective, producing tangible results and establishing your will, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
