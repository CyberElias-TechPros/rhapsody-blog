When you study the Scriptures, you’ll discover a consistent pattern in the lives of Abraham’s physical descendants: no matter how humble their beginnings, they ended in dominion and expansion. Consider Isaac; the Bible says he “...waxed great, and went forward, and grew until he became very great… and the Philistines envied him” (Genesis 26:13–14 Kjv). His life was a story of unstoppable increase.

If that was the testimony of the natural seed, how much more you who are the true seed of Abraham through Christ (Galatians 3:29)! The natural progression of your life is increase, advancement, and expansion in everything you do. Stagnation is inconsistent with your spiritual DNA. When you received the Holy Spirit, He’s supposed to do much more than make you speak in tongues and prophesy. He’ll cause everything you do to grow and expand. That anointing is the “mimshach” anointing, which in Hebrew means “expansion” or “to spread.” It causes everything you do to multiply and expand beyond your immediate sphere of contact.

God wants you to recognise that this grace is already at work in you. Maybe you’re in school, maybe you’re building a brand, coding an app, running a small business, creating content, or figuring out your next move; don’t despise small beginnings. That small channel can grow. That campus idea can become a company or even a conglomerate. So, start making plans for a greater and higher level at which you want to function. Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear Lord, thank you for the Holy Spirit that causes me to grow, increase, and make progress in every area of my life. As I recognise and take advantage of the power of the Holy Spirit today, I’m making progress in my studies, developing my talents, growing in influence, and becoming all you’ve destined me to be, in the Name of the Lord Jesus. Amen.

## Act

Today, take a practical step to “enlarge your tent.” If you want to improve your grades, learn something new or develop a talent, take the next step. Sign up for a course, practise a skill, or start working towards a goal you’ve been putting off.
