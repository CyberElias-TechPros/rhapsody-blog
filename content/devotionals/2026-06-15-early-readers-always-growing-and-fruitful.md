As God’s beloved child, His blessing works in your life everywhere you go. That means no matter where you are or what is happening around you, God’s desire is for you to grow and succeed. He has chosen you to be fruitful at all times.

Just like Isaac, who planted seeds during a tough time and received a great harvest, God can bless you even when things look difficult (Genesis 26:12).

So, for you to enjoy God’s blessing every day, you must study God’s Word and meditate on it every day. The Bible says that as you do so, you become like a tree planted by the water; always growing and always bearing fruit (Psalm 1:3). This is God’s plan for your life.

## Confession

I’m blessed and empowered by the Lord; therefore, nothing can stop my progress. I’m growing, increasing, and doing well by the power of the Holy 2222 Spirit. Amen!
