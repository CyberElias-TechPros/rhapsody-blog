God wants you to know His Word because His truth brings freedom.

When people don’t know what God has said about them, they may feel afraid or think something is wrong with their lives. But when you learn God’s Word, you discover who you truly are. The Bible says you’re blessed, loved, and protected by God (Psalm 5:12).

Knowing this truth fills your heart with faith and courage. God’s Word shows you that you can live above fear and every power of darkness. As you study and believe His Word, you’ll live in the freedom and victory God has given you.

## Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank you for your Word that teaches me the truth. As I learn and believe in it, I live in freedom, courage, and victory every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
