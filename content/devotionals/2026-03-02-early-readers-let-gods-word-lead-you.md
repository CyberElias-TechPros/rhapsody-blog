God’s Word doesn’t just give information— it changes us inside. As we read and meditate on God’s Word, our minds are renewed, and our hearts learn God’s way of doing things.

When you keep God’s Word in your mouth and mind, your life moves forward. You become strong, wise, and full of “good” success.

So, make it a habit: read, meditate on, and do God’s Word every day. Let it shape your choices, your words, and your desires.

## Confession

I yield to God’s Word. It trains my thinking and guides my steps, so I live a victorious life every day.
