You know how in video games you usually have an avatar? That character running around, jumping, and exploring is just the skin you control. The real you is the player holding the controller. In the same way, your body is like your earth-suit, your avatar. But the real you? That’s the spirit man living inside.

The one Peter calls the “hidden person of the heart” is the same one the Apostle Paul calls the “inward man” (2 Corinthians 4:16). The inward man is the human spirit, the one who can’t be seen with the optical eyes. The “outward man” is the body with its five senses. The real “you” is the inward man, not the outward man. When you speak, it’s the inward man that talks through your mouth.

You’re a spirit being, not a physical body. The body that you see is your house, not the real you (2 Corinthians 5:1). A lot of people get this backwards. They spend all their time and energy worrying about what their “avatar” looks like—their clothes, their skin, their hair—and they forget to upgrade the person inside. It’s important to recognise that you’re a spirit being, because the man inside is the one that God deals with and relates to, not the physical body. This is the reason the Bible says, “The spirit of a man is the lamp of the Lord, searching all the inner depths of his heart” (Proverbs 20:27).

The Lord relates with your spirit in leading and guiding you, not your mind or your body. The character you exhibit outside is a direct reflection of the state of your spirit, so God contacts you through your spirit. As you walk in this consciousness, you’ll live beyond physical circumstances; your body will comply with the inward man, and your life will be supernatural every day.

## Prayer

Dear Father, I pray that you would grant me, according to the riches of your glory, to be strengthened with might by your Spirit in my inner man, and that Christ may continually dwell in my spirit by faith, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Today, take a minute to talk to your spirit—the real you. Say out loud, “You’re the real me. You’re strong, you’re filled with God’s light, and you’re in charge.” Then, feed your spirit with the Word and pray in the Spirit.
