Sometimes, when you tell others about Jesus, not everyone will want to listen. Some may turn away or even laugh—but don’t be discouraged. Keep your focus on those who are ready to hear your message!

Jesus first shared God’s Word with the Jews, but many didn’t believe Him. So, He took the message to others who were happy to receive it. The Apostle Paul did the same: when some people rejected the Gospel, he preached to those who believed.

Be like Jesus and Paul. Pray for those who refuse to listen, but don’t stop sharing. Keep shining your light, and one day, they’ll want to know Jesus.

## Confession

I joyfully share God’s Word! I focus on those who listen, and my light shines brightly for Jesus every day. Hallelujah!
