Across the world, disagreements between people, groups and nations often grow into hostility. History shows how quickly differences can turn individuals into enemies. Many societies even justify loving those who support you and rejecting those who oppose you. Yet the Lord Jesus profoundly challenges this thinking.

The society tells you to love your friends and hate your enemies. On the contrary, the Lord Jesus is teaching you in the portion of the scripture you’ve just read to love both your friends and enemies. It’s important to note that He was addressing men who weren’t born again; people who still thought of their enemies as flesh and blood. Sadly, some young people still think this way, and sometimes pray against their classmates or peers, saying, “Oh Lord! Fight against my enemies, destroy them....”

You can’t be God-conscious until you’re conscious of God’s love. This is why you must always take out time to meditate on the love of God. Think about His grace, kindness and how He chose you to be His child, despite your imperfections.

Always remember that if you were the only one on earth, the Lord Jesus would still have given His life for you. Just as the man in Matthew 13:44 sold everything he had to buy a field because of the treasure in it, you’re the treasure Jesus saw that He willingly gave His life for. When you let the love of God dominate you as you meditate on His Word, you’ll cease to be enemy-conscious.

## Speak

Dear heavenly Father, I have set my eyes on you and your Word alone. As I meditate on your goodness and your love, my heart is completely overwhelmed by your love for me. By your Spirit, I demonstrate this love to others every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Today, choose one person who’s a bit “difficult” to connect with; pray for them, bless them in your heart, and do one kind act toward them.
