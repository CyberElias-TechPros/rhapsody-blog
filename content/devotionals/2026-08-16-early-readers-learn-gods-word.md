God wants His children to know His Word. The Bible says people can get into trouble when they don’t know what God has said (Hosea 4:6).

When you learn God’s Word, it helps you understand how to live and make the right choices. It also helps your faith grow strong. Just like a lamp lights the way in the dark, God’s Word shows you the right path to follow. The more you study the Bible and listen when it is taught, the wiser you become.

Always love and learn God’s Word, because it guides you and helps you live a joyful and victorious life.

## Prayer

Precious loving Father, thank you for your Word that teaches and guides me every day. Help me love your Word and learn it so I can always walk in your truth, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
