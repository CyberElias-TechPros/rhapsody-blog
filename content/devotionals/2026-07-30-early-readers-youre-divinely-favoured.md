As a child of God, youʼre never without help. Even if something doesnʼt go as planned, the Bible says God works all things together for good for those who love Him (Romans 8:28). Where you are right now is not a mistake—God can use it for your success.

Joseph was once a slave, yet the Bible says the Lord was with him, and he prospered (Genesis 39:2). Even in difficult times, Godʼs blessing still worked in his life.

Therefore, what you need is already in you. Trust the Holy Spirit, follow His guidance, and your life will always be beautiful and fruitful.

## Confession

God’s favour is upon my life, and His grace opens doors for me. Therefore, I’m surrounded with blessings, and I walk in victory every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
