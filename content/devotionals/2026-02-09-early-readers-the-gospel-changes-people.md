The Gospel of Jesus isn’t just good news— it’s God’s power that changes people! When anyone believes in Jesus, their life is transformed.

A person who used to do wrong things becomes new in Christ. The Bible says, “Let him who stole steal no longer, but rather let him labor, working with his hands what is good, that he may have something to give him who has need” (Ephesians 4:28). That’s what the Gospel does—it teaches us to live right and please God.

When you were born again, you were washed, made holy, and made right with God (1 Corinthians 6:11). Now, you’re God’s child, full of His light and love!

Keep studying God’s Word every day—it’ll keep changing you from the inside out.

## Confession

The Gospel has changed me! I live in God’s light and show His goodness everywhere I go. Hallelujah!
