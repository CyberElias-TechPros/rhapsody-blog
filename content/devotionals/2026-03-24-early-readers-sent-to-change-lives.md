You’re not ordinary—you’re sent by God to shine His light and change lives! Everywhere you go, you can make a difference by expressing love, helping others, and sharing the love of Jesus with them.

God has trusted you with His message. You’re His young ambassador—a messenger of hope and truth.

So, let your light shine brightly. Change your world by sharing the love of Jesus today!

## Confession

I’m a light in my world. I was sent by God to change lives with His love and power!
