In these last days, many of God’s children have become more acquainted with spiritual gifts than ever before. Yet, one area often misunderstood is the gift of knowledge mentioned in 1 Corinthians 12. Contrary to popular thought, this is not about telling secrets of people’s lives—that’s prophecy. The Bible says, “...the secrets of his heart are revealed...” (1 Corinthians 14:25 NKJV) through prophecy.

The gift of knowledge, however, is far more expansive. The word “knowledge” here comes from the Greek “gnosis”, meaning scientific or intellectual knowledge. This gift allows the Spirit of God to supernaturally impart understanding about divine things, the cosmos, and even practical fields such as medicine, law, engineering, or artificial intelligence. How powerful this is!

Think about it: the One who created the heavens and the earth, the plants, animals, and minerals, can teach you about them supernaturally. He can reveal solutions the world is desperately searching for through you. That’s why you must become more conscious of His ministry in your life.

Christ, the embodiment of wisdom and knowledge, lives in you. The Holy Spirit, the Spirit of knowledge Himself, dwells in you. He’s able to transmit divine insight to your spirit, enabling you to walk in higher dimensions of creativity and excellence. Be mindful of His power and presence. As you yield yourself to Him, you’ll not only receive answers but also become the answer the world is waiting for.

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for the Holy Spirit, the Spirit of knowledge, who dwells in me and teaches me all things so that I can deal wisely in all affairs of life. He’s the One who gives me absolute knowledge of concepts, ideas, circumstances, and situations with which I birth innovations and solutions to change my world, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Is there something specific you’d like the Holy Spirit’s insight on? Ask Him today to grant you that insight.
