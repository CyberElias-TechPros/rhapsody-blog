God is happy when His children walk in the truth. To walk in God’s truth means to live by His Word every day.

Jesus said He is the truth (John 14:6 ), and God’s Word is also truth. When you read the Bible and do what it teaches, you’re walking in the truth. This helps you make the right choices and live a life that pleases God.

Walking in the truth also helps others see God’s goodness through your life. Always love God’s Word and follow it gladly. As you do, your life will shine with God’s light.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for your Word which is truth. Help me live by your Word every day so that my life will please you, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
