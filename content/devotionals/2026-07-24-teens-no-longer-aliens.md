Before we were born again, the Bible says we were”aliens from the commonwealth of Israel and strangers from the covenants of promise, having no hope and without God in the world” (Ephesians 2:12). That was a real state of hopelessness indeed. But then the Bible says, “But now in Christ Jesus you who once were far off have been brought near by the blood of Christ” (Ephesians 2:13). Before the redemptive sacrifice of Jesus on our behalf, we couldn’t approach God because we didn’t even know Him; however, by His death on the cross, Jesus paid the price for our redemption and brought us into peace and union with the Father. He is our Peace.

No wonder the Bible declares, “Therefore, having been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ” (Romans 5:1). What this implies is that through Christ Jesus, you’ve been set at one with God; you’ve come home in God! His death brokered a peace accord between you and God, bringing about a “ceasefire” as it were. Thus, there’s no need anymore for you to live in apprehension that one day God may recall your wrongs and punish you, no! Jesus has already taken your punishment! The Bible says, the punishment that brought us peace was laid on Jesus on the cross: ”But He was wounded for our transgressions, He was bruised for our iniquities; the chastisement for our peace was upon Him...” (Isaiah 53:5).

This is what makes us bold to face life as champions. We’ve been awakened to the Fatherhood of God. We have unhindered and uninterrupted access to the Father twenty-four hours a day! Refuse to allow the devil deceive you and make you believe that God has a grudge against you—especially after a bad day, a mistake with friends, or failing a test. Jesus already settled every issue on your behalf and established peace between you and the Father: “For He Himself is our peace, who has made both one, and has broken down the middle wall of separation” (Ephesians 2:14).

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank You for sending Jesus to die for my sins and raising Him from the dead for my right standing with You. I rejoice, having been justified by the blood of Jesus and brought into peaceful friendship with You, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Take five quiet minutes to rest in God’s love. Thank Him that you’re free from condemnation, and let His peace fill your heart.
