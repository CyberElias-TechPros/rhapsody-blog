When God created the world, everything He made was good, and He gave man dominion over his creation. But when sin entered through Adam, death came in, and everything became subject to decay. Today, the world—living and non-living—is crying out for freedom. People struggle with sickness, poverty, and failures; even resources are misused.

The sons of God are the world’s only hope. The Scripture says all creation is waiting for the manifestation of the “sons” of God—the mature followers of Christ who know how to stand strong and make a difference, even as teens! These mature sons know how to overcome sickness, lack, and obstacles, and bring hope to others.

Make up your mind not to remain in the babyhood stage of Christianity. Grow to maturity because the world is waiting for you! God wants to deliver great things to His mature sons and daughters, for He knows they can handle responsibilities.

Take your Christian growth very seriously. It’s only in the position of maturity in Christ that you can take your place as the “huios” (mature son) of God and bring deliverance, healing, salvation, and joy to a hurting world.

## Prayer

Dear Father, I thank you for building me up through your Word and bringing me to a place of maturity and responsibility in Christ. I’ve located myself as a saviour from Zion and a world- changer, and by the power of your Spirit, I walk in this reality today, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Spend time with God’s Word today and apply one instruction you learn without delay.
