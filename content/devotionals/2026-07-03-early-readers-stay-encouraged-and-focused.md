Sometimes when you tell others about Jesus or invite them to church, they may not listen. When that happens, don’t be discouraged. Even Jesus experienced this. The Bible says, “He came to His own, and His own did not receive Him” (John 1:11). Yet God’s plan continued, and many lives were changed.

Paul also faced rejection when he preached about Jesus. Some people refused to listen, but Paul remained joyful and kept sharing the Good News with those who were ready to hear (Acts 18:1-6).

God wants you to do the same. Pray for everyone, but keep your heart glad and full of love. As you continue to shine for Jesus, others will be drawn to Him. Stay joyful and keep going!

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for your Word that guides me in all things. As I share your Good News with my friends, at school, and in my community, many children will come to know you, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
