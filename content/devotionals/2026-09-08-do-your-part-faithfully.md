There’re moments in your walk with God when He gives you specific instructions because of something extraordinary He intends to accomplish in your life. At such moments, precision matters. Obedience isn’t merely about doing something generally right; it’s about doing exactly what He said, the way He said it. Many miss this and wonder why certain results don’t manifest as expected.

Consider Moses. The Bible tells us he was with the Lord on the mountain for forty days and forty nights (Exodus 34:28). Not thirty-eight or thirty-five days. There was divine significance attached to the full duration because of what God intended to accomplish through him. If Moses had interrupted the process prematurely, he would have disrupted his participation in what God had ordained.

This is one of the ways spiritual things work. God is perfect in all His ways, and His instructions are never arbitrary. There’s purpose, meaning, and spiritual accuracy behind everything He tells you to do. When He gives an instruction concerning prayer, meditation on the Word, giving, soul winning, or any spiritual exercise, it’s important to follow through completely and faithfully.

The Bible says in Isaiah 1:19, “If ye be willing and obedient, ye shall eat the good of the land.” Notice He didn’t say if you’re merely willing; obedience is the corresponding action to faith. It’s your part in the divine arrangement. God will always do His part flawlessly but you must ensure you don’t neglect yours.

In the realm of the spirit, things function with divine order and exactness. He said in 1 Corinthians 14:40, “Let all things be done decently and in order.” Nothing is accidental with God. Even the words He speaks carry significance and purpose. Therefore, cultivate the habit of listening carefully to the Holy Spirit, especially in matters that concern your destiny, growth, and ministry. Learn to value His instructions and act on them accurately.

If the Spirit says pray, pray. If He says wait, wait. If He says act now, then don’t delay. Your consistency and faithfulness in following His instructions position you for the greater manifestations of His glory and the fulfilment of His purpose in your life.

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for the privilege of being led by your Spirit, to walk in obedience, accuracy, and faithfulness in every instruction you give me. My heart is attentive to your voice, and I’m synchronized with your perfect will, producing excellent results and fulfilling your purpose for my life, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
