Did you know that you have an excellent mind? The Bible says we have the mind of Christ (1 Corinthians 2:16). This means you can think pure, true, and right thoughts.

An excellent mind doesn’t enjoy gossip or negative talk. Instead, it chooses words that are true and kind.

To keep your mind pure and strong, keep filling your heart with God’s Word. His Word helps you think the right way.

Also, an excellent mind gives its best in everything—at school, at home, and when helping others. Don’t say, “It doesn’t matter.” It does! When you think right and give your best, the Lord can trust you with greater things.

So, choose excellent thoughts every day.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for giving me an excellent mind. My thoughts are pure, true, and right as I meditate on your Word every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.7
