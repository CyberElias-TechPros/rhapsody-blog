When you become a child of God, His Word begins to live and work in you. This means that what you say and do should reflect God’s Word in your heart. Jesus showed us this when He lived on earth.

Everything He did revealed God’s love, wisdom, and power. In the same way, God’s Word guides your thoughts, words, and actions every day. When you speak kindly, forgive others, pray, and do what the Bible says, you’re showing others God’s Word.

Your life becomes like a letter that people can read, showing them who Jesus is. Let God’s Word live in you, and shine through you everywhere you go.

## Prayer

Precious loving Father, thank you for your Word living in my heart. I live according to your Word, and your love, wisdom, and power are seen in my life every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.7
