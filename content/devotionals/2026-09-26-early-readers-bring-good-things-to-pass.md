Your heart is like a treasure box. That’s why the Bible says to guard your heart with all diligence (Proverbs 4:23). Because whatever you keep inside your heart will eventually show in your life.

Jesus said that a good person brings out good things from a good heart. That means your thoughts and words are important. So, fill your heart with God’s Word. Just like Joshua was told to meditate on the Word day and night (Joshua 1:8), you should do the same.

See yourself strong, kind, and successful. During your time of prayer, the Holy Spirit will give you good ideas and pictures in your heart. Speak those good things boldly, because your words, spoken in faith, help bring God’s blessings into your life.

## Confession

I declare that there’re no impossibilities with me. As I pray in the Spirit, I see the picture of my beautiful life in Christ, and as I speak, it becomes a reality. Glory to God!35
