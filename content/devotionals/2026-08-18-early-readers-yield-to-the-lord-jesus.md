Jesus wants you to live under His lordship every day. That means you let Him lead you, and you do what He says in His Word.

When you truly yield to Jesus, His power works in your life more freely. Some people think prayer and fasting are just ways to activate more power, but they also help your heart become more yielded to the Lord.

The more you submit to Jesus, the stronger you become in faith. Then you can stand against the devil without fear. Always remember: Jesus is Lord, and as you follow Him gladly, you’ll walk in His victory and strength.

## Prayer

Dear glorious Father, thank you for teaching me to yield to the lordship of Jesus. I gladly follow your Word, and your power works in me freely every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
