The Gospel is the Good News about Jesus—it’s God’s number one priority! That means it should be yours too. When you tell others about Jesus, you’re doing the most important work in the world.

God loves it when you share His Word, and He makes sure you have everything you need to keep doing it—wisdom, boldness, and even resources. Angels work with you to help make it happen!

So, wherever you are—at school, online, or at home—make the Gospel your number one priority.

## Confession

I love telling others about Jesus. Preaching the Gospel is my number one job, and I do it with joy and boldness!
