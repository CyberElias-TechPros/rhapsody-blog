God wants you to see yourself the way He sees you.

Through Jesus, you’ve become the righteousness of God. This means you’re His beloved child, filled with His life and goodness. When you understand this, it changes the way you think and act. You’ll no longer feel small or afraid. Instead, you’ll live with confidence, knowing God is with you. Always remember that you belong to Him and carry His nature in you.

Think, speak, and live like the wonderful child of God that you are every day.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for making me your righteous child. I see myself the way you see me, and I live with confidence and joy every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.11
