As a child of God, you’re called to live a life full of blessings.

The Bible says you were called to inherit a blessing. This means God wants good things to work in your life. When you receive Jesus, you become a new creation, and you belong to God’s family. No curse, fear, or power of darkness can control your life. God has already blessed you in Christ. So instead of being afraid, remember who you are.

You’re God’s child, and His blessing is upon you. Walk with joy and confidence every day because you belong to the Lord.

## Confession

I’m blessed in Christ. God’s blessings work in my life, and I live in joy, victory, and peace every day. Hallelujah!
