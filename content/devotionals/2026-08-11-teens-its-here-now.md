In 2009, an unknown Scottish woman stepped onto a talent show stage and stunned the audience with an extraordinary voice. Within minutes, someone many had overlooked became a global sensation overnight. Moments like that remind us how quickly a person can move from obscurity to the spotlight when the right opportunity appears.

Promotion doesn’t come from the east or west, but from God alone. He’s the only one who can supernaturally promote you in a way that’ll baffle your detractors. The promotion that comes from God is the kind of promotion that defies human logic and leaves people wondering how it happened. The Bible tells us how Joseph was sold into Egypt as a slave by his brothers. To further compound his ordeal, while serving in Potiphar’s house, he was falsely accused and imprisoned. Things looked bleak until the Pharaoh had a dream which no one but Joseph could interpret.

After Joseph interpreted the dream and proffered his advice on how to contain the coming famine, the king said, “Inasmuch as God has shown you all this, there is no one as discerning and wise as you. You shall be over my house, and all my people shall be ruled according to your word; only in regard to the throne will I be greater than you” (Genesis 41:39-40). Glory to God! Joseph went from prisoner to Prime Minister of a superpower nation in one day! That’s supernatural promotion---the promotion that comes from God.

A few verses before the opening Scripture, the king had been angry with all his wise men and threatened to kill them all. Yet, a little while later, Daniel was promoted! He received both the knowledge and the interpretation of the king’s dream and saved his life and the lives of many others. God used a situation that should have led to his destruction as a means of promotion for him!

Get ready to receive inexplicable promotions in school, in leadership roles, and in responsibilities people may think you’re “too young” for. This is your season to get promoted to higher levels and positions at work that may not look like they belong to you yet! Challenges will come that only you can solve. All you need to do is be diligent like Joseph and Daniel were, and at the right time, your promotion will come. Praise God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, I thank you for your wisdom functioning in me. That wisdom is the power of promotion in my life this year, and by it, I’m rising to greater heights at work, at school, in ministry by the Spirit, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Expect promotion today; declare: “This is my set time for favour and elevation!” Then watch for doors, opportunities or recognition.
