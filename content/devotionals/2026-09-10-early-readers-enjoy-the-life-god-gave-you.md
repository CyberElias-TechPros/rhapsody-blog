As God’s child, He wants you to enjoy all the good things He has given you. This means you don’t have to wait for everything around you to be perfect before you’re happy.

You can be joyful every day because God loves you and takes care of you.

In John 10:10, Jesus said He came so we could have life in abundance. That means a full and joyful life. The Bible also says God has given us everything we need for life and godliness (2 Peter 1:3). Glory to God!

Spend time learning God’s Word. As you do, you’ll discover all that God has given you to enjoy.

## Confession

God has given me a joyful life. I’m happy, and full of joy every day because of His love. Hallelujah!
