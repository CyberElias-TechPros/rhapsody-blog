A long time ago, the children of Israel built special altars to worship God. Noah built an altar after the flood (Genesis 8:20). Jacob also built an altar at Bethel to worship God (Genesis 35:3). In those days, the altar was the special place where they met with God.

But today, things are different. The moment you received Jesus into your heart, you became God’s temple. The Bible says, “...you are the temple of God and that the Spirit of God dwells in you” (1 Corinthians 3:16). That means God lives in you!

You don’t need a special place to talk to God. You can pray in your room, sing in the car, or thank Him at school. Wherever you are, God is with you because your heart is His home!

## Confession

God lives in me, and I carry His presence everywhere I go. My school, my home, and my neighbourhood are filled with His presence and glory. Amen!
