The Holy Spirit is God’s power at work inside you. He helps you love God, do good things, and live a happy, and victorious life.

When Jesus was on earth, He depended on the Holy Spirit. In the same way, the Holy Spirit helps you when you sing in church, help others, pray, or do anything for God.

The Bible tells us, “...Not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit...” (Zechariah 4:6). This means we don’t do God’s work on our own. God’s Spirit helps us.

The Holy Spirit lives in you to guide you, make you strong, and help you win every day. So remember to say often, “Thank you, Holy Spirit, for helping me!”

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for giving me the Holy Spirit to live in my heart and help me every day. I depend on Him to do all you’ve planned for me, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
