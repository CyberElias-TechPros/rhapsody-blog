As God’s child, you’re called to shine His light, not to point fingers at others or judge them. Jesus is the true Judge, and He always shows love and mercy.

In the Bible, King David made a grave mistake. God sent the prophet Nathan to correct him, and David was sorry for what he did. God forgave him and did not reject him (2 Samuel 12:1–13). This shows us that God corrects with love, not shame.

In the same way, God wants you to help people, not embarrass them. When others make mistakes, pray for them, love them, and let your light gently lead them back to Jesus.

## Prayer

Thank you, loving Father, for filling my heart with your love through the Holy Spirit. I’m kind, patient, and caring, and I choose to see the best in others, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
