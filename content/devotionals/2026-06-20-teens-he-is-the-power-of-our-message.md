Did you know that the Holy Spirit is the power behind the Gospel? Without Him, our words would be empty and lifeless. But with Him, our message comes alive with divine energy and proof. He’s the one who confirms the reality of Christ in us and through us.

The Holy Spirit makes the difference between mere religion and a living relationship with God. When you received Him, you received the ability to function like the Lord Jesus. You became a carrier of God’s power, capable of producing supernatural results. This means you’re not weak, confused, or helpless, even when school, friends, or life feels challenging. No one can live for, or serve God without the Holy Spirit. He’s your Comforter, Counsellor, Helper, Intercessor, Advocate, Strengthener, and Standby.

He’s in you and with you forever (John 14:16, NKJV). He’s your divine partner. He’s closer to you than your best friend and cares about every detail of your life. The more you fellowship with Him, the more His wisdom and power manifest in your life. Trust and depend on Him daily to guide your thoughts, inspire your actions, prayers, and energise your words.

With the Holy Spirit, you can never be ordinary or disadvantaged. He’s the One who makes you a bold and effective preacher and minister of reconciliation; read our opening scripture again. He helps you stand strong in your faith at school, online, and among your friends. He empowers you to live victoriously and manifest the glory of God. He’s the Father of glory and the Father that lives in you. He’s the power of our message.

## Prayer

Blessed Holy Spirit, I thank you for your indwelling presence and power in my life. You’re my comforter, teacher, and guide. As I yield to your ministry in my life, help me make wise choices, speak with love, and live for Jesus every day. I live in victory, excellence, and dominion; with you, I can do all things; I win only and always, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Never rely only on your skill or knowledge. Acknowledge His presence before every ministry act; let His power flow through your life visibly.
