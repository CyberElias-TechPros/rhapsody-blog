Did you know that Jesus is our Rock? That’s what today’s memory scripture teaches us. He is our strong Rock and our safe place. Just like a solid rock keeps a house standing firm, Jesus keeps you strong, safe, and secure.

Jesus once talked about two builders—one built his house on a rock, and the other built on sand. When the storms came, the house built on the rock stood strong (Matthew 7:24–27). You build your life on the Rock by studying God’s Word, meditating on it, and doing what it says.

Storms can be difficulties, school challenges, or scary moments. But when your life is built on God’s Word, you don’t have to be afraid. Why? Because the Lord is always with you, and He will protect you.

## Confession

Christ is the rock on which I stand; therefore, I’m set for a glorious and beautiful life. I’m always secure and strengthened because God’s Word is my firm foundation, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.2929
