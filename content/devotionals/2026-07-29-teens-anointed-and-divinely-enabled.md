Tyler: “How did Jesus do all those miracles?” Mary: “It’s because the Holy Spirit came on Him and anointed Him. And guess what? The same Holy Spirit is in you now.”

The word “Christ” is from the Greek word”Christos,” which refers to the Messiah or the anointed One, and His anointing. So contrary to what some folks believe, “Christ” isn’t Jesus’ surname; rather, it refers to the anointing that makes Him who He is. That anointing empowered Him supernaturally for His earthly ministry.

Remember, though He was Himself God, He ministered as the Son of man while He was on earth. But the anointing came into His life when the Holy Spirit came upon Him at the River Jordan (John 1:32-33), and that was when He became “the Christ.” He didn’t perform a single miracle until the anointing of the Holy Spirit came upon Him and made Him “the Christ.” That anointing gave Him the ability to do the mighty works He did.

Acts 10:38 says, “…how God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power, who went about doing good and healing all who were oppressed by the devil, for God was with Him.” And just like Mary told Tyler, that same anointing came into you when you received salvation. The Holy Spirit lives in you. That means you’re not ordinary. You’ve been divinely enabled. So, when you face exams, peer pressure, insecurity, or tough situations, remember you carry supernatural ability on the inside. Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for granting me the revelation of Jesus as the Christ. I also thank you for helping me realise that the same anointing that made Jesus the Christ is at work in me; therefore, I have the inherent supernatural ability to effect changes and do great things, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Pray in the Spirit for five minutes where you are. Focus your heart, block distractions, and consciously yield to the Holy Spirit.
