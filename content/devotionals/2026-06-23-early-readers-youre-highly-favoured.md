You’re highly favoured by God! This means God has chosen you to shine with His love and wisdom in everything you do. That’s what today’s memory scripture tells us: “For You, O Lord, will bless the righteous; with favor You will surround him as with a shield.”

Remember Mary, the mother of Jesus? God’s angel called her “highly favoured” because God had chosen her for a very special assignment (Luke 1:28). In the same way, God has blessed you and chosen you to be a blessing.

So, every day, remember that you’re favoured. Believe it and say it often. Declare that you have favour in all things and everywhere you go. Hallelujah!

## Confession

I’m highly favoured and blessed by God! In all that I do, and in every place, I experience great opportunities to be a blessing to my world! Glory to God! 3030
