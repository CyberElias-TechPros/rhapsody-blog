The moment you were born again, it became too late for you to be poor, for you were born into prosperity. The Bible says, “For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though He was rich, yet for your sakes He became poor, that you through His poverty might become rich” (2 Corinthians 8:9). Now you know why it’s not possible for you to be poor anymore!

Moreover, the prosperity that comes from God isn’t only about money, riches and other material wealth. It’s about the totality of your well-being—spirit, soul and body. It’s about having the blessing of God on your life in such a way that you’re able to impart the same life to someone else. It’s the manifestation of God’s grace upon your life which causes everything that concerns you to succeed exceedingly.

You’re born to prosper. Begin to function in this truth. The Lord already made the sovereign declaration that whatsoever you do shall prosper; therefore, let that be your mindset. Refuse poverty and poverty mentality. Refuse thoughts that say “I can’t,” “I’ll never,” or “people like me don’t succeed.” Recognise that you have a limitless and inexhaustible inheritance in Christ. Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for bringing me into the life of abundant prosperity, where I lack nothing and require no aid or support! I honour you for your wonderful blessings in my life, which flow out and touch others around me, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Today, whenever a thought of lack or poverty tries to dominate your mind, reject it immediately and declare, “I was born into prosperity. It’s too late for me to be poor. I’m blessed in the city and blessed in the field. Everything I do prospers!”
