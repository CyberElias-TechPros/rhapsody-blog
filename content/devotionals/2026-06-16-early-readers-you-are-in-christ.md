Being God’s child is more than going to church or saying prayers. It means God lives in you, and His love is heard in your words and seen in your actions.

The Bible says Jesus was raised from the dead for your sake to make you righteous (Romans 4:25). That means God sees you as pure and holy as Jesus is (Colossians 1:22). You’re a new creation. (2 Corinthians 5:17) Glory to God! So, today, live with confidence. You’re not ordinary; you’re God’s child, special, and loved by Him. Be bold, knowing God’s life is in you, and you can display His love everywhere you go.

## Confession

I walk in God’s light every day. I’m the seed of Abraham; therefore, I live above sickness, lack, and defeat. I’m victorious always. Hallelujah! 2323
