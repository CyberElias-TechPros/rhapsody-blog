John described Jesus as”...the true Light which gives light to every man coming into the world” (John 1:9). There are other lights in the world, but Jesus is the true light! As long as you’ve received Him into your heart and follow Him, you’ll never again walk in darkness. Why is that? It’s because the true light now shines from within you.

Light is that which describes; in other words, it shows you what exists. If you entered a pitch dark room, with no flicker of light, even though that room may be beautifully adorned with state-of-the-art décor, furniture and electronic gadgets, it would be impossible for you to see and appreciate their beauty. That’s because there’s no light describing or showing you the things present in that room.

In the same way, when a man doesn’t have Jesus in his life, he can’t know what true success, fulfilment, peace and prosperity are. Such a fellow gropes in darkness and in utter hopelessness—journeying aimlessly, with a ticket to nowhere. Restless will he be until he finds his rest in Christ Jesus, Who is the True Light that lights every man that comes into this world. He’ll light your life with success, peace, health, joy and prosperity.

That means you don’t have to guess your way through school, friendships, or your future. The Light lives in you. You’re not confused, lost, or stuck. You have divine direction. Recognise Jesus today as the light of your life; recognise Him as the only true light that can guide you in the right path; hearken diligently to His Word, for His Word is light (Psalm 119:105).

## Prayer

Dear Jesus, you’re the true light that lights every man that comes into this world; therefore, I recognise you as the only true light that guides me in the path of success, victory and greatness! As I hearken diligently to your Word and practise what it says, my whole life is flooded with light; I’m directed in the path of progress and prosperity. Amen.
