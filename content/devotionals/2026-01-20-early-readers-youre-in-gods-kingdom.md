The moment you were born again, you became a member of God’s Kingdom. That means you’re no longer under the control of the devil. You live in God’s Kingdom—a place filled with life, joy, peace, and health.

The Bible says you’re seated with Christ, far above all powers of darkness (Ephesians 1:21). This means sickness, fear, or the devil have no right over you.

So, don’t ever accept anything that comes from the devil. Always remember that you belong to Jesus, and nothing can harm you. You’re a child of light, reigning with Christ every day—because you’re in God’s Kingdom.

## Prayer

Thank you, Lord, for bringing me into your Kingdom of light. I walk in your light and reign over every power of darkness, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
