God has already filled you with many good things so you can live a happy and successful life. His love, wisdom, strength, and peace are inside you because you belong to Jesus.

This means you don’t have to beg God for good things—He has already given them to you!

So what should you do? Believe it and say it. When you speak with faith and act in love, the good things God put inside you begin to show. Everywhere you go, God’s goodness can shine through you.

Remember this every day: God has put good things in you, and you can bring them out!

## Confession

In all that I do, I bring out good things—good ideas, wisdom, and love—and I show God’s goodness everywhere I go. Amen.
