The Name of Jesus is full of power— there’s no limit to what it can do! When you pray or speak in His Name, it’s as if Jesus Himself is speaking.

That’s why you should always be bold. You can command sickness to leave, speak peace into your home, and declare success in your studies—all in the Name of Jesus!

Never be afraid or shy about using that Name. Jesus gave it to you so you can live victoriously every day.

## Confession

In the Name of Jesus, I have authority over all things. I use His Name to bring healing, peace, and blessings everywhere. Hallelujah!
