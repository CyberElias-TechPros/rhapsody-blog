Some children get worried about schoolwork, their family, or what will happen tomorrow. But God doesn’t want His children to live in fear. In Philippians 4:6, He tells us not to worry about anything, but to pray and tell Him what we need, with a thankful heart.

Jesus also said, “...be of good cheer; I have overcome the world” (John 16:33). That means He has already given us victory. So, you don’t have to live in worry or fear.

Whenever worries try to set in, talk to God in prayer and thank Him for helping you. Then His peace will guard your heart and mind (Philippians 4:7).

## Prayer

Gracious loving Father, thank you for your Word in me. With it, I reject every thought of fear or pain. Because greater is He that’s in me, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.37
