Do you know that as God’s child, you can win and have a beautiful life every day? You don’t have to wait until you grow older or receive a gift to be happy.

So, first, celebrate the progress you make today. It’s good to dream big and work hard in school or at home. But while you’re learning and growing, be glad about the things you can do now.

When you’re thankful for your day, you’ll feel peaceful, and it’ll create room for more blessings. So, take time to thank God for His blessings today.

## Prayer

Dear gracious Father, I’m full of joy always, and I live victoriously today, knowing you have perfected all that concerns me, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
