Job was a man who had lived in great affluence because he was richly blessed of God. But imagine the terrible words he uttered when he got into trouble! This is just like some folks; when they face even a small problem—like failing a test or dealing with family drama— they forget so quickly all the good things God has done for them. Not only do they wallow in self-pity, but they also want everyone to feel sorry for them.

If you read the whole chapter, it’s full of lamentation and self-pity. This is the result of fear and a lack of understanding of the nature of God. Job wished to die. He had given up. But the Lord was merciful and restored him. We must learn not to give up in times of adversity.

The man of faith is the man with a righteous heart that’s fixed on God and trusting in Him. He doesn’t accept defeat and refuses to give in. He doesn’t give consideration to anything aside God’s Word. He lives a consistent and persistent life of confession of the Word. People without faith complain about everything and focus on the negatives. Job opened his mouth and cursed his day. Christians, full of faith, however, open their mouths to encourage themselves and others, speaking God’s Word even when things seem tough.

Faith that possesses doesn’t speak carelessly. Therefore, no matter what the case may be, let the life you live be the life of faith. When school, friendships, or personal challenges try to knock you down, refuse to cower or give up; keep speaking God’s Word over your life, because faith always wins. Glory to God!

## Speak

My heart is full of God’s Word and my mouth speaks the Word only. I speak life to my body, my finances, my family, my job, my business... I declare that I am making progress and everything about me is improving and increasing in Jesus’ Name!

## Act

Spend a few minutes today simply worshipping the Lord for who He is: the King of glory and the beauty of divinity.
