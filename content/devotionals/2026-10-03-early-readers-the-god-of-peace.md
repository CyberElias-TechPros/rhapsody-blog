Today’s memory verse calls the Lord “the God of peace.” When Jesus was born, the angels announced, “Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, goodwill toward men” (Luke 2:14). This shows that peace is God’s plan for the world.

Jesus said, “Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you...” (John 14:27). His peace isn’t like the world’s peace— it comes from Him and lives in you.

That’s why the Bible tells us to pray for everyone (1 Timothy 2:1–2). As you pray, you release God’s peace into your world.

So, speak peace over your family, school, city, and nation every day.
