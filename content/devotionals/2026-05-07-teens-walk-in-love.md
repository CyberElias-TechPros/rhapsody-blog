Afamous sculptor once said, “Every masterpiece begins as an unfinished stone.” Throughout this week, you’ve been chiselling away at the rough edges— adding virtue, knowledge, temperance, patience, godliness, and brotherly kindness. Now comes the final touch—the one that perfects them all: charity, which means love.

Love is the greatest virtue because it’s the very nature of God. The Bible says, “God is love” (1 John 4:8). This love— agape in Greek—isn’t based on feelings or emotions. It’s unconditional, sacrificial, and divine. It’s the love that keeps giving, even when it’s not reciprocated.

All the virtues you’ve learned so far find their strength and expression in love. Virtue without love becomes pride; knowledge without love becomes arrogance; patience without love becomes endurance without purpose. But when love rules your heart, everything else works perfectly.

Agape love is the reason Jesus gave His life for us. It’s the power that makes you forgive, even when you’ve been wronged. It’s what moves you to pray for others, to give, to serve, and to see people through the eyes of Christ. Love makes your life beautiful, radiant, and truly productive.

Ephesians 5:2 says, “Walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us, and hath given himself for us an offering and a sacrifice to God.” That means living a life of selfless giving— putting others before yourself, being patient with their faults, and seeking their good.

This is the climax of the 7 virtues—the life of love. When you walk in love, you walk in God. You become a blessing wherever you go. Love makes you unstoppable, fruitful, and full of joy. It’s the key to a life of endless productivity. Glory to God!

## Speak

The love of God is shed abroad in my heart by the Holy Spirit. I live in love, I think in love, and I act in love. Through love, I see the best in others, I give freely, and I walk in perfect peace. Love rules my heart and guides my decisions. My life is fruitful, excellent, and full of divine productivity. Glory to God!

## Act

Today, choose one way to show God’s love to someone around you—whether it’s forgiving a friend, helping a family member, or simply being kind to someone who needs it.
