God wants you to live an excellent life! That means your life should bring Him glory. Our Lord Jesus was like that. He always did things that pleased God (John 8:29). You can live like Him too!

An excellent life also means being fruitful. Whatever you do, be it at school, home, or with friends, do it in a way that brings excellent results. The Bible says a person, a child of God, is like a tree planted by rivers of water, always growing and producing fruit (Psalm 1:3).

So, choose to live an excellent life; a life that pleases God, bringing pleasure to the Father!

## Confession

Thank you, loving Father, for giving me an excellent life. Therefore, I’m bringing forth results that bring pleasure to you always. Amen! 2727
