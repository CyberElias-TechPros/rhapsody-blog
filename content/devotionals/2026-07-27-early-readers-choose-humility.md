When Jesus lived on earth, He showed us what true humility looks like. Even though He is the Son of God, He allowed John to baptize Him (Matthew 3:13–15). He didn’t act more important than others. He chose to obey God.

He also told His disciples that anyone who wants to be great must be ready to serve (Matthew 20:26). He even washed their feet to show them how (John 13:13–15).

The Bible says Jesus humbled Himself and did what God wanted. As a result, God lifted Him and gave Him the highest name (Philippians 2:9–11).

So, to be great, you have to choose humility. Choose to do things in God’s way. Help at home. Be kind at school. Stay teachable. Remember, God lifts up the humble.

## Prayer

Thank you, heavenly Father, for your Word that teaches me all things. I’m kind, humble, and full of love towards everyone I meet today, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
