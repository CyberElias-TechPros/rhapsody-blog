The Bible says, “There are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit” (1 Corinthians 12:4), meaning that there are several gifts that God imparts to His children through the agency of the Holy Spirit. But from our opening Scripture, we can decipher that the gift of prophecy ranks above all the other gifts of the Spirit that you should desire. God wants you to operate the gift of prophecy.

The gift of prophecy isn’t just for apostles, prophets, evangelists or pastors; it’s for the Church! Every Christian ought to prophesy. To prophesy is to speak forth the Word of God in power, whether by foretelling future events, or as a rhema-word to cause a change in the now! The Bible says, “But he who prophesies speaks edification and exhortation and comfort to men. He who speaks in a tongue edifies himself, but he who prophesies edifies the church” (1 Corinthians 14:3-4). When you declare the mind of God in prophecy, others are edified, strengthened and comforted.

Thus, with the gift of prophecy in operation in your life, you can never be in darkness as to what steps to take in life. Whether it’s choosing which subjects to focus on, navigating friendships, or making plans for your future, prophecy can guide you. Moses, in the Old Testament, understood the power and importance of prophecy and wished that all of God’s children were prophets (Numbers 11:29). But thanks be unto God; now that we’re born again and have received the Holy Spirit, we’ve received the divine enablement and inspiration to prophesy. Hallelujah!

Consequently, whether you’re praying on your own or in the company of other Christians, you can prophesy. As the Word of God wells up in you under that influence of the Holy Spirit, open your mouth and speak forth those words in prophecy. Begin to desire the gift of prophecy. The Bible says the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man (not some but all) to profit withal (1 Corinthians 12:7). This means you, too, can prophesy, and you can begin right now! Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Precious Holy Spirit, I thank you for the gift of prophecy. Thank you for filling my heart and mouth with words of power to cause changes in my life and in the lives of those around me. I declare that my life is for the glory of God; my future is that of success, progress and prosperity! I daily dwell in peace and health, and I experience the favour of God in every area of my life, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Look for an opportunity to declare God’s Word concerning any situation today. Speak it forth with boldness.
