A few years ago, there was a calculated move by global powers—backed by dark, demonic forces—to take total control over our cities, schools, and even our churches. That was Covid. It was a wild, global crisis where the whole world was basically on "pause." The streets were a ghost town: no cars, no people, and our normal freedom was totally stripped away. These evil forces didn't care about people; they were ready to sacrifice anything to push their own agenda.

But I knew a secret: the power of the Church—the Body of Christ—is on a whole different level than anything they can throw at us. All we had to do was pray and praise God, and that’s exactly what we did. We linked up in prayer and took a stand to take back our streets and communities from the forces trying to "gatekeep" our world. And glory to God, we got the win!

Why does this matter to you? When your church or your pastor calls for prayer and fasting, don’t scroll past it or ignore the invite. There is so much we can crush when we work together as the Body of Christ! In Acts 4:31, we see how the squad’s collective prayer gave them the fire to speak the Word: “And when they had prayed, the place where they were assembled together was shaken; and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and they spoke the word of God with boldness.”

Check out Acts 12:5-16 for another "epic save." The church was praying hard for Peter while he was locked up in prison. Their prayers moved heaven, and an angel showed up to stage a miraculous jailbreak! Hallelujah! You should be glad every time you get to pray with other Christians. That’s when we "flex" the power of the Church. We refuse to let the enemy take what’s ours. Instead, we flip the script in our nations and declare total restoration in the Name of Jesus

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for the power of communal prayer and for the authority you've given us through Christ Jesus to stand against every principality and power of darkness. We resist every demonic agenda over the nations through wars, kidnappings, killings and injustice; we speak life, restoration and victory over our cities and nations, declaring peace within our borders, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Respond promptly whenever your church calls for prayer and fasting.
