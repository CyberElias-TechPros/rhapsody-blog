The Bible says all of the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden in Christ, and we know that Christ in the context of the scripture above doesn’t refer to Jesus alone: “so we, being many, are one body in Christ, and individually members of one another” (Romans 12:5). In other words, Christ refers to the Church for which Jesus is the Head, and we’re His body. Thus, if all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hid in Christ, that means nothing ought to be beyond your scope of knowledge as a child of God.

Let me give you a practical example: You might have lost something, like your earphones or a textbook, and you’ve turned your room upside down looking for it; you’ve exhausted every logical thought. But do you know that your spirit doesn’t forget? If you activate your spirit by speaking in tongues, your spirit will begin to function like a GPS! Before long, your spirit sends that signal to your mind, and you know where that thing is. Someone may ask, “Does this really work?” Emphatically, yes! The reason some of God’s people still function below their call is ignorance; they haven’t tapped into the reservoir of knowledge God has made available to them. No child of God should be in the dark about anything. Should you need insight for a school project, wisdom for a relationship, or clarity for a decision, through the agency of the Holy Spirit, you can access knowledge of any topic in the world.

Begin to practise how to relate with God’s treasure-house of knowledge through the Spirit. The next time you’re tempted to say, “I don’t know thus and thus,” stop yourself and say, “In the Name of Jesus, I have access to the treasures of wisdom and knowledge!” Then, begin to speak in tongues and let the Holy Spirit bring that knowledge home to your spirit and then to your mind, for you have access to all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge. Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for opening unto me the boundless storehouse of knowledge and wisdom in Christ. Ignorance and darkness aren’t part of my life because I have access to all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge. Thank you for the anointing of the Spirit that’s at work in me, causing me to know all things, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Before studying or doing homework, pray in tongues for 60 seconds, then ask: “Precious Holy Spirit, thank you for showing me what I need to know.”
