The righteousness of God at work in you is such a powerful thing; it causes everything in your life to align with His divine purpose and provision. This includes your studies, your talents, your future, and even the dreams in your heart. It’s a righteous thing with God to do you good because that’s His nature. It’s consistent with His righteousness to channel every necessary resource toward you, ensuring that you lack nothing for all He’s called you to do.

Ephesians 1:3 says He’s blessed us with ALL spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ Jesus. There’s no blessing from God that’s missing in your life; you have them all! So even when you feel “not good enough” or “not ready,” God has already equipped you. Also, ponder again what we read in our opening scripture: His divine power has given you everything you require for life and godliness. The word godliness there refers to your life of godliness, the things God has called you to do, your life in God.

So, think for a moment; what do you require to fulfil the godly life God has called you to? It’s available. You already have it. Whether it’s wisdom for exams, courage to say no to bad influence, strength to stay focused, or confidence to be yourself, God has supplied it. Satan may suggest otherwise, but he’s a liar and not a factor. Instead, declare, “I walk by faith in God’s Word. I have what God says I have. The Lord is my Shepherd; I refuse to lack. All things are mine.”

2 Corinthians 9:8 AMPC says, “...God is able to make all grace (every favor and earthly blessing) come to you in abundance, so that you may always and under all circumstances and whatever the need be self-sufficient [possessing enough to require no aid or support and furnished in abundance for every good work and charitable donation].” And He’s done it! Blessed be God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, I thank you for granting me all things that pertain to life and godliness. Help me trust you in school, at home, and among my friends. I live in absolute abundance, walking in my inheritance in Christ. I don’t lack anything, for you’ve made all grace to abound toward me, and I am furnished liberally for every good work, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Approach studying or a test with confidence that He’s given you wisdom.
