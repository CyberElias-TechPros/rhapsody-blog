God wants His children to keep learning and growing.

Today’s memory verse tells us to add knowledge to our faith. This means we should be eager to learn and understand more each day. When you study your Bible, read good books, and listen carefully when God’s Word is taught, your mind becomes stronger and wiser.

Knowledge helps you make better choices and live successfully. God doesn’t want His children to remain ignorant. Instead, He wants you to grow in wisdom and understanding. So be curious, ask questions, and enjoy learning new things that help you become better every day.

## Prayer

Dear righteous Father, thank you for teaching me through your Word. Help me grow in wisdom and knowledge every day as I learn and study, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
