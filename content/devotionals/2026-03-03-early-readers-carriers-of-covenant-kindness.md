When God blessed Abraham, He wasn’t thinking of just one man—He was thinking of the whole world! God said, “In your seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed.” That seed is Jesus Christ, and because you belong to Jesus, you’re part of that same blessing.

God didn’t only bless Abraham to receive; He blessed him to be a blessing. That means you’re a carrier of God’s kindness—His love, mercy, and favour. The Bible calls this special kindness, which means God’s deep, unending love that always brings good.

So when you show kindness, forgive quickly, speak lovingly, and help others, you’re spreading God’s covenant kindness. Everywhere you go— school, home, or church—God’s blessings flow through you to lift others and show His love.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for blessing me and making me a blessing. Your love and kindness flow through me to help others and make my world a better 8place, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
