God’s beauty and glory are in you, and they shine wherever you go. The Bible says God shines from Zion, and today, Zion is where you live as God’s child.

Think about queen Esther, in the Bible. She wasn’t only beautiful on the outside; God’s favour and grace made her shine from the inside too. Everywhere she went, people noticed something special about her, and God used her to help and save her people (Esther 2-8).

That same beauty of God is on you. It helps you do well, stand out, and walk with confidence everyday. So don’t let problems or challenges make you doubt yourself. Remember who you are in Christ.

## Confession

My life is full of God’s grace and excellence. His glory and beauty are seen in me every day. I show His goodness, wisdom, and love in all I do. Hallelujah!
