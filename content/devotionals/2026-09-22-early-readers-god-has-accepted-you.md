Some children think they must work very hard to be good and kind so that God will love them. But the Bible says we’re saved by grace through faith, not by our own works (Ephesians 2:8–9). That means God’s love is a gift. You don’t earn a gift; you simply receive it.

The moment you received Jesus, you became God’s child. He accepted you just as you are. The Bible says we’re justified with God by faith in Jesus Christ (Galatians 2:16). So, you don’t have to think you’re not good enough, because God loves you.

That means you can talk to Him freely and happily. You can now boldly say, “God loves me, and I belong to Him.” Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for your love and grace at work in me. Thank you for choosing me to be holy and without blame. Today, I walk in wisdom, and produce fruits of righteousness, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.29
