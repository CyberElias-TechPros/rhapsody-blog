As God’s child, His life shines in you like a bright light. The Bible says you’re the light of the world.

This means your life can help others see God’s goodness. When you speak kindly, show love, and do what God’s Word says, you’re shining His light. Just like a lamp brightens a dark room, your life can brighten the world around you.

Everywhere you go—at home, at school, or with friends—you can let God’s light shine through you. Remember, God’s light is in you, and it blesses everyone around you.

## Prayer

Dear gracious Father, thank you for making me your light. My life shines with your love and goodness everywhere I go, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
