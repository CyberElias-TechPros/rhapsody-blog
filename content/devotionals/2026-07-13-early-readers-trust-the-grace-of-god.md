As God’s child, the Lord has given you something very special—His grace. Grace is God’s help working in you and for you, even when things seem difcult.

A good example is Esther. When her people were in danger, she trusted God and bravely went before the king to speak for them. Because of God’s help, the king listened to her, and her people were saved (Esther 4:14–16; Esther 8:5–8).

That same grace is working in you today. It helps you learn, make good choices, and succeed. So be condent! Trust God’s grace—it will strengthen you and help you shine in your studies and in your body.

## Confession

Thank you, loving Father, for your grace that’s at work in me. It causes me to walk in favour, blessing, success, and victory every day. Hallelujah!
