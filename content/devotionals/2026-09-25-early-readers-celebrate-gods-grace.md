Have you ever done something that felt difficult at first, but you were able to do it? That’s God’s grace at work in you. Grace is God’s special help. It helps you understand your schoolwork, make wise choices, and do things you couldn’t do on your own. Every good thing you achieve is possible because God is helping you.

In James 4:6, the Bible says God gives grace to the humble. Being humble means remembering that God is the one who gives you the ability. And when you succeed, don’t say, “I did it all by myself.” Instead, thank the Lord for helping you.

So, celebrate God’s grace at work in your life!

## Confession

Thank you, faithful Father, for your grace, which brings favour, blessing, and beauty into my life every day. Amen.
