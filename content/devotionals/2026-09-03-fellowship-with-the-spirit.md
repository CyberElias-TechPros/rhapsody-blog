In our previous study, we made a very important point, which is the fact that the most important thing for anyone to do in this world is to discover God’s purpose and fulfil it. But sometimes, there are those who are already in the line of their calling in life, yet they don’t even know God chose them for it. They may not have enough understanding concerning God’s purpose for their lives, but they simply continue on the course life has presented to them.

To a certain extent, that may help, because they’re at least moving in a direction connected to their destiny. But in the journey of life, there’ll always be adversaries and adversities. The challenge is this: when obstacles arise, if you’re not connected with the One who knows what must be done to move beyond that point, your journey could be truncated.

The unexpected events of life could derail or even destroy what seemed to be a promising path. Not because you weren’t gifted, capable, or called but because you never truly discovered from the One who planned your life what His full purpose was and how to fulfil it completely.

This is why fellowship with the Holy Spirit is indispensable. No matter how successful your journey may appear outwardly, there’s a higher calling, a greater purpose, and a divine pattern that only the Spirit of God can unveil to you. He created you. He knows why you’re here. He knows the full extent of your calling and how to fulfil it to His glory and for His pleasure.

The Bible says in Revelation 4:11 that all things were created for His pleasure. We don’t live for ourselves; we live for Him. This consciousness gives meaning to your life and clarity to your pursuits. It helps you realize how much you need the Holy Spirit. When you walk in fellowship with the Holy Spirit, you receive divine direction, wisdom, strength and grace to fulfil your calling joyfully and victoriously.

Therefore, cultivate fellowship with the Spirit through studying the Word, prayer, and yieldedness to His guidance. Always tell Him how much you love Him and rely on His person, power and glory within you to put you over in all things. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for the blessing and ministry of the Holy Spirit who guides me in the path of your perfect will. My life fulfils your pleasure and brings glory to your Name today as I rely on the Holy Spirit, His power and glory within me, to put me over in all things, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
