Some children think they have to wait until they’re older, wiser, or more experienced before they can preach the Gospel. But that’s not what the Bible teaches! Jesus didn’t say, “Wait until you’re ready.” He said, “…Go into all the world and preach the gospel to every creature” (Mark 16:15).

When you were born again, God gave you His Spirit— the Holy Spirit. He’s your Helper and Teacher, and He gives you all the strength and courage you need to tell others about Jesus. That means you don’t have to wait to become “ready.” The Holy Spirit makes you ready!

Every time you talk to someone about Jesus, pray for a friend, or share something you learned from the Bible, you’re growing stronger. That’s how you grow spiritually—not by waiting, but by doing. You grow as you go!

And the more you share the Gospel, the more the Holy Spirit fills you with boldness, wisdom, and grace to do even more. So, don’t be shy or afraid. Keep learning God’s Word, pray often, and stay filled with the Holy Spirit. You’ll keep growing as a strong, joyful soul winner for Jesus!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for giving me your Spirit to help me share the Gospel. As I tell others about Jesus, I grow stronger, wiser, and bolder every day. Thank you for making me a fruitful worker in your Kingdom, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
