Did you know that everything in the whole world belongs to God? The Bible says, “The earth is the Lord’s, and all its fullness...” (Psalm 24:1). That means all the gold, animals, and good things come from Him.

Knowing all things belong to God, He wants you to give cheerfully and share with others. The Bible says in 2 Corinthians 9:8 (NLT), “…God will generously provide all you need. Then you will always have everything you need and plenty left over to share with others...” When you give with joy, God makes sure you’re never empty.

So, don’t be afraid to give. Trust God’s Word! As you give happily, He’ll keep blessing you so you can keep blessing others.

## Confession

Thank you, heavenly Father, for making all grace and blessings come to me. I always have more than enough to give to you, and to help others. Amen.
