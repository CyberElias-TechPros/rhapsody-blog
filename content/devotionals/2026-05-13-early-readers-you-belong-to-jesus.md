Baptism is more than being dipped into water. It’s a special way of showing that you belong to Jesus.

When someone is baptized, it means they’re saying, “I believe in Jesus, and I choose to follow Him.” The Bible teaches us that when we are baptized, we are joined to Jesus and become one with Him.

In the Bible, the Apostle Paul met some people who wanted to better understand baptism. He explained it to them, and they were baptized in the Name of the Lord Jesus (Acts 19:5). This showed that their lives now belonged to Jesus.

When you were baptized, something great took place—you were joined to Jesus, and His Name was proclaimed upon you. You now live in His love and His power. Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for Jesus and for His wonderful Name. Thank you for reminding me that I belong to Him and live every day in His love and power. 20Amen.
