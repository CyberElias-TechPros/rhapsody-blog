The Bible compares your tongue to a small rudder that controls a big ship (James 3:4). This means your words guide the direction of your life. What you say everyday matters.

So, choose your words carefully. Don’t say, “I can’t” or “I’m weak.” Instead, say what God says about you. Speak strength, health, and success. Jesus said you’ll have what you say (Mark 11:23).

Fill your heart and mouth with words of faith and thanksgiving. When you speak the right words, your life moves in the right direction—full of peace, grace, and excellence.

## Confession

God’s Word is in my heart and in my mouth. I declare that I’m strong, wise, and full of life. Amen.
