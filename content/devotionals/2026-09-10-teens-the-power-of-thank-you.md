The words “Thank you” are two of the most powerful words you can ever say. They’re words that convey heartfelt appreciation and gratefulness, which, when rightly expressed, leave in the hearts of the recipient an indelible impression of your sincere gratitude. Sadly, some people find it difficult to say “Thank you” even when the occasion warrants it. As a Christian, it matters that you express gratitude and manifest a grateful spirit at all times.

Did you ever go out somewhere and an usher showed you to a seat in church or at the theatre, and you sat down without saying, “Thank you”? Did a cab driver drive you safely to your destination, and you alighted without saying “Thank you”? Have you ever been in school and a classmate shared their notes with you before a test, and you just collected them and walked off? All of these are simple, everyday situations we experience, and sometimes we don’t realise we ought to be thankful for these little things.

God expects you to have an attitude of gratitude; therefore, you should always be quick to say “Thank you.” When people do things for you, be it a favour or even something they’re obliged to do, always remember to thank them for it. Train yourself to lavishly express gratitude. Let that joyful atmosphere of thanksgiving always fill your home! There’s so much in life to be grateful for; so, practise being thankful.

Every day, before you think of your long list of prayer requests, thank God for all that He’s already done! When you learn to express gratitude to God, and profusely thank people for what they do for you, you’ll be amazed at the favour, joy and promotion you’ll experience in your life.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for teaching me the importance of expressing gratitude. I’ll never neglect to say, “Thank you” for the favours and kindness shown to me. I thank you for giving me a thankful heart, and for helping me express profuse and unfeigned gratitude to everyone whom you use to bless me and do things for me, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Identify someone who has been a blessing to you recently. Compose a thoughtful, one-line note of appreciation and send it to them today.
