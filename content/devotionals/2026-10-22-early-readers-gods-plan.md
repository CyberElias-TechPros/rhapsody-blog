God has a special plan for your life. As you study His Word, you learn what He wants you to do. But distractions like worries, games, or unimportant things can pull your attention away. That’s why you must stay focused on Him.

Jesus said His goal was to do God’s work and finish it (John 4:34). Paul also kept going, not minding what happened (Philippians 3:13–14). They didn’t give up.

So, be faithful in what God gives you to do. Do it with all your heart. Stay focused, finish well, and please God every day.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for your plan for my life. I stay focused on you, and do what you’ve told me to do, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
