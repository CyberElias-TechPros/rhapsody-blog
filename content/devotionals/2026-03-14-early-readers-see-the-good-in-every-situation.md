Faith helps you see things the way God sees them. Sometimes things may look difficult, but God’s Word enables you to see the good and the possibilities in every situation.

When David faced Goliath, others saw a giant too strong to fight, but David saw a chance to show God’s power! (1 Samuel 17:37-47). That’s how faith works—it helps you see victory where others see problems.

Always look at life through God’s Word, and see endless possibilities around you.

## Confession

I see possibilities everywhere because I look with the eyes of faith. Everything works together for my good. Hallelujah!
