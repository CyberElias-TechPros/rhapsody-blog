Alot of folks know that Jesus defeated Satan through His death on the cross, but not many know exactly how the Master did it. When Jesus died, He didn’t ascend to heaven that same day; He had been made sin for us (2 Corinthians 5:21) and so had to go to hell on behalf of all men. When He got to hell, there was an actual combat between Jesus and the forces of darkness. The Bible says, ”the hostile princes and rulers He shook off from Himself, and boldly displayed them as His conquests...” (Colossians 2:15, WNT).

In hell, all the demons grappled with Jesus; they tried to make Him bow to Satan, but Jesus declined and threw them off Him: He then defeated Satan and rendered him powerless. The Bible tells us that Jesus went over to the side of Hades where Abraham’s bosom was, opened the prison doors, and led out all the righteous men that were in there, including Abraham, David, Solomon and many others (Ephesians 4:8).

It doesn’t matter to us whatever hierarchy of demons exists; they’re all powerless. Jesus has defeated their overall boss, and that victory is yours! Remember, the Bible doesn’t say, ”Resist demons, and they’ll flee from you”; it says”Resist the devil, and he will flee from you” (James 4:7). The boss of all demons himself flees from you, terrified when you resist him in the Name of Jesus, much less his little demons.

So, when fear tries to creep in before an exam, when peer pressure hits, or when negative thoughts whisper that you’re not enough, remind yourself: the devil is already defeated. You’re not trying to win. You already won in Christ.

## Prayer

Dear Lord Jesus, I thank you for rendering the devil powerless and giving me the victory and authority to keep him that way. I see how helplessly and completely defeated he is. As I resist him steadfast in the faith, I know for sure that he flees away in terror. Thank you for giving me permanent victory over the devil and the cohorts of hell. Amen.

## Act

When fear or intimidation tries to creep in today, you should laugh and say: “Ha! You’re already defeated. I already won in Christ.”
