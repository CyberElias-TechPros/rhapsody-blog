Did you know your words are powerful? Every time you speak, your words create things! That’s why it’s important to say what you want to see, not just what you feel.

David used his words wisely. When he faced Goliath, he didn’t talk about how big the giant was—he declared how great God was! And guess what? He won! (1 Samuel 17:45-47).

So, speak life! Say, “I’m strong in the Lord! I’m blessed and full of joy!” Remember, your words are not just sounds—they’re seeds that build your tomorrow.

## Prayer

God’s Word is in my heart; therefore, every word I speak is life and brings victories, blessings, and promotion, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
