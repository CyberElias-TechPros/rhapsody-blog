The Lord has given us the beautiful opportunity to make choices. These choices may include which friend to keep, what to say in a tough situation, or even which outfit to pick. The good news is that in all these choices, the Holy Spirit always knows what’s best for you!

Remember young Samuel in the Bible? When God first spoke to him, he didn’t understand right away. But when Samuel learned to listen, he began to hear God clearly and followed His direction (1 Samuel 3:1–10).

So, whenever you feel unsure, don’t worry. Ask the Holy Spirit for help, and He’ll lead you step by step, because He knows the details and will always guide you right.

## Confession

Dear loving Father, thank you for your Spirit in me. I’m not helpless or confused, because He guides me in all things, in Jesus’ Name.
