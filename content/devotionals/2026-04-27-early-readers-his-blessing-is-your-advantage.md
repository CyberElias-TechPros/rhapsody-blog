As God’s child, what truly makes you stand out isn’t your looks, talents, or where you live—it’s the blessing of the Lord on your life. God’s blessing is His special power at work in you that causes you to succeed, shine, and do well in everything you set your hands to.

Isaac experienced this blessing. Even during a famine, when the ground was dry and nothing seemed to grow, Isaac planted his seeds—and the Bible says he reaped a hundred times more because the Lord blessed him (Genesis 26:12–14). That’s the power of God’s blessing!

That same blessing is at work in you today. It brings health, favour, wisdom, and success. It helps you excel in your studies, your friendships, and everything you do. You are blessed to prosper and blessed to win— always. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for blessing me with health, wisdom, favour, and peace. I walk in your blessing every day, and everything I do prospers, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
