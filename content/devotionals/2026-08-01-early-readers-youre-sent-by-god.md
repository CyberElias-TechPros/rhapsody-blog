Did you know that God has sent you to shine for Him? The Bible tells us about John, a man sent by God to tell people about Jesus. In the same way, God has also sent you.

You’re not on earth by accident. You’re God’s child, and your life has a special purpose. Jesus said, “As the Father has sent Me, I also send you” (John 20:21). This means you can help others know about Him. When you speak kindly, pray, and tell your friends about Jesus, you’re being a witness for Him.

Be bold and joyful. God has sent you to shine His light and help others follow Him.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for sending me to shine your light. I declare that I’m bold and effective in telling others about Jesus, and helping them know and follow 6you, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
