Throughout history, God has used people who were young or seemingly unlikely to accomplish extraordinary things. A teenage girl named Joan of Arc led armies. A teenage shepherd named David faced a giant soldier whom everyone else feared, and his courage changed the course of a nation. A boy provided five loaves and two fish that Jesus used to feed thousands. You don’t have to wait until you’re older, wealthier, or more established to matter. The size of your resource matters less than the size of your purpose.

True success isn’t just about getting good grades, building a career, or having nice things. True success comes when you understand God’s vision and choose to be part of it. And what’s His vision? He wants His divine presence taken to the nations of the earth, and He’s counting on us— His kids—to do it. You might be a student today, but you’re already a Kingdom ambassador. Your talents, creativity, and social influence can all point people to Jesus. Everything you do should connect to this bigger purpose: winning souls and expanding God’s Kingdom.

Start now. Whether it’s from your allowance, a small job, or a gift you receive, learn to give cheerfully and intentionally. When you give towards church projects, outreaches, or missions, you’re helping someone, somewhere, hear about Jesus. Avoid the mindset that says, “As long as I have enough for me, I’m good.” You’re called to think bigger. God can trust you with resources when your heart is set on His Kingdom. As you stay committed to supporting the Gospel, He’ll increase your capacity and position you for greater impact.

## Prayer

Dear Lord, I thank you for making all grace abound towards me so that I may always be self-sufficient, possessing enough to require no aid or support, and furnished in abundance for Kingdom expansion! Thank you for multiplying my resources for increased giving. I declare that I’m a financier of the Gospel, and my capacity to give is increased daily in the Name of Jesus. Amen.

## Act

Make a plan to give for the Gospel this week. As you give, say, “Lord, I give this for the expansion of your Kingdom. I’m reaching the lost for Jesus through this seed.”
