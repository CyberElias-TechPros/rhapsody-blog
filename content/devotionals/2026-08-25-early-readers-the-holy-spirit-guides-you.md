God has given us a wonderful Helper called the Holy Spirit. The Bible calls Him the Spirit of truth because He helps us understand God’s Word.

When you listen to Him, He guides you to do what is right and helps you avoid wrong choices. The Holy Spirit reminds you of what God has said in the Bible and helps you live by it. You’re never alone because the Holy Spirit is always with you.

As you listen to Him and follow His guidance, you’ll continue to walk in the truth every day.

## Confession

The Holy Spirit lives in me and guides me into truth. I listen to His voice, and walk in God’s Word every day. Hallelujah!
