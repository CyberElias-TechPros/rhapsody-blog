When you were younger in the faith, it might have seemed like God answered your prayers very quickly—even when you didn’t always pray the right way. That’s because, like a caring Father, God gives His children time to grow and learn.

But as you grow in the Lord, He expects more from you. He wants you to understand His Word, pray with knowledge, and live by faith. The Bible says that those who keep learning and practising God’s Word grow from “milk” to “strong meat.” They become mature Christians who know how to handle every situation with wisdom.

So, keep studying God’s Word and putting it to work. As you grow in your understanding, your life will keep shining brighter every day—from grace to grace, and from glory to glory!

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for helping me grow in your Word. I learn every day and become wiser, stronger, and more mature in my walk with you. My life shines brighter and brighter, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

Hebrews 5:13–14; Ephesians 4:15
