Worshipping God is very special. The Bible teaches us that true worship comes from our hearts.

Sometimes people clap when they feel excited or happy, which can be a way to show joy. But real worship is more than clapping. True worship is when you speak to God, sing to Him, pray, and lift your hands with love and respect.

Jesus said God wants us to worship Him in spirit and in truth (John 4:23–24). This means our hearts must be focused on Him. When you pray, sing, and thank God sincerely, you’re giving Him the worship He deserves.

## Prayer

Gracious heavenly Father, I worship you with all my heart. My words, songs, and prayers honour you today and always. Thank you for teaching me how to truly worship 8you, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
