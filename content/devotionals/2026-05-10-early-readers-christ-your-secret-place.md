Did you know there’s a special place where nothing can harm you? It’s called the secret place of the Most High! This means that no matter what is happening around you or in the world, you’re safe in God.

King David understood this when he said, “Lord, You have been our dwelling place” (Psalm 90:1). Today, that secret place is found in Christ. And now that you are born again, you live in Him— and He lives in you!

So even when things seem complex or uncertain, always remember this truth: Christ is your safe place, every day and at all times.

## Prayer

Thank you, dear loving Father, for your presence in and around me. I know that no harm or evil can come near me because I dwell in Christ, my secret place, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
