The Christian life is impossible without the Holy Spirit. No one can truly fulfil the will of God, walk in consistent victory, or live effectively for the Lord without the ministry of the Holy Spirit in their lives. This is why the gift of the Holy Spirit is one of the greatest blessings ever given to humanity.

The Holy Spirit is more than an influence or a spiritual experience. He’s God’s final solution. It doesn’t matter what challenges, limitations, or difficulties a man may face in life; God answered them all in His gift of the Holy Spirit. When the Holy Spirit came into your life, you ceased to be ordinary. You became an associate of the God-kind.

This is what the Apostle Paul understood when he said, “I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me” (Philippians 4:13). The Holy Spirit is your strength, your wisdom, your guide, and your helper. He causes you to function with divine capacity. Furthermore, He brings you into deep fellowship with the Father and guides you in the path of righteousness. He helps you know and walk in God’s perfect will.

With Him, you’ll never walk in confusion, uncertainty, or defeat. He’ll conduct you with divine wisdom and lead you in perpetual victory. This is why the Bible enjoins us to be perpetually filled with the Spirit: “Don’t get drunk with wine, because it makes you lose control. Instead, keep on being filled with the Spirit” (Ephesians 5:18 CJB).

Notice it’s a continuous instruction. He doesn’t want you filled occasionally or only during special meetings. The infilling of the Holy Spirit is for your everyday life. Every day, maintain the consciousness of His presence and yield yourself to be continually filled with Him. Speak in tongues often. Meditate on the Word. Yield to His guidance. Walk in love. The more filled you are with Him, the more you’ll manifest the glory, wisdom and excellence of the divine life.

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for the precious gift of the Holy Spirit, your final solution to the needs of all men. I’m filled with the Spirit and guided in wisdom, victory and excellence. I walk in the fullness of the blessings of the Gospel and in your perfect will, and I manifest the life and glory of Christ today, everywhere, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
