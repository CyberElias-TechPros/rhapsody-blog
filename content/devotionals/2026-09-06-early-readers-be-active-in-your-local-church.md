The moment you were born again, you became part of God’s big family. The Bible tells us not to stop meeting together (Hebrews 10:25). This means you’re not meant to stand alone— you’re meant to be planted and grow with other children of God.

God wants all His children to belong to a local church.

The church is a place where you learn God’s Word, are encouraged to do what is right, and feel loved and safe. In your local church, people also pray for you and support you.

The Bible says the Holy Spirit helps us pray according to God’s will (Romans 8:26–27). As you attend and take part, you grow stronger and wiser in the Lord.

## Prayer

Precious heavenly Father, thank you for making me a part of your big family. I’m active in my local church, and I serve you with joy, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
