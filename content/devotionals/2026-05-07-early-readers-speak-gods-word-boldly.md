As God’s child, the Lord expects you to speak His Word with boldness and confidence. When the Bible says, “...we may boldly say,” it means you can tell what God has already said—without fear!

Jesus showed us how to do this. When the devil tried to trick Him, Jesus didn’t argue or panic. Each time, He said, “It is written,” and spoke God’s Word (Matthew 4:1–11). And guess what? God’s Word always won!

So, whenever fear, sickness, or doubt tries to come to you, don’t stay quiet. Speak God’s Word boldly. The Word in your heart and on your lips brings victory every time!

## Confession

God’s Word is on my lips! I speak life, health, success, and victory every day. I’m a winner always. Hallelujah!
