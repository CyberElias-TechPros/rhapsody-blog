Eternal life isn’t just about living forever; it’s God’s life inside you right now, giving you confidence, strength, and purpose every day. It’s the God life in you, the organic and existential attributes of deity in you, which make you different from the natural man. This life makes you superior to Satan, sickness, disease, failure, and death.

The Lord Jesus didn’t come to give us religion; He came to give us the God-life (John 10:10). In John 10:27–28, He said, “My sheep hear My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me. And I give them eternal life, and they shall never perish….” It makes you indestructible and invincible. It means your life is not controlled by fear, peer pressure, failure, or negative opinions. It makes you a partaker of the divine nature. As one who’s born again and has received the God-life, you’re one hundred per cent divine in your spirit, even while living in a human body and going to school every day. You’re a hundred per cent divine despite having a human body.

Another beautiful truth is that you’re a distributor of that life—the God-life in you. God’s life in you is meant to affect your classmates, friends, and family. Anything and anyone you touch comes alive because you’re born in the image and similitude of the life giver, the Lord Jesus Christ, the second and last Adam (read 1 Corinthians 15:45–47 ESV). As He is, so are you in this world (1 John 4:17).

You’re a life-giver, a minister of life. Wherever you go, you bring healing, peace, and transformation. When you lay your hands on the sick, the life in you flows out of you to bring restoration. When you speak words of faith, life is transmitted into every situation. You’re not meant to blend in with negativity, gossip, or hopelessness. You’re of a higher class, a divine species, chosen and separated by God to manifest His life, glory, and righteousness in the earth. Hallelujah!

## Speak

Dear Father, I thank you for giving me eternal life and thus elevating me to your class of being. Help me to see myself the way you see me and live boldly every day. I function with divine wisdom, power, and excellence. I’m a dispenser of life and the fragrance of your glory everywhere; thank you, Lord, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Acknowledge the God-life at work in you now. Think, speak, and live from your divine identity.
