As God’s child, your life isn’t by chance. God has a special plan for you! He already designed your path before you were born. The Bible says you were created in Christ Jesus to do the good works that God planned ahead of time (Ephesians 2:10).

This means you’re not here by accident— God planned for your existence. So, you can be at peace, knowing that everything will always work out for your good (Romans 8:28).

Therefore, live with confidence and joy. Remember, you’re in God’s perfect plan, and His plan is to “…give you a future and a hope” (Jeremiah 29:11).

## Confession

I rejoice because my Father has the best plan for my life. Therefore, I’m at rest knowing He’s guiding me in the right path. Hallelujah!
