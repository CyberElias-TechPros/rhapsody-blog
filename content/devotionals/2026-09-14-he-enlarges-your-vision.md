When God chose Saul to become king over Israel, there had to be a transformation in his life before he could function in that divine calling. Though he looked physically impressive, head and shoulders above everyone else in Israel, he still had a small vision of himself and of life. He came from the tribe of Benjamin, the smallest tribe in Israel, and his thinking reflected those limitations.

But God had chosen him for something far greater than his background, upbringing, or personal estimation of himself. This was why the prophet Samuel said to him, “... the Spirit of the LORD will come upon thee, and thou...shalt be turned into another man.” That transformation was necessary because God needed to enlarge Saul’s vision, increase his capacity, and empower him to see, understand, and function according to divine purpose.

It’s part of what happens in your life when you receive the Holy Spirit. He changes the way you see. He enlarges your vision. He enables you to perceive what others can’t perceive and understand things from God’s perspective. This is important because many people may all look at the same thing and yet not see it the same way.

Men interpret life differently because of the level of vision, understanding, and insight that they have. But one of the beautiful blessings of the Holy Spirit in your life is that He opens your eyes to realities others may never recognize. He gives you wisdom, foresight, understanding, and divine interpretation.

Perhaps you’ve looked at yourself merely from the standpoint of your human abilities, family history, or present circumstances. It’s time to see yourself differently. You’ve been empowered and equipped by the Spirit to fulfil your divine purpose and accomplish the impossible, the unthinkable, and the unimaginable.

Even now, the Spirit of God is working in you. He’s increasing your capacity for greater impact, greater influence, and greater accomplishments. He’s bringing you into contact with people, opportunities, and resources that align with His purpose for your life. Many are going to wonder how you accomplished what you did in such a remarkable way. It’s the result of the wisdom and grace of the Holy Spirit at work in you. He’s found in you one through whom He can fulfil His purpose and establish His righteousness in the earth. Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for the ministry of the Holy Spirit in my life. My vision is enlarged, my understanding is increased, and I see from your perspective. I walk in divine wisdom, excellence, and supernatural ability today, fulfilling your purpose for my life gloriously, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
