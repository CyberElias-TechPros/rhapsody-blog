Many believe the harvest is something for the future, but the Lord Jesus boldly corrected that mindset. He said, “…lift up your eyes and look at the fields, for they are already white for harvest!” (John 4:35 NKJV). The fields are not just ripe—they are white, ready to receive the Gospel of salvation. Look around you. In your school hallways, your campus, your social media circles, there are people quietly searching—hungry for hope, truth, and freedom. They might not wear it on their faces, but deep inside, their hearts are crying out for the liberty that only Jesus gives.

Romans 8:19 NKJV reminds us, “For the earnest expectation of the creation eagerly waits for the revealing of the sons of God.” That revealing happens through you— through your life, your words, your love. You’re called to be the light that dispels darkness, to bring the captive into freedom (Acts 26:18).

Do not be deceived into thinking people aren’t interested. Many are waiting for someone to lovingly share Christ’s grace and compassion. That someone is you. The harvest is not distant or abstract—it is right where you are: at your school, family gathering, neighborhood, and even online. Open your eyes. The harvest is right where you are. So, be available, be sensitive. The harvest isn’t only ripe, it’s urgent, and the time to reap is now

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for opening my eyes to see the harvest around me. I respond with love, faith, boldness and compassion to this challenge of the harvest, taking my place as a faithful labourer in your field, bringing many to salvation, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Today, look around your school, your friend group, or even your online feed. Is there someone who seems lonely, confused, or curious about faith? Start a simple conversation and share the message of Jesus with them.
