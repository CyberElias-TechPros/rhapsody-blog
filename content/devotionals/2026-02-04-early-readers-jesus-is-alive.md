Did you know that when Jesus died on the Cross, you were there with Him? Everyone in the whole world was! He didn’t just die for us; He died as us. That means when Jesus died, our old sinful life ended. And when He rose again, we received a brand-new life from God.

So you see, the best part isn’t just that He died—it’s that He rose again! His resurrection gave us eternal life. Now, everyone who believes in Jesus receives this life of righteousness and victory.

The greatest news of all is that: Jesus is alive! And because He lives, we live too!

## Prayer

Dear gracious Father, thank you for the Gospel and the power of Jesus’ resurrection. I have eternal life and live in victory every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
