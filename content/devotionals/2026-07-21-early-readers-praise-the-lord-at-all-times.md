Did you know that singing to God can release His power? Singing praises to God is not just music; it’s a way to show Him you trust Him. And when you praise Him, He moves on your behalf.

The Bible shows us at different times how God’s power was released through praise. In 2 Chronicles 20, God’s people were surrounded by enemies. Instead of being afraid, they sang praises to God. As they sang, God fought for them, and they won. Paul and Silas were in prison, but they sang to God too. Suddenly, there was an earthquake, their bonds were loosed, and they were set free (Acts 16:25–26).

So, when something seems difcult, refuse to worry. Sing to God! Your praise is like a key that opens the door for God’s power and brings victory!

## Prayer

I give you thanks, loving Father, for your faithfulness, mercy, grace, and love in my life, family, school, and community. I will sing of your love every day and everywhere, in 28Jesus’ Name. Amen.
