God wants you to live every day strong,

joyful, healthy, and successful. The Bible says

the path of the just is like the shining sun, that

shines ever brighter (Proverbs 4:18). That

means your life is designed to keep getting

better—full of progress and victories!

But sometimes challenges may show up—

maybe with your schoolwork, your body, or

someone you care about. When that happens,

don’t feel sad or worried. Instead, be strong

in God’s grace. That means keeping your

focus on God’s Word and trusting what He

has said about you.

So no matter what you face—whether a

subject that seems hard or a challenge in

your body—stay strong in God’s grace. Keep

speaking faith-filled words. Hold on to God’s books!testimony. Hallelujah!

## Confession

Thank you, Lord, for Your grace in my life. Every day, I’m strengthened by the Holy Spirit with power and ability to win and do great things. Hallelujah!
