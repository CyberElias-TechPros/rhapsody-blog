As Lisa and Jordan chatted after school, they came across an astonishing headline on Jordan’s tablet.

“Did you see this?” Lisa asked, her eyes widening. “A fifteen-year-old just graduated top of their class in mathematics in the UK!”

Jordan shook his head in disbelief: “Fifteen? That’s... unbelievable! How’s that even possible?”

Lisa smiled: “You know, it made me think... if God can do that, what can’t He do through us?”

“What if the kid didn’t believe in God?” you may ask. Well, in our opening scripture above, Jesus addressed a group of people and told them nothing shall be impossible to them. This group of people weren’t Christians at the time; no one could have been born again since Jesus hadn’t died and risen from the dead. Now, if people who didn’t have eternal life were being told by Jesus that they could use their faith for anything, what about you who are born again, having the life and nature of God in you?

It therefore means there’s no limit to how much you can achieve in life! You’ve been called to a life of unending success, triumph and prosperity. You have the supernatural ability to do exploits because you’re filled with the Holy Spirit (Acts 1:8). Failure, defeat and the down-life shouldn’t be part of your life. All you need is to be conscious of God’s ability in you. Notice that the Lord Jesus didn’t say, “And nothing shall be impossible unto God;” rather, He said, “Nothing shall be impossible unto you.”

Think about it: if nothing is impossible to you, it means you can do anything and be everything God has planned for you to be. So, when obstacles appear, don’t shrink back in fear or despair. It doesn’t matter the strength of your adversaries; refuse to cower or be intimidated. Remember, the power of God in you is greater than any problem before you. Go on and win today. Glory to God!

## Speak

I’m a winner in everything I do. There’s nothing I can’t handle because God’s own strength is working inside me. I’m done with focusing on what’s stopping me or letting obstacles get in my head; my path is set for success, victory, and big things. I can literally do all things through Christ, because He’s the one giving me the power to crush my goals.

## Act

Identify one “mountain” in your life—something that seems impossible; don’t just pray about it; speak to it. Say, “Mountain, be removed! I’m not limited by you. Nothing is impossible to me!”
