When you let the Holy Spirit guide you, He

fills you with power and helps you do what God

wants you to do. He can inspire your words,

your prayers, and even your actions!

Sometimes, the Holy Spirit moves so strongly

that you feel full of joy, boldness, or a deep

desire to pray. That’s what happened to me

when I was a young boy. One day, during

family prayer time, I began to pray in tongues

for the first time—the Holy Spirit filled me, and

I prophesied!

The Holy Spirit can do the same in you. He’s

always with you—helping you pray, worship,

and preach the Gospel. The Bible says, “Then

the Spirit entered me when He spoke to me,

and set me on my feet” (Ezekiel 2:2). The more

you listen and obey Him, the more He works get children’sthrough you. So, stay open and ready for His other languages!leading every day, because He lives in you and

will never leave you!

## Prayer

Dear gracious Father, thank you for giving me the Holy Spirit to guide, teach, and move me to do your will. I’m always ready to listen, obey, and speak your Word with boldness 30and love, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
