Prayer is one of the most important things we do as God’s children. It’s how we talk to our heavenly Father, and it’s how we make things happen in our world. That’s why the Bible tells us to pray without ceasing—that means to pray often and never give up!

When you pray, your words don’t just stay here on earth—they go up to God like sweet-smelling perfume. In the Bible, King David said, “Let my prayer be set before You as incense…” (Psalm 141:2). God hears every prayer, and He loves it when you talk to Him.

Through prayer, you can change things! You can pray for your family, friends, school, church, and even people in other countries. The Bible says the effective, fervent prayer of a righteous man avails much (James 5:16).

So, don’t only pray when you feel like it. Make prayer your lifestyle! You can pray in the morning, and at night.
