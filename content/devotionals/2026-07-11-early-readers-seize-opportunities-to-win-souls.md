The Holy Spirit is your helper, and He

loves to guide you. He shows you the right

time and the right words to tell others about

Jesus. When He gives you an opportunity,

He wants you to notice it and act on it.

In the Bible, the Holy Spirit told Philip to

go near a man riding in a chariot. The

man was reading God’s Word but didn’t

understand it. Philip obeyed immediately.

He explained God’s Word, and the man

believed in Jesus and was baptized (Acts

8:29–38).

Sometimes, the Holy Spirit may prompt

you to talk to a friend about Jesus, invite

someone to church, or share a scripture.

When that happens, don’t be shy. Be bold, get children’sexcited, and ready to talk to them about other languages!Jesus.

## Confession

I thank you, precious Father, for the Holy Spirit in me. I follow His guidance and instruction as He leads me to help others know the Lord. Amen.
