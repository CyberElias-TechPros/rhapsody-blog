The kings of the earth employ painstaking efforts to raise and culture their children, because they’re royalty. Their kids don’t walk, talk, dress or behave like everyone else. The expectations are even greater when the child in question is the heir-apparent. That’s why Solomon said, “I was my father’s son, tender and the only one in the sight of my mother. He also taught me, and said to me: “Let your heart retain my words; keep my commands, and live’” (Proverbs 4:3-4).

Solomon certainly wasn’t David’s only child, yet he said, “I was my father’s son” because he was the one groomed for the throne. There was a special training David needed to bequeath to him, which the other sons didn’t get. As God’s children, we’re His heirs: ”and if children, then heirs—heirs of God and joint heirs with Christ...” (Romans 8:17). You’re a joint-heir with Jesus Christ!

You’re not an ordinary person; you’re a prince of power with God. Your heavenly Father owns the whole world and has placed you in charge of His possessions. No wonder the Bible says all things are yours (1 Corinthians 3:21). What an honour to be an heir of God! You’ve been called to reign in life as a king. Make up your mind to walk, talk and live as a king that you are.

Refuse to use beggarly language; the children of kings don’t talk cheap; they exercise authority. In your prayers, don’t beg. Relate with God as a son. He loves you and knows you’re His heir, who has a right to His inexhaustible inheritance. Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, I thank you for making me your heir. Thank you for my royal lineage! You’ve made me a king and a priest; therefore, I refuse to live a beggarly and ordinary life, for I was born to reign and effect changes in the realm of the spirit through my words, and your Word in my mouth is producing abundant results, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Today, police your language. Reject every beggarly or ordinary statement.
