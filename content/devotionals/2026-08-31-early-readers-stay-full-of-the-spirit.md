God wants His children to stay full of the Holy Spirit. When you pray in tongues, sing spiritual songs, and give thanks to God, your spirit becomes stronger.

The Bible says praying in tongues builds you up (Jude 1:20). It helps you become bold and full of joy. Just like eating healthy food makes your body strong, spending time praying and worshipping God strengthens your spirit.

You can do this anywhere—at home, in church, or while praying quietly. As you keep doing these things, the Holy Spirit fills your heart with courage, faith, and joy every day.

## Confession

The Holy Spirit lives in me. I pray, sing, and give thanks, and my spirit is strong and full of joy. Hallelujah!
