When you show love, don’t wait for people to thank you or give you something in return. God Himself is your rewarder (Colossians 3:23–24). And the best part is this: God never forgets your act of love. He always blesses those who show kindness and care for others.

So, be that child who spreads love everywhere—help someone in need, share what you have, and always speak kind and gentle words. That’s how you show God’s light to the world and treat others well.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for filling my heart with your love. Through me, others see your goodness and feel your love, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
