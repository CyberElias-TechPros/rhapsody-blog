The moment you were born again, God became your Father, and you became His child. That makes you special, different, and full of His life.

Jesus knew He came from God, so He spoke and acted with confidence (John 6:38). In the same way, you should always remember where you came from—God! The Bible says you’re a child of the Most High (Psalm 82:6).

So speak and act like God’s child—full of faith, love, and confidence. You belong to Him, and His life in you makes you strong and victorious every day.

## Confession

I know who I am. I’m God’s child, born of Him. I walk in victory, success, and glory every day. Hallelujah!
