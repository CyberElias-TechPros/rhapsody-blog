Throughout history, there have been moments when God moved in extraordinary ways to accomplish His purpose. In such times, the crucial question isn't merely what God is doing, but whether those He has positioned for that moment recognise their role in it. When the Jewish people faced annihilation in Esther's day, God had already positioned Esther in the palace.

What appeared to be a personal elevation was, in reality, part of a larger divine purpose. Esther understood that her position, influence and opportunity carried a significance greater than herself. Don’t go through life unaware of the opportunities God has placed before you. Never see the events of your life as ordinary and your assignments as routine.

God often works through seemingly ordinary situations to accomplish extraordinary things. The difference lies in recognising your place in His unfolding purpose. This is why spiritual discernment is so important. You must learn to recognise the significance of the opportunities, responsibilities and assignments God entrusts to you.

The Lord didn’t place you where you are by accident. Recognise the value of your contribution to His Kingdom and respond with faith and diligence. Know your place in time and participate effectively in what God is doing through His Church in these last days.

## Confession

I walk in spiritual discernment and recognise the opportunities, responsibilities and assignments God has given me to fulfil my role faithfully and effectively in the ministry of the Gospel. The Gospel is advancing through me, and the Name of Jesus is known and magnified in all the earth. Amen.
