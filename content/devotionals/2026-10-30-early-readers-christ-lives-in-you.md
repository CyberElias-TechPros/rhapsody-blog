When you received Jesus into your heart, you received everything you need for life and godliness. Christ in you means God’s life, wisdom, strength, joy, and victory are now in you.

Jesus didn’t give you blessings from far away—He gave you Himself. Because He lives in you, you’re not empty or lacking anything good.

So, don’t speak as if you have nothing. Speak God’s Word with boldness. In Christ, you have peace, health, courage, and all you need to live a joyful and victorious life every day.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for giving me all I need in Christ. I live a glorious and beautiful life every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
