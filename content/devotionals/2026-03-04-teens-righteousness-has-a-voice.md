Notice how a child naturally imitates the language of their parents. They pick up the accent, the tone, and even the expressions without trying. Why? Because it’s their nature to echo what they hear at home. In the same way, when you received the life of God at the new birth, you received His righteousness—and with it, His vocabulary. Now, you don’t talk like the world. You speak the language of your Father: the language of victory, dominion, and faith!

The Bible says, “The righteousness which is of faith speaketh…” (Romans 10:6). Righteousness has a voice, and it declares what God has said. When you affirm, “I am the righteousness of God in Christ Jesus” (2 Corinthians 5:21), it’s not a mere positive confession. It’s truth released into your world! You’re saying what God has said. Hebrews 13:5- 6 NKJV declares, “…For He Himself has said… So we may boldly say….”

Your inheritance in Christ is activated by words. The Lord Jesus Himself said you shall have whatsoever you say (Mark 11:23). You can’t reign in life unless you talk as the royalty that you are. Your righteousness empowers your speech to produce divine results. Refuse to speak defeat, lack, or fear. Instead, let your words align with God’s Word: “I walk in divine health. I’m marvellously helped of the Lord. My life is upward and forward only!”

The righteousness of faith calls things that are not as though they were; it speaks reality, and the Word of God is the reality. When you speak the Word of God in faith, you align your life with heaven’s will. You silence doubt and confusion. You activate angels. You chart a course of divine success. So, speak boldly. Declare your health, peace, prosperity and victory. Express your righteousness through your words.

## Speak

I am the righteousness of God in Christ Jesus. I speak words of life, power and faith. My words are full of faith, hope and love, and they establish God’s will in my life, in the lives of others, and in my environment. I walk in divine favour and victory today.

## Act

Express your righteousness even now by making faith-filled declarations of the Word.
