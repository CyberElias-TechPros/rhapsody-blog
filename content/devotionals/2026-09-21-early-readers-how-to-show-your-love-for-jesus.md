When you love Jesus, you’ll want others to know Him too. One way to show your love for Jesus is by doing His Word. Jesus said, “...Go into all the world and preach the gospel to every creature” (Mark 16:15).

You can tell your friends at school, your neighbours, and even people in your community about Jesus. Tell them that God loves them and sent Jesus for them.

Loving Jesus isn’t just saying it with your mouth; it’s doing what He says. So, be bold and kind. Share the Good News with every child you meet. That’s how you show your love for Jesus!

## Confession

I love you, Lord, now and always. I share your love, mercy, faithfulness, and goodness with everyone I meet today. Amen.
