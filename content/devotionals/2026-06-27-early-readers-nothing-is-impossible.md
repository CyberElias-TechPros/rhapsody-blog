Have you ever felt like giving up on something? Maybe your classmates say, “This project is too hard. No one can do it!” But you’re different. You have God and His Word in you!

In the Bible, God called Moses to lead the Israelites out of Egypt, even though the task looked too big. He also promised Abraham that he would become the father of many nations, even when it seemed impossible. Yet both Moses and Abraham trusted God, and He did amazing things through them. This shows that when you trust God, nothing is impossible for Him to accomplish through you.

So never give up! Put your trust in God, speak His Word, and do what it says. With God on your side, you’ll win! Hallelujah!

## Confession

My thoughts are excellent, and I can do all that God says I can. Therefore, nothing is impossible for me. Glory to God! 3636
