Jesus said that you’re the light of the world. Light doesn’t argue with darkness—it simply shines. This means when you’re kind, loving, and truthful, you bring God’s light to others.

Your light shines through your words and actions. When you help someone, tell the truth, forgive, or talk about Jesus, you’re shining.

The Bible says a city set on a hill cannot be hidden (Matthew 5:14). So, always let your light shine.

The Bible also says, “Arise, shine; for thy light is come” (Isaiah 60:1). Let others see Christ in you by the way you live every day.

## Prayer

Dear precious Father, thank you for making me a shining light in my world. Through me, others see your love and come to know you, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.17
