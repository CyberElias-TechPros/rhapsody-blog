When you’re in school, and your teacher is teaching, you’re encouraged to take notes so you can remember what you learned. In the same way, God wants His Word written—not just in a notebook—but in your heart.

How does that happen? It happens when you meditate on God’s Word and say it out loud. Each time you say God’s Word, it is being written inside you.

That’s why it’s good to keep speaking God’s Word about your life. As you meditate on it, whisper it, and say it boldly, God’s Word stays in your heart and helps you live victoriously.

So make it a daily habit to enjoy God’s Word. When you do, it will guide you and help you every day!

## Confession

God’s Word is in my heart and on my lips constantly. As I meditate on it, it brings me victory, success, and joy. Hallelujah!
