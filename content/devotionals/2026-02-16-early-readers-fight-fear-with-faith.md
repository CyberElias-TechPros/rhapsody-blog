Sometimes, the devil tries to bring fearful thoughts or worries to your mind. But you don’t have to let them stay there! You have a powerful weapon—your faith.

Faith is your shield, and it stops every wrong thought or fear the devil sends. Whenever you feel afraid, speak God’s Word out loud and say, “I refuse to fear! I’m full of faith and victory!”

That’s how you fight fear—with faith-filled words. God’s Word in your mouth will always defeat the enemy. Hallelujah!

## Confession

I live by faith every day. I use my faith to win over fear, worry, and every lie of the devil. Glory to God!
