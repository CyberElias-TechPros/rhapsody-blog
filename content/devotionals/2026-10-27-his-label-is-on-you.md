When God's Word becomes a revelation to your spirit, it changes the way you see yourself. One of those profound revelations is what we just read in Ephesians 2:10: "For we are his workmanship...." It means you’re God’s handcraft, His excellent work of art. He certified you as the best and placed His label on you.

Think about a manufacturer who places its label on a product. That label is more than a name; it's a mark of ownership and identity. By putting its label on the product, the manufacturer is saying, "This is our workmanship." In the same way, God says, "You are my workmanship." He's bold to tell the whole world that you're His work.

You aren't a product of chance, nor are you the outcome of your past or your circumstances. You're God's workmanship, recreated in Christ Jesus. He fashioned you according to His own design and purpose. He created you to express His excellence, wisdom and glory.

If you want to get the best out of any piece of equipment, you consult the manufacturer's instruction manual. It's the same with your life. The One who made you also gave you His Word as your instruction manual, so you can discover who you are, what you were created for, and how to live in the fullness of His purpose.

As you live by His Word, the excellence of His workmanship is expressed through your life. Never think of yourself as ordinary or insignificant; everywhere you go, remember whose workmanship you are. Let your thoughts, words and actions reflect the excellence of the One who made you.

## Confession

I am God’s workmanship, His excellent handiwork, recreated in Christ Jesus unto good works. Divine excellence, wisdom and glory are expressed through me today. My life is the manifestation of Christ’s beauty, glory and perfection. I’m the best, and everything I do is perfect. Glory to God!
