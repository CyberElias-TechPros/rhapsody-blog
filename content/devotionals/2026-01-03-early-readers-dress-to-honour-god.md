Have you ever dressed up for a special event, maybe your birthday, a wedding ceremony, or church? You probably picked your best clothes because you wanted to look your best. The way we dress says a lot about who we are. That’s why God wants us to dress in a way that honours Him.

In the Bible, God told Moses to make special clothes for Aaron, the high priest. These clothes weren’t ordinary; they were made to show God’s glory and beauty. The people who made them were filled with God’s wisdom because those garments were for His service alone (Exodus 28:3).

This teaches us something important: God cares about how we present ourselves. As His children, we should always look neat, clean, and tidy. Our clothes don’t have to be expensive; what matters most is that our appearance reflects God’s excellence and shows that we belong to Him.

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for making me your child, full of beauty and excellence. I choose to live wisely; and when I’m dressed, I bring you honour, showing that I belong to you, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
