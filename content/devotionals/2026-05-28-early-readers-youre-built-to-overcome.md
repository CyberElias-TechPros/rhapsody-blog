God has made you strong on the inside! You’re not weak or helpless—He has given you everything you need to win in every situation. So, when things get tough, remember that God’s power is working in you every single moment.

Think about David. Everyone was afraid of Goliath, but not David. He faced the giant with faith. And because God was with him, he defeated the giant (1 Samuel 17:40–50). That same courage is in you today!

So, when you face challenges at school, at home, or anywhere else, don’t give up or feel small. Stay calm and say, “God is with me; therefore, I win always.” That’s how to live victoriously!

## Confession

I live in victory and success always. I’m born to win, because greater is He that is in me than he that is in the world. Glory to God!
