When Adam disobeyed God, sin entered the world, and people became separated from God. But Jesus came and obeyed God completely—He died and rose again so we could be made right with God.

That’s the beauty of the Gospel! Jesus took our place, and now we live in Him; free from sin and full of His righteousness.

When you receive Jesus, you become brand new inside—clean, holy, and full of God’s love. Isn’t that beautiful?

## Confession

Thank you, Lord Jesus, for making me righteous and holy. The Gospel is beautiful, and I’ll share it with everyone!
