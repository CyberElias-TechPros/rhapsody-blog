The love and graciousness of the Lord toward all men are beyond description. From the very beginning, His desire has always been to bring man into fellowship with Himself and make him a partaker of His divine life and glory. This is why He offered humanity three extraordinary gifts, gifts so sublime that no human effort or achievement could ever earn them.

The first of these gifts is eternal life. We read in our opening scripture: the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord. This gift wasn’t offered to a select few, neither is it reserved only for Christians. No. It was offered to the whole world. Anyone who believes in Jesus Christ receives eternal life—is born again. This life is superior to Satan, death, and the grave.

The second is the gift of righteousness. Romans 5:17 says, “...they which receive abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness shall reign in life by one, Jesus Christ.” No man can stand before God on the basis of his own righteousness because human righteousness is inadequate before Him. Therefore, God lovingly offered His own righteousness to humanity through Jesus Christ.

When you receive His righteousness, you stand before Him without guilt, condemnation, or inferiority. You stand accepted in Christ and as righteous as Jesus is righteous.

The third is the precious gift of the Holy Spirit. Through the prophet Joel, God declared, “…I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh…” (Joel 2:28). What an amazing blessing! Also, Acts 2:38 tells us, “Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.”

The painful reality, however, is that not everyone has accepted these precious gifts. Many still attempt to earn what God freely offers by grace. Salvation in Christ, in which comes eternal life, righteousness, and the Holy Spirit is a gift to be received, not what you work or struggle to attain. These are the greatest gifts ever offered to man. Receive them wholeheartedly and walk in the fullness of their blessings today.

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for your immeasurable love and for the precious gifts of eternal life, righteousness, and the Holy Spirit. I walk in the reality and fullness of these blessings today, reigning victoriously in life and manifesting your glory everywhere, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
