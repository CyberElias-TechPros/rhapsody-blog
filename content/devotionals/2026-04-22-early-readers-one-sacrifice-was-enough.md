Before Jesus came, God’s people had to offer animals every year to cover their sins. But those sacrifices couldn’t make anyone truly clean on the inside. They were only temporary. Something perfect—something that would work forever—was needed.

That’s why Jesus came. He didn’t bring another sacrifice… He was the sacrifice!

The Bible calls Him “...the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world” (John 1:29). Jesus gave His life as the perfect sacrifice once and for all, so your sins could be taken away completely and forever.

Because of Jesus, you never have to feel guilty or ashamed. You don’t need to “fix” yourself or try to earn forgiveness. Jesus already paid the full price—perfectly and forever. Hallelujah!

## Confession

Dear heavenly Father, I thank you for the sacrifice of Jesus Christ on my behalf. Therefore, I’m perfect, righteous, and justified! Hallelujah!
