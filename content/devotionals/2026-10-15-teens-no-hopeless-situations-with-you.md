As Jane dropped her backpack on the couch, she sighed deeply: “I don’t think this can ever change,” she muttered. “I’ve tried everything.”

Her older brother, Derek, looked up from his Bible: “Everything?” he asked with a smile. “Did you try God’s way?”

Jane frowned: “What do you mean? This is a hopeless situation!”

Derek shook his head gently: “Hopeless to whom? From man’s perspective, maybe. But with God, there’s no such thing as a hopeless situation.”

As a young person who’s born again, you’re not meant to react to life the way everyone else does. You’ve been called to function by divine principles. That means you don’t panic when things go wrong; you respond with faith. The life in you is the life of a victor. You were born to reign, even in your classroom, on your campus, in your workplace, and among your peers. When someone says, “There’s no way you can pass that,” or “You’ll never make it,” don’t be discouraged. Get excited! That’s your opportunity to put your faith to work. Pray. Declare God’s Word over your situation.

Speak life instead of fear. Say, “The greater One lives in me. I’m an overcomer. I can do all things through Christ!” God loves to see you stand strong. The Bible says His wisdom is revealed through us. Every time you refuse to give up, every time you choose faith over fear, you’re showing the world—and even the spiritual realm—that the power of God is real and active in your life.

Remember, the inherent dynamic ability to turn hopeless situations around came into your life when you received the Holy Spirit. That confidence you need, that turnaround in your family or academics—it’s not out of reach. This is the set time to demonstrate the life of victory that God has brought you into. Praise God!

## Speak

I can never be disadvantaged in this life because the greater One lives in me, and He’s given me the inherent dynamic ability to change hopeless situations. I have the power and wisdom to turn every unfavourable situation around for my good, to the glory of the Father! Every challenging situation that I face is an opportunity for me to demonstrate my faith and manifest the glory of God. Hallelujah.

## Act

Look at the most challenging situation you’re facing right now. Instead of being discouraged, get excited! Say, “This is my opportunity to prove the Word of God. Greater is He that is in me! I speak change to this situation, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.”
