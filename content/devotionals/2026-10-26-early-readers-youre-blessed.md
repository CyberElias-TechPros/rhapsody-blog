As God’s child, what makes you different isn’t where you come from or what you have; it’s God’s blessing in your life. This blessing helps you succeed and do well.

The Bible says a blessed person is like a tree planted by rivers of water, always growing and bearing fruit (Psalm 1:3). That’s who you are! It means you’ll continue to make progress and produce good results.

So, say it: “I am blessed!” Believe it and walk in it every day. You’ll keep moving from one success to another. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Thank you, precious Father, for blessing me. I’m like a tree planted by the rivers, always growing, succeeding, and producing good results, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
