As God’s child, your faith should always be in God and His Word, not in objects.

Some people think they need special things, such as handkerchiefs, water, or other items, to receive help from God. But the Bible teaches that our relationship with God is not built on objects. The Bible did tell us that Paul’s handkerchiefs and aprons healed the sick (Acts 19:11–12), but he never taught us to depend on them.

God lives in you by His Spirit, and you can talk to Him anywhere and anytime. Your prayers, your faith, and your trust in His Word are what matter most. Always remember that God hears you and cares for you. Put your trust in Him and in what His Word says.

## Prayer

Dear faithful Father, thank you for teaching me to trust you with all my heart. My faith is in your Word, and I know you’re always with me, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
