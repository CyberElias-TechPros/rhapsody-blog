God wants you to do everything with the right heart. You don’t need to do things just to be seen or praised by others. Instead, do everything to honour the Lord.

In the Bible, the prophet Elijah challenged the prophets of Baal on Mount Carmel (1 Kings 18). They called on their false god, but nothing happened. When Elijah prayed, he said, “...let it be known this day that you are God in Israel and I am your servant…” (1 Kings 18:36). Then God answered him with fire! Elijah did it to honour God.

So, when you sing, help at home, or do well in school, do it for God’s glory. Stay humble, and let your actions show others how good God is.

## Prayer

Loving heavenly Father, thank you for helping me do everything for your glory. My thoughts, words, and actions honour you every day, in Jesus’ 24Name. Amen.
