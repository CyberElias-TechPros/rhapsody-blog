Jesus came to give you life, strength, and perfect health. Sickness isn’t from God; it’s from the devil. That’s why you should never accept sickness in your body.

One day, Jesus met a man who was hurting and troubled. With just His Word, Jesus set him free, and the man became calm, happy, and completely restored (Mark 5:1–20). That’s what Jesus does— He brings healing, peace, and a new life!

God wants you well today. He wants you strong, joyful, and full of His life. The Bible says, “…that you may prosper in all things and be in health…” (3 John 1:2). So, speak God’s Word over your body every day and trust His plan to keep you in divine health always.

## Confession

The Spirit that raised Jesus from the dead lives in me; therefore, sickness, weakness, and disease have no place in my body. I walk in perfect health always! Amen.
