Jesus gave us something very powerful— His Name! When you use the Name of Jesus, impossible and hopeless situations are changed. It’s not about how loud you shout or how strong you feel; what matters is believing in the power of His Name and using the authority it gives.

That’s exactly what Peter and John did when they met a man who couldn’t walk. Peter knew he had something that could change the man’s life. So, he boldly said, “…In the Name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk!” Immediately, the man jumped up, walking and leaping for joy! (Acts 3:6-8).

The Name of Jesus still works today! You can pray for the sick in His Name, and they’ll be healed. You can use His Name to bring peace, victory and change everywhere you go. Hallelujah!

## Confession

My life is for glory and excellence because I do all things in the Name of Jesus. Therefore, I live victoriously and dominate my world, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
