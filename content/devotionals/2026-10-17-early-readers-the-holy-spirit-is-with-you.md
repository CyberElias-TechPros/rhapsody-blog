The Holy Spirit is your special helper and friend. He lives in you and is always with you. This means you’re never alone!

Jesus went to heaven, but He sent the Holy Spirit to be with us every day. The Holy Spirit helps you know God, teaches you what is right, and gives you power to live the way God wants you to. The apostles did great things because they had the Holy Spirit. He guided them and made them bold.

So, always remember He’s with you: in school, at home, and everywhere. With Him, you can live in joy, do great things, and please God every day.

## Confession

The Holy Spirit is in me and with me; therefore, I’m never alone. He’s my helper, comforter, and everything I need to live an excellent life. Hallelujah!
