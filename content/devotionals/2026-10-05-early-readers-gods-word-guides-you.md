The Bible isn’t just an ordinary book or storybook. It is God’s Word given to guide you. The Bible says all Scripture is inspired by God. This means He guided those who wrote it so we can know His truth.

When you study and meditate on God’s Word, you learn what is right and how to live. It teaches you, corrects you, and helps you grow every day.

So always trust God’s Word and let it guide your life. It is a lamp to your feet and a light to your path (Psalm 119:105).

## Prayer

Dear faithful Father, thank you for your Word. It guides me, teaches me, and helps me live right every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
