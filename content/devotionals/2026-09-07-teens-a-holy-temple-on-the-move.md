Physicists have discovered a strange phenomenon called quantum entanglement, where two particles become so deeply connected that they behave as one system, even when separated by vast distances.

Our opening scripture above alludes to this concept, albeit at a much more advanced, spiritual level. It shows us that it isn’t just our spirits that are joined to the Body of Christ; even our very bodies are part of Him! Notice the Apostle Paul didn’t say, “Your body is a member of Christ,” which would have led us to believe he was speaking in a generic sense; he said, “…your bodies are members of Christ,” meaning your own body—yes, the one you use every day—is part of Him.

No wonder the Bible says “we are members of His body, of His flesh and of His bones” (Ephesians 5:30). This means the life of Christ is flowing through you in real ways, even when you’re doing homework, hanging out with friends, or playing sports. Christ literally lives in you! He dwells in your spirit and expresses Himself through your physical body. This is why sickness, disease, and weakness ought not to thrive in your physical body. When you’re conscious of this truth that Christ lives in you, it’ll be impossible for you to experience any infirmity!

The reason many have suffered so much trouble in their health is their lack of knowledge. According to the provisions of the Gospel, it ought not to be possible for any child of God to be sick or suffer affliction of any kind. Your body isn’t ordinary; it’s Christ’s expression point on earth; Christ rules and functions in your physical body. Meditate on this truth and let it gain the ascendancy in your spirit. Live with the daily consciousness that your body is a member of Christ. As you do, sin, sickness, and disease will be completely banished from your life.

## Prayer

My body is a member of Christ; therefore, the life and nature of Christ is manifested in me. Sickness, sin, and death can find no place or expression in my physical body, for my body is the temple of the Holy Spirit. Only the glory and beauty of God is manifested and expressed in my physical body.

## Act

Stand in front of a mirror. Point to your eyes, mouth, hands, and feet, saying each time: “These are members of Christ.”
