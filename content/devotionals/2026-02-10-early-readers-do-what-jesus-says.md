When Jesus told the servants at the wedding to fill the jars with water, they obeyed—and a miracle happened! The water turned into wine (John 2:7-10).

That’s the power of obedience. When you act on God’s Word quickly and gladly, you’ll always see great results in your life.

Don’t hesitate when God speaks to you through His Word or in your heart. Do what He says, and you’ll enjoy His blessings every time!

## Confession

I’m quick to act on God’s Word. I obey His voice and enjoy His blessings every day. Amen!
