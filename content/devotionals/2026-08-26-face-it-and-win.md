In Christianity, you’re not called to run from challenges but to face them and win. Many have assumed that once they become Christians, life should be completely free of challenges. So when they face difficulties, they begin to wonder, “Why do I still have problems? Why do I feel pain? Why doesn’t everything change immediately when I pray?” But that thinking misses an important truth about the Christian life. It’s a journey.

If there are no challenges to your faith, then your faith can’t work. Faith is proven, exercised, and perfected in the face of opposition. Life isn’t defined by how smooth things are; you’re promoted through the tests you overcome. It brings to mind the words of the Master in Revelation 2:26: “And he that overcometh, and keepeth my works unto the end, to him will I give power over the nations.”

You don’t advance without the tests of life. Even in ministry, great success doesn’t happen overnight. There are stages, experiences, and victories that position you for greater responsibility. Sometimes, God allows you to face certain situations, not for defeat, but for your victory. He knows you’ll win because He knows what He’s put inside you. When you win, you’re decorated for more. That’s the divine pattern.

So, don’t crumble under pressure; don’t give in to fear or frustration; stand firm. Declare like Paul, “But none of these things move me…” (Acts 20:24); You're more than a conqueror (Romans 8:37). Learn to look the adversary in the face and declare, “No matter what you do, I’m a victor in Christ Jesus. Greater is He that is in me than he that’s in the world.”

It makes no difference what you face; you have the comfort of God in your spirit and the consolation of the Holy Ghost. He reminds you continually to go on and win, knowing that every challenge is an opportunity for you to triumph. No wonder He says, “My brethren, count it all joy when ye fall into divers temptations” (James 1:2).

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for the victory you’ve given me in Christ Jesus. I stand strong in faith, declaring your Word with boldness. I refuse to be moved by circumstances, for greater is He that’s in me than he that’s in the world. I walk in strength, dominion and power today, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
