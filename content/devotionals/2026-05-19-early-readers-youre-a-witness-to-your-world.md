A witness is someone who tells others what they know to be true.

When you were born again, you became a witness for Jesus. This means you can tell people around you, your friends, classmates, and neighbors about Him. You can share His love and His truth everywhere you go.

Jesus said He is the Vine and we are the branches (John 15:5). This means that when we stay close to Him, good things show in our lives. One of those good things is telling others about Jesus, and sharing God’s message of hope and salvation.

So remember this: telling others about Jesus is very important. Be a witness for Him every day, wherever you are.

## Prayer

Thank you, precious Father, for making me a witness of your truth and grace. Every day, I boldly share Jesus with others everywhere I go, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
