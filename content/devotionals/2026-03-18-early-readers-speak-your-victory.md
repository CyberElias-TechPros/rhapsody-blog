Your words have power! When you speak, you can bring life, joy, and victory—or sadness and fear. That’s why it’s important to speak faith-filled words every day.

When David faced Goliath, he didn’t cry or hide—he spoke boldly, declaring that God would give him victory. And that’s exactly what happened! (1 Samuel 17:37).

You can win the same way—by speaking what God says about you. So, use your words to create your victory!

## Confession

I speak words of faith and life. I win every time because my victory is in my mouth!
