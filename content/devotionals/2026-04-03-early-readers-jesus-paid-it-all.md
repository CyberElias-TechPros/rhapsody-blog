Today is Good Friday—a very special day when we remember that Jesus paid the full price for our sins long before we were born. Because of what He did, you never have to pay for your sins again. Not now, not ever!

God sent Jesus, His perfect and spotless Son, to save the whole world. That’s why John the Baptist excitedly shouted, “Behold! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!” Jesus became the final and perfect sacrifice for everyone.

And now, just like John, you can joyfully share this Good News with others: Jesus has already paid the price! Through His blood, anyone who believes is forgiven, cleansed, and free to live as God’s beloved child. Glory to God!

## Confession

The Lord has sanctified, glorified, and justified me; therefore, I’m the righteousness of God! I have eternal life in me! Glory to God!
