The Lord Jesus was resolute about His purpose. He knew why He came and never allowed distractions. He declared, “For the Son of Man has come to seek and to save that which was lost” (Luke 19:10 NKJV). In John 4:34, He said, “…My food is to do the will of Him who sent Me, and to finish His work.” Every word, every action, was aligned with the Father’s mission.

Remarkably, after fulfilling His work, He turned to us and said, “…As the Father has sent Me, I also send you.” (John 20:21 NKJV). That means His mission has become ours. He commanded in Mark 16:15, “Go into all the world and preach the gospel to every creature.” This is the divine mandate.

Matthew 24:14 NKJV gives us God’s timeline: “And this gospel of the kingdom will be preached in all the world as a witness to all the nations, and then the end will come.” We’re living in prophetic days—the season of “extra time.” But extra time doesn’t mean rest; it means urgency. It’s not a break from the mission but a divine opportunity to intensify it.

The Lord Himself set the example. He said in John 8:29 NIV, “The one who sent me is with me; he has not left me alone, for I always do what pleases him.” Our lives must echo the same commitment. Success in God’s eyes is not measured by human applause but by faithfulness to His instruction. Your accountability will be on the basis of your participation in the Gospel.

So, resolve to stay focused. Let your speech, your actions, and your priorities all mirror the Master’s commitment. Preach the Gospel everywhere, every day, with the consciousness that necessity is laid upon you. Nothing in this world is more important than this.

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for setting me on a path of divine purpose. I walk in alignment with your will and in the consciousness that necessity is laid on me to preach the Gospel. My life is a reflection of true and unwavering commitment to the cause of the Gospel, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Today, share the Gospel with at least one person—whether in a conversation, through a message, or on your social media.
