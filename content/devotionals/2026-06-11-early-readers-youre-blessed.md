As God’s child, you don’t need to be afraid of anything. The Bible says if you’re in Christ, you’re a new creature (2 Corinthians 5:17). That means you were born into God’s blessing!

In the Bible, a man named Balaam tried to curse God’s people. But no matter how much he tried, he couldn’t speak curses; only blessings came from his mouth. He had to admit, “No curse can touch Jacob; no magic has any power against Israel.” (Numbers 23:23 NLT). Just like Israel, you’re special to God.

You’re blessed and protected because you’re in Christ. God’s good plan for your life can’t be stopped. Nothing meant to harm you can succeed. Glory to God!

## Confession

I am in Christ, and Christ is in me. Therefore, nothing can hurt me. I’m protected and secure, and I walk in God’s blessings every day. Amen! 1616
