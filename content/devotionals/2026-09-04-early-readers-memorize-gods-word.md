Memorizing the Scriptures is important for the child of God. This is how you carry God’s Word with you everywhere. The Bible says, “Your word I have hidden in my heart…” (Psalm 119:11). Even if your Bible isn’t in your hand, you can keep His Word in your heart.

When you memorize Scriptures, you fill your mind with God’s truth. Keep doing this until the Word becomes part of you. Then, when you speak, God’s Word in your mouth brings power, courage, and victory.

You can start by choosing one verse each day. Read it out loud, say it again and again, and think about what it means. Before long, you’ll remember it easily! As you keep God’s Word in your heart, it will guide you and make you wise.

## Confession

God’s Word is working in me mightily. As I meditate on His Word, I grow in wisdom, and I do everything excellently. Hallelujah!
