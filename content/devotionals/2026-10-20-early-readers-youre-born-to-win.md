As God’s child, you’re born to win. No matter what happens around you, you can live above fear and trouble because you belong to Jesus and share in His victory.

Jesus said He has given you authority over all the works of the devil, and nothing shall harm you (Luke 10:19). Keep this truth in your heart and mind.

So choose faith, not fear. Speak God’s Word and act on it. Remember, you’re safe, sound, and victorious every day because God is with you.

## Confession

My life is full of God’s glory and goodness. I live by His Word, and I walk in victory over fear, sickness, and every challenge. Amen.
