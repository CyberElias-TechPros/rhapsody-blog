Shine for God every day by being a good example to others. Your words, actions, and thoughts should show God’s love.

Think about Daniel. Even as a young boy in a foreign land, he stayed faithful to God. He prayed, helped others, and did what God’s Word instructed him to do (Daniel 6). Because of this, he became a shining example to his friends and everyone around him.

Being an example doesn’t mean trying to impress anyone. It means being the best version of yourself—that is, the person the Lord has made you to be. Be kind to your friends, speak nicely, and help others. Let God’s Word guide your choices and thoughts always.

## Prayer

I’m the light in my world, and my life is for God’s glory. Therefore, I manifest His love everywhere I go and bring praise to His name, in Jesus’ Name. Amen. 3939
