To compromise means to settle for something or to do something you didn’t plan to do. The Bible says that in the last days, some people will choose to do wrong and ignore God’s Word because others are doing it (2 Timothy 3:1–5). That’s why it’s important for you to always stand for what is right.

Sometimes, others may try to make you follow the crowd or do something you know isn’t good. As God’s child, always stand for what is right. Choose to do His Word, and do what pleases Him.

A good example is Daniel in the Bible. Even when others around him did wrong, he chose to obey God and did not compromise (Daniel 1:8). Like, Daniel, always stand for what is right, and let God’s Word guide your choices.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for your Word in me. It guides me in all my choices and decisions. In all I do, I stand for what is right, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
