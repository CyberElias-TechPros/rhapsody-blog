Awatchmaker once said that time is the only material he works with that cannot be repaired once wasted. A broken gear can be replaced; a cracked glass can be restored—but a lost moment is gone forever. In God’s eternal calendar, moments are weighty, pregnant with purpose, and charged with divine opportunity. Heaven values time, and so must you.

Time is one of the most precious resources you have, and the urgency of the hour demands that you use it wisely. The Lord Jesus Himself emphasized this truth when He said, “I must work the works of Him who sent Me while it is day; the night is coming when no one can work” (John 9:4). He revealed the narrow window we’ve been given to fulfil the Father’s purpose. There’s coming a time when the freedom we now enjoy to preach the Gospel will no longer be available. Doors that are open today may soon be shut. That’s why you must act with urgency.

Evangelism is not something to postpone. Delay can mean eternal loss for someone who needed to hear the truth from you today. That co-worker, neighbour, or friend may not get another chance. You are their light. You are God’s point of contact to bring them out of darkness into the glorious liberty of the sons of God. The Bible describes those without God and without Christ in this world as hopeless and doomed (Ephesians 2:12). But you are their hope.

This is not the season for complacent Christianity; it’s a time for bold, Spirit-led action. The nations are ripe for harvest, and heaven is counting on you. Be sensitive to the Spirit. Seize every moment. Let your life be aligned with heaven’s urgency. As the Apostle Paul declared, “Necessity is laid upon me; yes, woe is me if I do not preach the gospel!” (1 Corinthians 9:16). The night approaches, but for now, we shine. Every moment matters. Every soul counts. Do all you can—while you can.

## Speak

I recognize the urgency of the Gospel and I act swiftly. I am sensitive to divine timing, and I seize every opportunity to preach, to shine, and to win souls. I will not delay, and I will not be silent. Hallelujah!

## Act

Don’t wait for the perfect moment or until you feel “ready.” Start sharing the love of Jesus with just one person this week—maybe a classmate, a neighbour, or someone you often see but never talk to about Christ.
