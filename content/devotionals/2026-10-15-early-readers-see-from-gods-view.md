God wants you to look beyond what your eyes can see. This means trusting His Word and believing what He says, even when things look different. When you see this way, you stay confident in God’s promises.

In the Bible, John was told to “come up” so he could see from God’s view (Revelation 4:1). God’s Word helps you see that you’re special, healthy, and victorious in Christ.

The Bible says we walk by faith, not by sight (2 Corinthians 5:7). So, believe and act on God’s Word, and you’ll live in joy, success, and peace every day.

## Prayer

Thank you, gracious Father, for your Word. No matter what happens around me, I see victory, success, and the good life I have in Christ, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
