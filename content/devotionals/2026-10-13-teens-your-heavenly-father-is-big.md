There are no laws in heaven or on earth against dreaming big. So, as a Christian, you’re at liberty to dream and dream big. Let your dream of success be big enough for God to get involved with it. That’s why I love the man Joseph in the Bible. As a young lad, his dreams were out of this world! He was basically a teenager talking about leadership and influence while still living under his father’s roof. Though his brothers hated him for all his extraordinary dreams of greatness, he kept on dreaming and telling them about his dreams.

A teenager with a big dream can attract God’s attention. That’s why even in prison, Joseph kept rising to the top. He eventually became the prime minister of Egypt and preserved both Egypt and several other nations from famine. It’s time for you to dream and dream big. It’s a decision you must make. Dream big about your academics, your creative gifts, your tech ideas, your ministry, your influence.

Stretch your vision. Refuse to put your mind to sleep; keep dreaming! See yourself doing big things. Have a different way of thinking. Your Heavenly Father is big, and His resources are limitless. Not only is He able to cause your visions of success to come to pass, but He’s also more willing and enthusiastic about your success than you could ever be. Glory to God!

## Speak

My mind is continually open to receiving new ideas. I refuse to see myself as small in life, for the God I serve is the limit-breaker! I see my greatness, my increase and my influence bursting forth on every side, in the Name of Jesus! Amen.

## Act

Take a piece of paper and write down the biggest dream you can imagine for your life. Don’t limit yourself by your current circumstances. Make it so big that only God can accomplish it. Then, thank Him for making it happen.
