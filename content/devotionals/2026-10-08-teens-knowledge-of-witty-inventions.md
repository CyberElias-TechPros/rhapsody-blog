A young man once asked me, “How come many scientists have invented a lot of things for the world to enjoy, and Christians haven’t done anything?” The young man asked an intelligent question, but the mistake he made was that he saw Christians as one group and scientists as another group; he never thought of the fact that many scientists are Christians.

There’s no telling what a Christian who has received the Holy Spirit can achieve. He has the greatest Teacher living inside him, and there’s absolutely no subject in life He’s deficient in. As one who’s filled with the Holy Spirit, you have knowledge of witty inventions, for the Holy Spirit is the Spirit of wisdom. Therefore, you’ve got to blaze new trails and chart new frontiers in different fields of human endeavour.

The Holy Spirit living in you is your unparalleled advantage to be a trailblazer. He can grant you insights into fields that haven’t even been discovered yet! You can be the first to discover the cure for cancer, HIV/AIDS, and other medically incurable ailments. The world is looking for problem-solvers who’ll make new discoveries that’ll permanently solve the many social, political, and economic ills that are plaguing the world today. Don’t let your imagination go to sleep. Put the wisdom of God that’s functioning in you to work, and become a creator. Become an inventor. It’s time to break new grounds.

## Speak

I’m breaking new grounds and blazing new trails in my school, among my friends, and in the things I create. I don’t just follow the crowd. I lead with purpose. For I have the wisdom of God and the ingenuity of the Spirit working in me. I have ideas, solutions, and creativity that come from Him, and they set me apart.

## Act

Today, spend five minutes praying in tongues and listening for a creative idea from the Holy Spirit. It could be for your home, your school, or your city. Get a notebook and write down any ideas that come to you.
