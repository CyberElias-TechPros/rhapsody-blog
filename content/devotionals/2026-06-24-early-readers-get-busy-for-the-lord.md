Jesus loves everyone, and He wants everyone to have a relationship with Him. That’s why God wants His children to gladly do His work of telling others about Jesus. He wants you to be busy for Him. Being busy for the Lord means loving others, sharing the Gospel, and helping people know the Lord better.

Even though you’re young, you can still serve the Lord. You can pray for your friends, family members, and neighbours; be kind, obey your parents, and tell your friends about Jesus. When you do these things, God is pleased with you, and your efforts will be rewarded with resounding success. Hallelujah!

## Confession

I’m diligent in telling others about Jesus and doing the work of the Lord. I live each day preparing for the coming of the Lord, and bearing good fruits, in Jesus’ 3131 Name. Amen.
