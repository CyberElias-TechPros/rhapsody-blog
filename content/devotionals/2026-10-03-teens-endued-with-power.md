The first of the seven Spirits of God mentioned by the Prophet Isaiah in the scripture above is the Spirit of the Lord. That’s the Spirit of dominion. The Spirit of the Lord gives you boldness; He anoints you with power for service and puts you in charge of situations. This was what the prophets of old experienced whenever they had to declare God’s Word without fear or intimidation. The Spirit of the Lord came upon them and empowered them to dominate their circumstances.

When the Holy Spirit takes charge of your life, timidity becomes a thing of the past. He enables you to take charge of situations; He produces the boldness and dominion of God in you so you can do things that are consistent with God’s will. You’re not ordinary! God has called you to shine in your generation. Stand out, not by showing off, but by walking boldly in His power, even when your peers may be uncertain or doubtful.

Refuse to allow fear or intimidation. It’s time to walk in boldness and dominate your environment and take charge of the circumstances of your life. Don’t allow pressure from friends or any challenge to intimidate you. Every time you go out, say, “I go with the Spirit of dominion!” This is the

1010 day of the manifestation of the power of the Spirit; this is the hour of His glory; this is the set time for you to rule your world and reign in life! Walk in boldness and manifest the dominion of the Spirit. Glory to God!

## Speak

I have the Spirit of dominion! Nothing and nobody can intimidate me or stop my progress. Fear and timidity are completely banished from my life because the boldness of God is in my spirit! I’m reigning in life and dominating my world for the Lord Jesus. I manifest the glory of God’s Kingdom everywhere I go. Hallelujah.

## Act

Before you step out today, look in the mirror and declare: “I go with the Spirit of dominion! I’m not ordinary. I have the boldness of God in me, and I take charge of every situation I face today.” 1111
