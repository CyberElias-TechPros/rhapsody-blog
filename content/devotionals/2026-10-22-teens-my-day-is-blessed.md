No successful farmer walks onto unplowed, unseeded ground and looks forward to a harvest. Long before planting season, they prepare the soil, enrich it, and speak with confidence about what the land will produce. They understand that the condition of the ground before planting largely determines what comes up later. Your morning declarations are like preparing the soil of your day; you’re getting the ground ready to produce what you intend to grow.

In Christ, every day for you should be a day of joy, blessings, thanksgiving, and rejoicing. That means you should look forward to having a great day, every day! This is God’s will for your life. Our opening verse shows us just how we can walk in this reality; it says, “And God blessed (spoke good of) the seventh day….” This is a profound revelation of God’s truth! You ought to bless your day. This is possible because you’re created in the image and likeness of God, to live and function like Him. So, you ought to take a cue from the Lord and do what He does.

Psalm 68:19 says, “Blessed be the Lord, who daily loads us with benefits….” There are blessings and benefits for you every day; expect them and speak them forth! Don’t talk like unbelievers who say, “I’m having such a bad day.” Instead, take charge of your morning; bless your day and tell it exactly what to produce for you.

As you wake up, speak productivity and favour into your day. Declare that the Spirit of God is leading you to be at the right place at the right time. Declare that He’s opening up “main character” opportunities for you and giving you the wisdom to recognise and win all the way.

## Prayer

Heavenly Father, I thank you, for I receive all the favours and benefits you’ve ordained for me today. The lines are fallen unto me in pleasant places, and the land is subdued before me, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Say these words aloud, “My day is blessed! I walk in the supernatural and manifest the righteousness of God today. I walk worthy of the Lord this day, pleasing Him in every way, bearing fruit in every good work, and increasing in the knowledge of Him! Hallelujah!”
