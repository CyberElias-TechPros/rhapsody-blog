As God’s child, you don’t just get healed when you’re sick—you can live in divine health every day! That means no sickness can stay in your body because the life of God flows in you.

When Jesus died and rose again, He gave you this wonderful gift—His very own life, called Zoe. Zoe keeps you strong, healthy, and full of joy.

So, no matter how you feel, say, “I have divine health in me!” Keep speaking it, and you’ll stay healthy and full of life every day.

## Confession

I have God’s life in me. It keeps me strong, healthy, and full of energy every day. Hallelujah!
