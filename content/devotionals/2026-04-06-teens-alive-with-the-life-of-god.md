The Lord Jesus didn’t come to start a religion; He came to give us life—divine life! He gave up everything, even His own life, to make us sons of God. When you understand the depth of His sacrifice, it changes your entire perspective. You begin to live victoriously every day, knowing that His death, burial, and resurrection brought you an everlasting life—free from sin, sickness, poverty, and death.

The sacrifice of Jesus wasn’t a mere symbol; it was real, and so is the freedom it brought you. The Bible says, “Knowing that Christ, having been raised from the dead, dies no more. Death no longer has dominion over Him.” (Romans 6:9 NKJV). Glory to God! Death has no dominion over you either because you’re in Him.

Through His resurrection, He imparted His very nature to you. You’re now alive unto God! You’re not just forgiven—you’re recreated in righteousness. This is why you can boldly declare, “I’ll never be sick in my life! I’ll never be broke in my life!” These are not empty words; they’re the realities of your new nature in Christ.

The Bible declares, “They which receive abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness shall reign in life by one, Jesus Christ” (Romans 5:17). You’re not trying to “make it”; you’ve already made it in Christ! The old nature is gone; the new you is full of divine life.

So, live with this consciousness: you’re not a victim— you’re a victor! You’re not the defeated trying to win; you’re the one who already reigns in life through Christ Jesus. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Precious Father, thank you for the finished works of Christ, which has made me righteous, victorious, and alive unto you, free from sin, sickness, poverty and every oppression of the enemy. I live in the fullness of the life of Christ, manifesting your glory everywhere, in Jesus’ Name. Amen

## Act

Today, take a few minutes to declare boldly who you are in Christ! Say it out loud: “I have the life of God in me! I’m full of health, strength, and divine wisdom. I reign over sin, sickness, and poverty. Christ lives in me, and I live in victory every day!”
