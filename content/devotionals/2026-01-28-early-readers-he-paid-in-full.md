Do you know what it means to be debt-free? It actually means that you’re not owing anyone anything. That’s exactly what Jesus did for you! He paid for your sins in full!

Think about it: if someone paid all your school fees, would the school still ask you to pay? Of course not! Jesus paid the debt of sin on your behalf. Now, you’re completely free from sin. “For sin shall not have dominion over you, for you are not under law but under grace” (Romans 6:14).

Therefore, walk in the newness of life—free, forgiven, and full of God’s righteousness. Hallelujah!

## Confession

Thank you, loving Father, for the death, burial, and resurrection of Jesus Christ. Henceforth, I walk in your light, living a new and joyful life in Christ Jesus. Hallelujah!
