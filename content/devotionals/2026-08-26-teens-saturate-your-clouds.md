Look at the sky on a rainy day and notice how the clouds gather, heavy with moisture, before any drop falls. Rivers swell, crops grow, and life flourishes, all because the clouds were saturated before the rain came. Just as nature takes its time to prepare for the shower, the changes we seek often require patient preparation.

As a child of God, nothing is impossible with you. No matter how hopeless a situation may look, you can effect a change by consistently speaking God’s Word concerning that situation. Some people have found themselves in a situation where they were confessing God’s Word to change an unfavourable circumstance, but after a while, they became frustrated and gave up because, from the natural standpoint, nothing seemed to have changed. They even felt more depressed when those around them started asking mockingly, “You’ve been saying all that stuff from the Bible for weeks; why hasn’t anything changed?”

Perhaps you’re reading this today, and you’re in that situation; you feel like giving up; I have a word of encouragement for you: keep speaking your faith! Don’t give up on prayers just because you don’t see results yet. The Bible says if the clouds be full of rain, they empty themselves upon the earth (Ecclesiastes 11:3). You have to understand that rain doesn’t just fall from the sky; the clouds have to be saturated with moisture. When that happens, you see the thick, dark rain clouds gathering in the sky. Nobody needs to do anything extra—the rain will pour down naturally.

As it’s in the physical, so it is in the spiritual realm. If you hold on just a little longer, the rain of blessings you’ve been expecting will pour down on you. It may seem like nothing is changing while you’re speaking forth the Word of God, but those words are actually working in the spiritual realm. They’ll produce results by changing your situation.

So, keep saturating your clouds with your confessions! If someone asks, “Why hasn’t anything changed yet?” just smile and say, “I’m still loading up the clouds.” In your journey of faith, never give up! When challenges come, go to the Word. Meditate until you receive that personal message (Rhema) from God, and once you do, keep saying it over and over. That Word will grow strong and finally change your situation.

## Speak

Is there a situation you’ve been praying about that hasn’t changed yet? Don’t give up. Today, find 3-5 scriptures that speak to that situation. Write them down and speak them out loud with faith. Remember, if it didn’t work, it was not faith, because faith always works!

## Act

Think of one situation you’ve been praying about. Write down two specific faith confessions related to it and say them aloud at least three times today—morning, afternoon, and night.
