On the Day of Pentecost, something wonderful happened. Jesus’ disciples were filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in other tongues as He gave them words (Acts 2:4). This gift wasn’t only for them—it’s for all of God’s children, including you.

When you pray in tongues, the Holy Spirit helps you pray beyond your understanding. Even if you don’t know the meaning, God does. As you pray this way, you become stronger,As you pray this way, you become stronger, joyful, and full of faith.

So, pray in the Spirit often—anytime and anywhere. As you do, you’ll grow stronger and hear God more clearly.

## Prayer

Thank you, precious Father, for the gift of speaking in tongues. As I pray in the Spirit, I’m strengthened, and I walk in victory, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
