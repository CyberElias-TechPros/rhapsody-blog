Meditation is more than being quiet and thinking. It means softly saying Godʼs Word again and again until it fills your heart. As you speak the Word, it becomes real to you. It begins to guide you and make you bolder.

Thatʼs why God told Joshua to meditate on His Word day and night so he would prosper and make wise choices (Joshua 1:8). That instruction is for you too. The more you meditate and speak Godʼs Word, the more it guides you every day.

So, take time daily to study, speak, and think on Godʼs Word. As you do, itʼll shape you, strengthen your faith, and help you grow into the strong child God made you to be.

## Confession

All things are working for me, and I prosper in all that I do, because I’m blessed. Everywhere I go, I display God’s glory, blessing, and grace. Amen.
