God wants you to be kind and ready to help others. Even if someone is unkind to you, don’t stop doing good. God is pleased when you keep choosing what is right.

Jesus told a parable about “the Good Samaritan” (Luke 10:30–37). A man was hurt and left on the road. Some people saw him but walked away. But the Samaritan stopped, cared for him, and made sure he was safe.

You can do the same. Be kind, share, and help others every day. Keep doing good—God sees it, and your reward will come.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for blessing me and making me a blessing. I’m kind and ready to help others every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
