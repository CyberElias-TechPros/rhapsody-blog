Faith is your shield—it protects you from fear, doubt, and every wrong thought the devil tries to send.

When you feel worried or afraid, lift up your shield by speaking God’s Word. Say, “God is with me!” or “I’m more than a conqueror!” That’s how you block the enemy’s lies and stay strong in faith.

The more you hear and speak God’s Word, the stronger your faith becomes. So, keep your faith shield up always!

## Confession

My faith is strong! Therefore, I use God’s Word to stop fear, worry, and every lie of the devil. Hallelujah!
