In sports, coaches often teach athletes to smile or celebrate after a mistake or during physical exhaustion. This isn’t because the situation is funny, but because the physical act of celebrating can help interrupt the cycle of panic and fatigue. It gives them the strength to finish the race. There is a profound spiritual truth here: celebrating before the victory appears is actually a source of supernatural strength.

You may say, “What if the circumstances of my life aren’t such that should give room for rejoicing? What if school feels overwhelming? What if my friends misunderstand me, my grades drop, or things at home feel tense? How am I supposed to rejoice then?” That’s precisely the time to rejoice! Yes, your joy is independent of circumstances; it’s rooted in your spirit. It’s a supernatural delight that persists even when the storms rage. When faith is challenged, that’s the perfect moment to celebrate. The Bible says, “...with joy you will draw water from the wells of salvation” (Isaiah 12:3).

“Salvation” is an all-encompassing word; it refers to soundness, preservation, divine health, success, prosperity, and every blessing that the death, burial and resurrection of Jesus Christ consummated for the Christian. In other words, God wants you to know that you must keep your joy afloat to experience the wonderful blessings of salvation. So, even when exams are tough, peer pressure is loud, or the future feels unclear, you can still stand strong inside. Learn to maintain a joyful spirit even in the midst of daunting and trying circumstances, for the joy of the Lord is your strength(Nehemiah 8:10). Glory to God!

## Speak

I declare that the joy of the Lord is my strength, and that joy saturates my spirit and bursts forth in refreshing streams of rejoicing. I rejoice always in the Lord my God, who has filled my heart with gladness and my mouth with songs of rejoicing.

## Act

Today, set aside five minutes for a laughing session. Find a quiet place; meditate on all the good things God has done for you, and just start laughing. Laugh at the devil. Laugh at the challenges.
