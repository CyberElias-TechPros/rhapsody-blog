I love the Lord Jesus; nobody ever spoke like Him. His words were absolute and profound. In the scripture above, he said, “I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.” Notice He didn’t say, “I am a way” or “I am one of the ways”; rather, He said, “I am the Way!” Letting us know that He is the [only] Way; therefore, no man can get to the Father except by Him.

In a world where people say, “Just follow your heart” or “All roads lead to God”, Jesus made it clear that not all roads lead to God. Coming into a living relationship with Jesus by declaring Him as Lord of your life is the only God-ordained and acceptable means of salvation for all mankind!

The Bible says, “Nor is there salvation in any other, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved” (Acts 4:12). In other words, no other Name has been given, or will ever be given among men by which they can be saved, except the Name of Jesus. It’s not about trends, popular influencers, or world religions. Salvation is in Jesus alone.

That means salvation isn’t in the name of Buddha or Mohammed, or any other religious leader in the world. Therefore, as a teenager, you don’t have to wait until you’re “older” to have a real relationship with God. Let Him reign and rule in your life as Lord. Make Him the personality of your deepest affection, and He’ll give you that peace, joy and fulfilment that you desire in your life. Praise God!

## Speak

Dear Jesus, with my heart I believe, and with my mouth I declare that you’re Lord of my life and saviour of the world. You’re the Author of life, the Prince of peace, and King of the ages. I thank you for guiding me in the path of life and leading me in the way of supernatural success and permanent prosperity.

## Act

Today, preach the Gospel unapologetically. Share the truth with love and zero shame.
