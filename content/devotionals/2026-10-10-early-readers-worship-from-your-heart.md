Sometimes a song can make you feel excited, but excitement alone isn’t true worship. True worship is when your heart is focused on God, and you honor Him with your words.

Jesus explained this when He spoke to the woman at the well. He said true worshippers worship the Father in spirit and truth (John 4:23–24). This means your worship should come from your heart and be real.

So, when you sing or pray, think about God’s goodness, love, and greatness. Worship Him sincerely, and your life will show His love every day.

## Prayer

Thank you, gracious Father, for the opportunity to worship you in spirit and truth. I worship you with all my heart and glorify your Name, now and always. Amen.
