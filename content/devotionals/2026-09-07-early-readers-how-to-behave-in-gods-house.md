The Bible teaches us how to behave in God’s house. God’s house can mean three things. First, your body is God’s house because He lives in you. Second, the church family is God’s house. Third, the building where we gather to worship is also called God’s house.

As God’s child, your actions and words should show love and respect. One way to do this is by keeping your body neat and clean. The Bible says, “…present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to God…” (Romans 12:1).

Also, when you’re in church, stay focused, listen, learn, and worship. It’s a special time to pay attention and honour the Lord. Be kind and helpful to other children in church. When you behave this way, you show that God lives in you.

## Prayer

Dear gracious Father, thank you for teaching me how to live for you. I take care of my body, behave well in church, and treat other children of God with love, 12in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
