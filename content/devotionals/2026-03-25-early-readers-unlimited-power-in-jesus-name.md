The Name of Jesus is full of power! When you use it, anything that’s wrong must stop—sickness, fear, or trouble. That’s why Jesus said we can ask anything in His Name, and it’ll be done (John 14:13-14).

There’re no limits to what you can do when you use the Name of Jesus. You can pray for your friends, speak to situations, and see miracles happen.

That Name gives you authority over all things—use it boldly!

## Confession

I have unlimited power in the Name of Jesus! I use it with faith, and great things happen everywhere I go!
