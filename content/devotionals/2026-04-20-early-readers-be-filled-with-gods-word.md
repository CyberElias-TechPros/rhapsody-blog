Think about a device with a completely dead battery. Even if it has lots of amazing features, it can’t work until it’s charged. That’s how your life is without God’s Word. The more you fill your heart with the Word, the more supercharged you become—full of power, wisdom, peace, and answers!

God created the whole world by His Word (John 1:1–3). And now, that same powerful Word lives in you! So, let God’s Word shape the way you think, the way you speak, and the way you live. When you speak the Word with faith, it produces results.

Don’t let your Bible stay closed. Open it every day. Read it, meditate on it, and speak it. As you do, you’ll walk in victory every single day. Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for the power of your Word in me. Your Word fills my heart, guides my steps, and produces what it talks about in my life, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
