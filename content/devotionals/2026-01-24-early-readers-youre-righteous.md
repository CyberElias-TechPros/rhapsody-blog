God is righteous; righteousness is His nature. Think about it: God can never be wrong. He’s always just, right, and perfect. That’s why He’s God! And you know what’s amazing? You’re just like Him! He gave you His nature of righteousness the moment you were born again. It’s His free gift to all His children.

For example, Abraham was called righteous because he believed God (Genesis 15:6). Righteousness gave him boldness to walk with God and receive His blessings.

That’s what righteousness does for you too— it gives you the confidence to stand before God without fear, guilt, or shame. So, never think or feel unworthy of God’s blessings and goodness because you’re God’s righteousness in Christ Jesus!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you, for giving me the gift of righteousness to stand before you without fear or guilt. Therefore, I’m bold and confident every day because your nature is in me. Hallelujah!31
