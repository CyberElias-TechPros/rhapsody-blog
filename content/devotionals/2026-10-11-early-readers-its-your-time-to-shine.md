God has made you His light in the world. Light shines and pushes away darkness. So, even when things seem scary or confusing, you don’t have to be afraid—Shine, because you’re God’s light!

The Bible says, “Arise, shine; for your light has come…” (Isaiah 60:1). This means it’s your time to shine. When you believe God’s Word, do what is right, and show kindness, you shine brighter.

People will see Jesus in you and learn about Him. So, be bold and let your words and actions show His love everywhere you go.

## Confession

I am the light of the world. God’s glory is seen in me and through me. I bring hope, peace, and joy to my school, family, and community. Hallelujah!
