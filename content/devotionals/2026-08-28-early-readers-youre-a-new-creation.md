The moment you received Jesus, something amazing happened—you were born again. This means you became a new creation in Christ. God gave you a brand-new life through His Spirit. Your life now comes from Jesus, the Lord from heaven.

Even though you may look the same on the outside, something wonderful has changed inside you. You now belong to God’s family and carry His life in you. Because of this, you can live with joy, confidence, and victory every day. Always remember who you are—a new creation with God’s life shining in you.

## Confession

I’m a new creation in Christ. God’s life is in me, and I live in joy, victory, and strength every day. Hallelujah!
