Now that you’re born again, the Lord Jesus isn’t just around you or near you; He’s in you! Hallelujah! This means sickness, fear, and anything not of God has no place in your life.

Your life is filled with joy, health, peace, and victory every day. And guess what? It’s not reliant on where you live or what country you live in. Hallelujah!

So, walk in the consciousness of who you are in Christ Jesus and what He’s done for you through His Word. Don’t see yourself as an ordinary person. Rather, see yourself as a child of God— full of His glory because Christ is in you!

## Confession

I’m special because Christ lives in me! His power, beauty, and glory shines through me every day. Therefore, I live in victory and show His greatness wherever I 34go. Hallelujah!
