I once heard of a farmer who carefully fenced his vineyard, not to restrict the vines, but to protect them. Within those boundaries, the plants flourished and yielded abundantly. In much the same way, God has given us His Word as our “fence”—not to limit us, but to preserve us in divine health, strength, and victory.

Too many live at the mercy of sickness—hospital today, pharmacy tomorrow—tossed back and forth in a cycle of weakness This shouldn’t be your experience as a child of God. Don’t allow such in your life; refuse to let Satan toy with your body. The moment he notices you yield to minor symptoms, he attempts to escalate them. But praise God, you have the power to stop him!

Remember the words of the Master to Simon Peter: “Simon, Simon! Indeed, Satan has asked for you, that he may sift you as wheat. But I have prayed for you, that your faith should not fail” (Luke 22:31-32 NKJV). The Master didn’t pray that Peter wouldn’t be tested but that his faith would not fail. That’s the same with you today—your faith is the victory that overcomes the world!

Don’t settle for a life of recurring ailments. If you’ve been in and out of school because of health issues, you can decide today that “enough is enough.” Declare boldly: “I refuse to live in sickness and weakness. I have eternal life, and therefore I walk in divine health every day.”

While as a result of ignorance you might have tolerated sickness in the past, it should no longer have a place in your life now that you know the truth. Don’t ignore pain, hoping rest will solve it. No—release your faith through words. Your tongue is your instrument of victory! Use it to rebuke infirmity, command wholeness, and establish divine health in your body. Every time you speak God’s Word, you are setting your world in order. Speak life, speak strength, speak health! The Holy Spirit has empowered you to reign over sickness, disease, and every work of darkness.

## Speak

Dear Father, I thank you for the gift of divine health, my inheritance in Christ Jesus. I’m healthy and strong, and I walk in the fullness of the divine life. Every fibre of my being is inundated with eternal life. Hallelujah!

## Act

Speak words of health, wholeness and wellness over your body now.
