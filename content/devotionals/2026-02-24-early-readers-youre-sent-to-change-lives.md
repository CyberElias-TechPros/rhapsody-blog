Jesus gave you a very special job—to tell the world about Him and help others live for God! You’re not just a child; you’re a world-changer.

Every time you pray for someone, show kindness, or tell someone about Jesus, you’re changing lives and making heaven rejoice. Angels love to see you at work for God!

So, be proud of your calling. You’re God’s champion, filled with His Spirit to make a difference in your world every day.

## Confession

I’m sent by God to change lives! Through me, others see His love, power, and glory. Hallelujah!
