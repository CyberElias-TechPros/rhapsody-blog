Have you ever wondered what you would do if

Jesus suddenly appeared in front of you? Would

you shout for joy, kneel down, or just stand

amazed? Whatever you do will come from what

is in your heart—your spirit.

Think about Saul, who later became Paul. He

was on his way to hurt Christians when suddenly,

a bright light from heaven shone around him.

He heard Jesus speak, but because his heart was

full of anger and confusion, all he could say was,

“Who are you, Lord?” (Acts 9:5). His response

showed the condition of his spirit at that moment.

That’s why it’s so important to fill your heart with

God’s Word—by reading your Bible, listening to

God’s teachings, and praying in tongues every

day. When God’s Word fills your heart richly,

your spirit becomes trained to hear, recognize, Scan toand respond to God the right way.

## Confession

My heart is full of God’s Word; therefore, I’m always attentive to His voice, and I respond the right way, every time!
