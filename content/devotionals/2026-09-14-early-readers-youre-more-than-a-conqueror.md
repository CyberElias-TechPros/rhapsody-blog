Did you know that you always win in Christ? The Bible says, “…thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ” (1 Corinthians 15:57). This means you’re not a loser—defeat isn’t for you. God has already given you the victory.

So, no matter what happens at school or anywhere else, remember that the One in you is greater than the one who is in the world (1 John 4:4).

Think about David. He won his battles because he trusted in God (1 Samuel 17:45–47). But you have something even greater—the Holy Spirit lives in you!

So, keep thinking about this truth. Be bold and fearless, you’re more than a conqueror. Glory to God!

## Confession

I have the life of God in me, and I’ve overcome the world. I always win, make progress, and excel in all I do. Amen!
