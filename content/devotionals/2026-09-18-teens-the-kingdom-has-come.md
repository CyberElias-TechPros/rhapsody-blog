I Jesus gave them a format; He said, “In this manner, therefore, pray: Our Father in heaven, Hallowed be Your name. Your kingdom come...” (Matthew 6:9-10). He taught them to pray in expectation of the coming Kingdom of God because at the time, He hadn’t died, and the world was being dominated by another kingdom, which is the kingdom of darkness.

However, after His death, burial, and resurrection, when He met His disciples, His message to them changed. He said to them: “All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth. Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations...” (Matthew 28:18-19). All the authority in the universe was vested in Jesus, and He’s given us that authority as His Kingdom representatives. He wants you to go ahead and manifest His kingdom to the world. No wonder He said in Luke 17:21: “...indeed, the kingdom of God is within you.” There’s no need to pray for the Kingdom to come anymore, for that prayer has been answered in us, the new creation.

If you’re born again, you’ve become the carrier of the Kingdom of God; the Kingdom is in you! When you walk into your school, your home, or your sports practice, it means God’s Kingdom has arrived there. Hallelujah! The world has suffered for too long from the influence of Satan’s kingdom, and it’s up to us now to dominate this world with the principles of God’s Kingdom.

Rise to your calling as a kingdom-carrier; don’t allow the devil run things around you. You’ve been given the highest authority in the universe, the Name of Jesus, to use in ruling over the devil and negative circumstances. You can put a stop to sickness, war, death, poverty and all the other corrupting influences of the kingdom of darkness. You can superimpose peace, righteousness, love, joy, health and prosperity in your own sphere of contact. Glory to God!

## Speak

I’m a carrier of God’s Kingdom and an ambassador for Christ. I carry that divine presence and influence of God wherever I go, dispelling the forces of darkness and superimposing the principles of God’s kingdom in my world. I fulfil my God-given mandate of making students of the nations, even as I reveal to them the victorious life in Christ, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Walk into your school or home today and silently declare: “The Kingdom of God just arrived here with me. Things are about to shift.”
