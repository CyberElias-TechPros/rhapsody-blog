One day, a woman who had been sick for twelve years touched Jesus’ clothes, believing she’d be healed. Right away, she got well! Jesus said, “…your faith has made you well...” (Mark 5:34).

That same kind of faith works today. When you believe God’s Word, miracles happen. It doesn’t matter how big the problem seems—your faith in God always brings results.

So, whenever you pray or believe for something, don’t doubt. Keep your heart full of faith, and you’ll see God’s power at work in you!

## Confession

My faith is alive and working! I believe God’s Word, and it brings healing, peace, and joy to my life. Hallelujah!
