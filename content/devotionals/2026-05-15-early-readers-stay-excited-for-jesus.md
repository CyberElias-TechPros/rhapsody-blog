When Paul shared the Good News about Jesus, he did it with great joy and excitement. He didn’t need anyone to push him. He loved Jesus so much that he was always happy to tell others about Him (1 Corinthians 9:16).

That’s how God wants us to be, too—happy and excited to serve Him.

Sometimes people may say you’re “doing too much for God” and advise you to slow down. But don’t let that stop you. Keep your love for Jesus strong. Whether you’re sharing God’s Word, helping someone, or praying for your friends, do all with a joyful heart.

Remember, you’re not doing God’s work alone. God is working through you!

So stay excited, keep loving Jesus, and never let anything put out your fire for Him.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for choosing me and making me a blessing to others. Your power is at work in me every day, helping me share the Good News with those around me, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
