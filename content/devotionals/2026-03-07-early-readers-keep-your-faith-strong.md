Faith is like a strong muscle—you have to use it for it to grow. When you study God’s Word and pray in the Spirit always, your faith becomes strong and active.

Don’t wait for a problem before you start exercising your faith. Speak God’s Word every day. When you say, “I’m strong in the Lord,” or “I live in health,” your faith gets stronger.

Just like charging a battery, praying in tongues keeps your spirit full of power. The more you do it, the bolder and stronger your faith becomes!

## Confession

I build my faith every day through God’s Word and prayer. My faith is strong, active, and full of power.
