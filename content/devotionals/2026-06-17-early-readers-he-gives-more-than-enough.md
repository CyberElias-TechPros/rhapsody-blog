Did you know God loves to take care of

you? Sure, He does! Remember Elijah’s story?

He had enough, even during a famine. When

there was no food in the land, God sent

him to a widow’s house, and her jar of flour

and oil never ran out (1 Kings 17:14). God

doesn’t just give “a little;” He gives more than

enough! God supplied for Elijah every day!

That’s how God treats you too. He has

blessings, ideas, help, strength, and grace

waiting for you each day. That’s why Psalm

68:19 says “Blessed be the Lord, Who daily

loads us with benefits...”

So, instead of worrying about what you

need, speak God’s Word as you declare:

“God supplies everything I need. I always get children’shave more than enough!”

## Confession

Thank you, Lord, for blessing me every day. You load me with benefits each day. Today, I have more than enough because you supply all I need. Amen! 2424
