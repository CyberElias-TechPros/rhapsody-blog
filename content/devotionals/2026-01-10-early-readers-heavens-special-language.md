Have you ever heard people from different countries speak? Their languages and accents can sound very different! But when God’s children pray in tongues, it’s like we all speak the same language—because we do! It’s a language from Heaven.

Speaking in tongues is a special gift from God. It’s not an earthly language from any country—it’s given by the Holy Spirit. That’s why, no matter where we live or what language we speak every day, when we pray in tongues, our voices sound united. We’re all speaking Heaven’s language from God’s Spirit.

So, speak in tongues often! It helps you talk to God in a deeper way, builds up your spirit, and fills you with joy and strength. Be like Apostle Paul, who said he spoke in tongues more than anyone else!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for the gift of speaking in tongues. As I pray in tongues, I’m strengthened, filled with joy, and in perfect tune with you, in Jesus’ Name. Amen!15
