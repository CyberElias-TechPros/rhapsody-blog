A ransom is the full payment made to rescue someone and set them completely free. And that’s exactly what Jesus did for you! He paid the full price so you could be free from sin, guilt, fear, and everything that could separate you from God.

Jesus said, “...the Son of Man did not come to be served, but... to give His life a ransom for many” (Mark 10:45).

That means He gave His life for you— willingly and lovingly. Isn’t that wonderful?

Because Jesus paid this price, God doesn’t see you as guilty or unworthy. No! In Christ, you’ve been washed, made righteous, and brought into God’s family. You’re clean, holy, loved, and precious to Him.

So always remember that Jesus is your ransom. He made peace between you and for the Lord every day. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for your great love shown through Jesus Christ. Because He paid the full price for me, I stand before you holy, clean, and without blame. I live every day rejoicing in your righteousness, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
