Jesus didn’t come like a movie superhero with special effects or gadgets. He came as God in human form! He took your place on the Cross, died for your sins, was buried, and then rose again. Everything He did was because He loves you—and the whole world.

That’s why this is truly the greatest and most exciting story ever!

Long before Jesus came, God spoke through Moses and the prophets about a Savior who would rescue the world (Deuteronomy 18:15). Jesus is that Savior! He fulfilled God’s promise—and now, because you’re in Christ, you’re the wonderful result of that promise!

And now that you’re a part of this beautiful story, God wants you to share it too. When you tell others about Jesus—His love, His sacrifice, and His resurrection— you’re spreading the greatest news in the world.

## Prayer

Dear gracious Father, thank you for giving us Jesus. As I boldly share His story of love with everyone I meet, their hearts are opened to receive it, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
