Did you know that when you pray, something beautiful happens in heaven? The Bible says your prayers rise up to God like sweet-smelling incense.

In Bible days, priests burned incense as part of worship. Today, your prayers are like that incense before God. When you talk with Him, thank Him, or pray for others, your words rise to Him like a sweet fragrance.

King David understood this when he said, “Let my prayer be set before You as incense…” (Psalm 141:2). God loves to hear your prayers, so keep praying always because He plans to answer. Your prayers are precious to Him.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, you’re worthy of my praise and worship. As I pray and give thanks, my words rise to you as a sweet fragrance, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
