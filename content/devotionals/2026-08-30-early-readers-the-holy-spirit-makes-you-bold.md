The Holy Spirit gives God’s children boldness. Before Peter received the Holy Spirit, he was afraid and even said he didn’t know Jesus (Matthew 26:69–75).

But after he was filled with the Holy Spirit, there was a great change. Peter became brave and spoke about Jesus in front of many people. The same Holy Spirit lives in you today. He helps you be courageous when you want to tell others about Jesus.

Maybe you want to invite a friend to church or talk about God at school. The Holy Spirit helps you speak with confidence and joy. With Him in you, you can always be bold.

## Prayer

Precious heavenly Father, thank you for the Holy Spirit who lives in me. He makes me bold and joyful to share the Good News of Jesus everywhere I go, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
