The Bible isn’t just an old book of stories— it is God’s special Word to you! Every time you read it, God’s Word comes alive in your heart to change you, strengthen you, and make you everything it talks about. That’s why the Bible calls it “the Word of His grace.”

The Word of His grace is God’s power working inside you. When you believe it and speak it in faith, it makes you bold, courageous, wise, and excellent—at home, in school, and everywhere you go!

Timothy grew up loving God and His Word, and it made him wise and strong (2 Timothy 3:15–17). Just like Timothy, fill your heart with God’s Word every day, and you’ll live a life of excellence. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear precious Father, thank you for your Word that builds me up and makes me wise. I’m victorious and successful always, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
