When you were born again, you became a king and a priest in God’s Kingdom. That means you’re not meant to struggle or live in fear—you’re meant to reign!

Kings don’t beg or complain. They speak with confidence and lead with authority. As God’s child, your words have power. You can speak peace, healing, and success wherever you go.

So, always think and talk like a king. Walk tall and full of joy, knowing that Jesus has made you royalty. Hallelujah!

## Confession

I’m a king and a priest in Christ. I walk in victory and rule over challenges every day. Hallelujah!
