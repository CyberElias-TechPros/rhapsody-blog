The Name of Jesus is the most powerful Name in the whole world! When you use His Name, all of heaven backs you up.

Peter used that Name when he told a man who couldn’t walk, “In the name of Jesus Christ, rise up and walk!” And immediately, the man jumped up and walked (Acts 3:6–8). That same Name works for you today!

So, whenever you pray, heal the sick, or declare God’s Word—do it in the Name of Jesus. His Name always brings victory.

## Confession

In the Name of Jesus, I acknowledge that I have power over sickness, fear, and every problem. I live in victory every day. Hallelujah!
