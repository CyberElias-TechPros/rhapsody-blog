To be filled with the fullness of God is to carry within you the very essence of divinity—the totality of His being, His wisdom, and His glory. This is not a promise reserved for heaven; it is the present reality of every child of God. In Christ, you are replete with God.

The Scripture uses the Greek word “Plēro”ō to describe this fullness. It conveys the idea of a vessel bearing the maximum load, full and running over. That is who you are: completely inundated with the divine nature. There is no room for fear or weakness; you are full of God.

The Lord Jesus lived in this consciousness. He declared, “...For as the Father has life in Himself, so He has granted the Son to have life in Himself” (John 5:26 NKJV). The same life of the Father flowed through Him in its fullness. Now, that same life has been imparted to you.

God’s plan is for the Church to walk in this very consciousness—fully awakened to our divine origin and expression. At the rapture, He will present to Himself a glorious Church, not one escaping the world in fear, but one triumphant, radiant with His glory, and mature in the expression of His divine life.

Therefore, refuse to think small or live as though you are ordinary. You are the dwelling place of the Almighty, the visible expression of His invisible presence. Carry this awareness everywhere you go. Let the fullness of God in you find expression in your words, your decisions, and your conduct.

## Prayer

I’m filled with the fullness of God, with the totality of His divine essence, His very being. Christ in me is the hope of glory; I’m full of His power, wisdom and glory, and I walk in the consciousness of this truth. I live victoriously, fulfilling God’s purpose for my life. Amen.

## Act

At intervals today, quietly affirm, ‘I am full of God’s wisdom and glory,’ and let that consciousness guide your thoughts words and actions.
