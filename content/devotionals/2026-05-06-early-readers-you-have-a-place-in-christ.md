After Jesus rose from the dead, He went to heaven and sat in the highest place—the right hand of God (Colossians 3:1). And here’s the excellent news: Jesus didn’t take that place only for Himself—He got it for you too! That’s the place He referred to in John 14:2.

The Bible says that now, because you’re in Christ, you’re seated with Him in heavenly places (Ephesians 2:6). That means you share in His victory, His authority, and His glory.

So when you pray, speak, or act in the Name of Jesus, remember where you belong. Jesus represents you in heaven, and you represent Him here on earth. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear heavenly Father, thank you for giving me a place in Christ—a place of victory, authority, and joy. I live and walk in this truth every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
