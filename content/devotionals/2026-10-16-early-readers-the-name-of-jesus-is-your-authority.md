The Name of Jesus isn’t ordinary— it’s your authority. When you use the Name of Jesus, it’s like Jesus Himself is speaking. The Bible says God gave Jesus a Name above every name (Philippians 2:9-11). This means everything listens to His Name.

In the Bible, Peter said, “…in the Name of Jesus…rise up and walk,” and the man was healed (Acts 3:6-8). That same power is given to you!

You can use the Name of Jesus when you pray, when you’re faced with a difficult task, or when you need help with anything. Use the Name with faith and boldness, knowing that His Name works. Remember, you have victory through the Name of Jesus every day.

## Prayer

Dear precious Father, thank you for giving me the authority to use the Name of Jesus. As I live and use the Name, I see changes in and around me, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
