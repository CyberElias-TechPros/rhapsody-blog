To be consecrated means to be set apart specially for God. In the Old Testament, Aaron and his sons were chosen and set apart to serve God as priests (Numbers 3:3). But today, every child of God is chosen and set apart for God.

This means you belong to God and are special to Him. You’re not like the rest of the world. So, live like someone who belongs to God—do what is right, speak truth, and show love.

Always remember who you are and enjoy the life of joy, health, and victory God has given you.

## Prayer

Thank you, loving Father, for setting me apart for your glory. I belong to you, and I live to please you every day. Amen.
