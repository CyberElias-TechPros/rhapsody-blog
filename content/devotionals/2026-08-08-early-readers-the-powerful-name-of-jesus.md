The Name of Jesus is very powerful. God has given Him a Name that is greater than every other name.

The Bible says that at the Name of Jesus, every knee should bow in heaven, on earth, and under the earth. This means nothing is greater than His Name. When you pray in the Name of Jesus, you’re using the authority He has given you. That’s why we say His Name with faith and respect.

Whether you’re praying, thanking God, or helping others know Him, remember that the Name of Jesus carries power, victory, and salvation.

## Confession

The Name of Jesus is powerful. I speak His Name with faith, and I live in victory every day. Hallelujah!
