Imagine walking into a dark room and suddenly, a single candle is lit. Imagine walking into a dark room, and suddenly, a single candle is lit. That small flame reveals what was hidden, guides your steps, and brings clarity. This simple picture reveals a profound truth about your identity in Christ. You are that light! Not just any light, but the light of the world.

The Lord Jesus declared, “You’re the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hidden” (Matthew 5:14 NKJV). This is not a suggestion; it’s a divine command. God’s glory in you is meant to radiate through your words, your love, and your conduct. Your life as a Christian is designed to expose deception and to illuminate the path of righteousness for others.

Do not hide your faith or dim your light. Let your actions and your love reflect the transforming power of Christ within you. You are His epistle, written not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God (2 Corinthians 3:3). Even those who have never read the Bible can read Christ in you through your life.

Isaiah 60:1 NKJV commands, “Arise, shine; for your light has come! And the glory of the Lord is risen upon you.” This is your call to action! God has made you a burning and shining light. So stand up, shine brightly, and illuminate your world with the truth, grace, and righteousness of the Gospel. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for making me the light of the world. I shine brightly in my world today, illuminating lives with your love, truth and grace. My light draws men to Christ and glorifies you always, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Find one way to shine today—whether it’s kindness, courage, or a simple smile. Let your light change someone’s world!
