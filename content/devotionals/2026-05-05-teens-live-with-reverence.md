After learning about patience, the next virtue the Bible says to add to your faith is godliness. When you think about godliness, what comes to your mind? For many, it sounds like an old religious word, but it’s one of the most beautiful expressions of our life in Christ. The Greek word is “eusebeia,” and it means piety—reverence, devotion, and deep respect for God.

Godliness is living every day with the consciousness that you’re in the presence of God and in the presence of angels. You’re never alone; the Holy Spirit is in you everywhere you go. That awareness changes how you think, speak, and act. You become conscious to do what’s right and excellent.

Some people are careless and irreverent in their conduct. They talk anyhow, act anyhow, and forget that they carry God’s presence. But you’re different. You’re born again, and the Spirit of God lives in you. So, bring piety into your life. Let reverence shape your character. That’s what godliness means. As you grow in Christ, you start building a character of excellence. There are things you stop doing— not because they’re necessarily wrong, but because they’re not excellent. You stop speaking certain ways, dressing certain ways, or acting without grace. Why? Because you represent God!

You’ve been called to a life of honour, discipline, and excellence. When others cut corners, you choose integrity. When others are loud and disrespectful, you speak with grace. When others live to please themselves, you live to please God.

Remember, godliness isn’t about pretending to be spiritual; it’s living consciously in His presence. You carry His glory, so let it show in your words, your attitude, and your relationships. This is the life that produces true success and productivity—a life that reflects the very nature of God. Hallelujah!

## Speak

The nature of God is fully expressed in me. I’m godly, righteous, and full of the Spirit. My words are gracious, my actions are pure, and my life is a reflection of His glory. I live every day with reverence and devotion to God, walking in love and producing fruits of righteousness. Glory to God!

## Act

Let your reverence for God be evident in your actions today. Walk in love, maintain purity, and choose righteousness.
