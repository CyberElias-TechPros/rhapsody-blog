Prayer is very important, and God listens to us whenever we pray. He is willing to answer us too. The Bible shows that people also prayed at night. During those moments, they poured out their hearts to Him.

Praying at night means taking quiet time to talk to God. Remember, it’s quieter at night. So, you can tell Him how you feel, thank Him for His goodness, and tell Him your requests without the noisy distractions of daytime.

Prayer isn’t only for daytime. You should pray before you sleep, and trust God to bring whatever you’ve prayed about to pass. He loves and cares for you, and always hears you when you call on Him!

## Prayer

I pray that all children all over the world live for the Lord, and that they are safe from bad influences in the world, in Jesus’ Name. Amen. 3535
