Sometimes, life places us in situations we don’t fully understand at first. We find ourselves in certain families, schools, workplaces, or communities, wondering why we’re there and what difference we can really make. Yet, many of these moments are not random at all; they’re part of God’s loving and purposeful plan.

This was true for Queen Esther. She lived in the palace enjoying comfort and favour, unaware that her position carried a greater responsibility. It was only when Mordecai spoke to her that she realized God had placed her there for a reason—to stand up at a crucial time and make a difference for her people. Esther discovered she was born for such a time as that.

There is nothing accidental about your life. Predestination is at work in you. Your birth, your placement, your opportunities, and your assignment in the Kingdom are all divinely orchestrated. You were born for such a time as this—strategically positioned by God to influence your world for His glory. God placed you in your family, workplace, city, and nation as a soul winner and a Kingdom ambassador. Everything about you—your education, talents, resources, and personality—has been entrusted to you for Kingdom use. Therefore, don’t withhold your voice when it’s time to speak. Don’t retreat in fear when faith demands action. Live unto Christ and fulfil His purpose for your life.

The Bible says, “And He died for all, that those who live should live no longer for themselves, but for Him who died for them and rose again.” (2 Corinthians 5:15 NKJV). Your number one job on earth is clear: you’ve been given the ministry of reconciliation (2 Corinthians 5:18). Nothing must hinder your passion for spreading the Gospel.

Let your light shine where and when it matters most. Like Esther, boldly declare, “If I perish, I perish,” because living for the Lord is worth everything. You’ve been trained and called for this moment. Now is the time to act and say like Paul, “But none of these things move me; nor do I count my life dear to myself, so that I may finish my race with joy, and the ministry which I received from the Lord Jesus, to testify to the gospel of the grace of God.” (Acts 20:24 NKJV).

## Speak

Blessed Father, thank you for choosing and positioning me for such a time as this. I am bold, ready and willing to fulfil your purpose for my life. I declare that my light shines brightly, and I make lasting impact for the Kingdom wherever I go.

## Act

Today, ask the Holy Spirit to show you one way to stand for Christ where you are, and boldly act on it.
