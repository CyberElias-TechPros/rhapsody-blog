Have you ever wanted to tell someone about Jesus? That’s a wonderful thing to do! But before you speak, there’s something very important you should do: pray.

The Bible teaches that the Holy Spirit guides people into truth (John 16:13). He is the One who helps them understand God’s Word. No matter how clearly you explain it, He is the One who opens their hearts. When you pray for someone before sharing the Gospel with them, you’re asking God to help them understand, believe, and receive His love.

So, always pray for the people you talk to about Jesus. As you do, the Holy Spirit will work in their hearts, and God’s Word will produce fruit in them.

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, I pray for every child I meet to share the Good News with today. I declare that you help them to understand and love your Word, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
