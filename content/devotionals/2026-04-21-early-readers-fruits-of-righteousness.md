Did you know that just like a tree is known by the fruit it produces, your life also shows who you are by the things you say and do? An orange tree can only grow oranges—not apples! In the same way, because you’re God’s child, your life produces fruits of righteousness.

Jesus said, “I am the vine, you are the branches...” (John 15:5). That means He’s the main tree, and you’re connected to Him like a branch that gets its strength and life from Him. Everything Jesus did—healing the sick, helping the hurting, speaking loving words—were fruits of righteousness. And the Bible says, “...as He is, so are we in this world” (1 John 4:17).

That means you can show the same love, kindness, joy, and goodness everywhere you go because His life flows through you!

So, let your life shine with good fruits every day—so others can see Jesus in you.

## Confession

I’m a tree planted by God. Therefore, I grow strong and healthy, and my life produces good fruits of love, joy, and kindness for everyone to see. Amen!
