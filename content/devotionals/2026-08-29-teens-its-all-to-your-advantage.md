Have you ever felt like life is totally unfair? Like, no matter what you do, things just keep going wrong? Maybe friends have let you down, school is tough, or your dreams feel so far away. That’s what we’re discussing today.

If you ever find yourself in a difficult situation where things look bleak, don’t despair; rather, rejoice, because that seemingly negative situation is part of the grand process for your promotion. Joseph’s brothers sold him as a slave in Egypt (Genesis 37:28), but from prison, Joseph rose to become the Prime Minister of Egypt. No wonder the Bible says, “count it all joy when you fall into various trials” (James 1:2), because the result is certain: they’ll turn out for your good!

Think about this: everything around you—your friends, your school, even social media—can somehow work in your favour when you trust God. Just like the stars in their courses fought for Deborah against her enemies (Judges 5:20), the things in your world can line up to help you win. Even in moments that feel totally out of your control, God has your back. Remember Joshua commanding the sun and moon to stand still (Joshua 10:12-14)? God made creation work for Joshua’s advantage. In the same way, God is setting things up in your life, even the stuff that seems random or frustrating, to make sure you win.

God has ordained your life, so no challenge can truly overwhelm you. When trials and pressures come, face them with the mindset of someone who already has the advantage. Praise God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for this wonderful life you’ve brought me into. I rejoice to know that I function from the position of advantage every day. Every situation I face is an opportunity for my promotion, as you’ve ordained all things to work always together for my good. Amen!

## Act

Whatever happens today, even if it seems unfair, declare aloud: “This is working for my good and promotion!” Thank God for it.
