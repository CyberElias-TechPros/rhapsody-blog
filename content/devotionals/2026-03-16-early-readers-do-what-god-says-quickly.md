At a wedding, the organizers ran out of wine. Mary, Jesus’ mother, said to the servers, “Whatever He (Jesus) tells you to do, do it”. They obeyed. They filled the pots with water, and right away, a miracle happened—Jesus turned the water into wine!

This shows us something powerful: whenever you act quickly on God’s Word, you’ll see His power at work. Don’t wait or doubt—just do what God’s Word says!

Be quick to obey God’s Word and listen to the voice of the Holy Spirit. That’s how miracles happen!

## Confession

I’m quick to obey God’s Word. I act on it right away, and His glorious power is revealed in my life every day.
