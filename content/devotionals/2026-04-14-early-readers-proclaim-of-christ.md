When Jesus was on earth, He went everywhere doing good and healing people (Acts 10:38). One day, He healed a woman who had been bent over for eighteen years! With just one touch, she stood up straight again (Luke 13:10–13). What a miracle!

The Bible says Jesus came to destroy the works of the devil (1 John 3:8). Those works include sickness, sin, fear, pain, and death. And through His death and resurrection, Jesus defeated them all—completely! That means you now have victory every day. Hallelujah!

So, if anything tries to trouble you—fear, sickness, or worry—don’t accept it. Speak up boldly and say: “Jesus has given me life, health, peace, and victory!”

That’s how you proclaim the victory Christ has already won for you.

## Confession

I have the life of God in me. My life is full of health, strength, joy, and victory every day. Hallelujah!
