The moment you were born again, God gave you a special job: to share His love and tell others about Him. But do you know something? Your friends or classmates don’t just listen to what you say; they also watch how you live. Your actions can speak even louder than your words.

If you tell others to be kind, you must be kind too. If you say Jesus is loving, your life should show love. The Bible says God has made us ministers to serve Him (2 Corinthians 3:6). That means He helps you live the right way.

As you study and do God’s Word, it changes your character. Then, when people see you, they’ll see the love, joy, and goodness of Jesus shining through you.

## Prayer

Dear precious Lord, in all I do, your love, goodness, righteousness, and kindness are expressed through my words and actions to everyone in my school, neighbourhood, and community, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
