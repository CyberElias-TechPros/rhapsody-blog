Ajeweller once held up a tiny, uncut stone that looked ordinary to the untrained eye. It wasn’t shining, wasn’t polished, and didn’t seem impressive. Yet he smiled and said, “This is priceless.” Why? Because he knew what it would become once refined. Many people look at men and women through the lens of appearance, status, or behaviour, but God sees something far deeper—eternal worth.

To God, one soul is worth more than all the wealth, resources, and power in this world. That truth may sound staggering, but it defines Kingdom values. The Bible declares, “For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son…” (John 3:16 NKJV). The Lord Jesus didn’t die for systems or structures; He died for people. Every soul you encounter carries eternal value, irrespective of their background, choices, or present condition.

The true price of a soul is revealed in what was paid for it—the precious blood of Jesus Christ. The Scriptures say we were redeemed not with silver or gold, “…but with the precious blood of Christ…” (1 Peter 1:18–19 NKJV). That means every person you meet is worth the blood of God’s Son. Think about that! When you understand this, your perspective changes. You no longer see people as interruptions, inconveniences, or statistics; you see them as treasures.

When the value of a soul becomes real to you, evangelism becomes a love-driven mission. You’ll pray with compassion, speak with urgency, and reach out beyond your comfort zone. Heaven itself rejoices over one sinner who repents (Luke 15:7). If all of heaven celebrates one soul, how much more should we labour for many?

Therefore, make soul winning your habit and lifestyle. Deliver the souls of men from darkness into light and from the power of Satan unto God. Nothing could be more fulfilling.

## Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank you for opening my eyes to see people the way you see them. Thank you for helping me appreciate and recognize the true value of a soul; this makes my heart burn with compassion for the lost as I passionately preach the Gospel, delivering them from the domain of darkness into your marvellous light, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

## Act

Today, pray for at least one person by name who doesn’t yet know Christ. Share the Gospel with them and invite them to church tomorrow.
