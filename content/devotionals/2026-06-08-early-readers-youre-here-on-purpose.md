Queen Esther didn’t realize that God put her in the palace for a special reason. But when her uncle, Mordecai, explained what was happening, she understood that her place as the Queen wasn’t just for comfort; it was part of God’s plan to rescue the Jews (Esther 4:14).

Just like Esther, God placed you in your family, school, and community so you can shine for Jesus. Your gifts, talents, and personality were given to you so you can help others know God’s love (2 Corinthians 5:18).

So be bold! Let your light shine. Like Paul, choose to finish your God-given assignment with joy (Acts 20:24)!

## Confession

I’m not an accident! I was born to bring glory and praise to God’s Name. Therefore, I’m bold and ready to shine brightly everywhere I go, in Jesus’ Name. Amen. 1313
