You are not an accident—never! God made you on purpose and for a special reason. He created you to show His goodness, love, and glory to the world. The Bible says you are chosen, special, and precious to Him (1 Peter 2:9). That means your life is meant to shine and help others see how wonderful God is.

Jesus said, “You are the light of the world...” (Matthew 5:14). Just like Joseph, who kept trusting God even while in prison and later became a great leader, God can use you wherever you are to show His greatness.

So, walk with joy and confidence every day, knowing that God made you wonderfully and lovingly to reflect His beauty and light. Hallelujah!

## Confession

I am God’s special creation. He made me to show His love and goodness. Through my life, others see God’s light and are blessed. Hallelujah!
