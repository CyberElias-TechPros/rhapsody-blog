God wants you to live a happy, blessed, and successful life—and one of the reasons is so you can be a blessing to others! The Bible says, “I will bless you… and you will be a blessing” (Genesis 12:2 GW). That means you don’t always have to look for help; instead, God wants you to be the one showing love, care, and kindness to those around you.

Joseph is a perfect example. Even when people treated him wrongly and things seemed hard, he still chose to help others. God used him to save an entire nation— and even his own family (Genesis 39–41). His kindness and faithfulness made a big difference!

Today, choose to be God’s helping hand by showing love to your friends, family, classmates, and anyone who needs care. Every act of kindness you show is like doing it for Jesus Himself!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for blessing me so I can bless others. I always have more than enough to give, and I show your love and kindness everywhere I go, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
