As God’s child, you’re not ordinary. You’ve been chosen to reign in life! That means you’re a king and a priest in Christ Jesus. Kings don’t beg or live in fear—they rule with authority.

God wants you to think, speak, and act like royalty because that’s who you are. When you talk, your words carry power. When you pray, things change. You’re meant to walk in victory, not defeat!

So, walk tall and bold every day, knowing you’ve been born to reign in life with Christ.

## Confession

I’m a king and priest in Christ Jesus. I reign in life and display God’s glory everywhere I go!
