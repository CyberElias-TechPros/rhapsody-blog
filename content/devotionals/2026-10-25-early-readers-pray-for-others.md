God loves everyone and wants all people to be saved (2 Peter 3:9). That’s why He wants you to pray for others—your friends, classmates, and children around the world.

In the Bible, Elijah stood for God even when others didn’t listen. He encouraged the people to turn back to God (1 Kings 18:21). He didn’t give up.

You can do the same in your own way. Pray for your friends, show them kindness, and tell them about Jesus. As you do this, you’re helping others know God and receive His love.

## Confession

I’m ready and excited to pray for others and share God’s love. Through me, many children will know Jesus. Amen.
