God doesn’t just tell you what to do—He gives you the power to do it! The Holy Spirit inside you fills you with strength, courage, understanding, and the desire to do what pleases God.

In the Bible, God gave Ezekiel an important message to deliver. At first, Ezekiel felt weak and unsure. But when the Holy Spirit entered him, he became strong and bold, and he spoke God’s message with confidence (Ezekiel 2:2).

The same Holy Spirit who strengthened Ezekiel lives in you right now!

So, when the Holy Spirit gently nudges your heart to pray, help someone, give, forgive, or share God’s Word, respond with joy. He’ll guide you, empower you, and help you do great things for the Lord every day!

## Prayer

Dear loving Father, thank you for helping me do what pleases you. I choose to listen to the Holy Spirit and follow His guidance every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
