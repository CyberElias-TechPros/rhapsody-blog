Jesus often spent time praying. He talked with the Father with faith, knowing He was heard.

His disciples noticed this. They saw that Jesus prayed regularly and that His prayers made a difference. So, one day, they asked Him, “...Lord, teach us to pray...” (Luke 11:1). They wanted to learn how to talk to God the right way.

You can pray like Jesus, too. When you pray with faith and trust in God, your prayers make a difference. They bring changes, blessings, and grace to your life, and to others around you.

## Prayer

My prayers are powerful and effective. As I pray in faith, I see answers and positive changes in my life and in the lives of others, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
