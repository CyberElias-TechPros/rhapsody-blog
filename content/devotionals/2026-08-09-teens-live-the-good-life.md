Every ecosystem on earth depends on order. When the seasons run their normal course, crops grow well, rivers flow steadily, and animals can find the food they need to survive. But when that order is disrupted, even strong systems begin to struggle. In the same way, life often produces its best results when we live according to the order established by God.

Everything God created was good and especially made for your comfort and provision. That’s why the Word says all things are yours to enjoy! But sadly, some young Christians believe they have to suffer and settle for the lesser things in life. They’ve been taught that it’s wrong to look good, and it’s better to be poor on earth and go to heaven than to be rich on earth and go to hell. Being rich and looking good have nothing to do with going to hell or heaven—including enjoying your youth responsibly.

The Bible says in 2 Corinthians 8:9, “For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though He was rich, yet for your sakes He became poor, that you through His poverty might become rich.” Jesus suffered that we might not suffer; He became poor that we might become rich; He brought us out that He might take us in. He tells us in Isaiah 1:19,“If you are willing and obedient, you shall eat the good of the land...” This means your life becomes enjoyable when you decide to walk in obedience to the will of God.

Obedience is an act of submission to the leadership of God. When you obey God’s command, you come under His authority. As such, He’s empowered to act on your behalf. But when you disobey, you break His hedge of protection around you, and you give the enemy the chance to strike.

Start from where you are and respond to God’s Word today. Be conscious that indeed all things are yours and that He’s given you all things that pertain to life and godliness. Walk in obedience so you will enjoy all He has given to you and made available for you. How much you enjoy life depends on you. Start today!

## Speak

I’m an obedient child of God. I’ve obeyed the Gospel, and I do the Word. Because I’m willing and obedient to the Word, I eat the good of the land, and I walk in health and prosperity. Hallelujah!

## Act

Name one thing you desire, whether small or big, and declare: “All things are mine; I receive God’s best in this.”
