God made the world by His Word, and He made you to speak just like Him. That means your words have power! The Bible says, “...we also believe and therefore speak” (2 Corinthians 4:13). This shows that faith talks!

When you want to change a situation, don’t complain or worry. Rather, say what you want to see! God spoke light when the world was dark, and light appeared. You can do the same—speak health, peace, success, and joy into your life.

When you meditate on God’s Word, it fills your heart and gives you the right words to speak. Every time you declare God’s Word, you’re creating the future you desire. So, keep saying good things over yourself and others, and watch your words come alive!

## Prayer

Precious heavenly Father, thank you for giving me your Word and teaching me to speak like you. My words are full of power; I declare peace, health, and victory in my life every day, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.15
