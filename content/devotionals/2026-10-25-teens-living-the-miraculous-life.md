The life and ministry of the Lord Jesus was an unending stream of the miraculous. He fed five thousand people with a little boy’s lunch, raised the dead, walked on water and did so many other mind-blowing miracles which are recorded for us in the gospels. These weren’t exaggerated stories or legends; they were real moments where God’s power showed up in amazing ways. Though Jesus has gone to heaven, He’s left the Holy Spirit here in the earth (John 14:16) to continue the work of the Father through us.

He said in John 20:21, “...as the Father has sent Me, I also send you,” meaning our lives ought to be an unending stream of the miraculous as well. You ought to expect miracles and participate in the miraculous since the Holy Spirit lives in you. He’s the power of the Godhead! He’s the doer and performer of miracles. Jesus said, “...the Father who dwells in Me does the works” (John 14:10); that’s the Holy Spirit! He’s the one that indwells us with the miracle-working power of God.

You don’t have to wait until you’re older to see God move. He can work through you right now. This is your set time to receive that miracle you desire. The Bible says, “...as He (Jesus) is, so are we in this world” (1 John 4:17). In other words, as He is supernatural, so are we. Hallelujah! If He had a daily experience of the miraculous, then the story should be no different with you. Recognise that the Holy Spirit is working in you, with you and around you, though you may not see Him with your optical eyes. That means a miracle can take place in you, around you and through you, in any place, at any time! Glory to God!

## Prayer

Dear Father, thank you for making it possible for me to experience your miraculous power in my life and in the lives of those around me. Thank you for channelling that power towards me, and also for making me a medium through which that power can flow to touch others. My life is an unending stream of the miraculous because the Holy Ghost lives in me! Amen.

## Act

Expect something miraculous to happen today. Be ready to perform a miracle, for the power is in you. When you see it, give God the glory.
