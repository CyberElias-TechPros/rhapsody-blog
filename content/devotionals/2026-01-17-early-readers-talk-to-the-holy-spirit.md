Did you know you can talk to the Holy Spirit every day, just like you talk to your best friend? Yes, you can! He lives in you, helps you, and guides you in all things.

That’s why it’s important to make a habit of talking to Him often. The Holy Spirit is always with you, and He loves to talk with you too.

You can talk to Him about everything and anything—your homework, your friends, or even your plans. Be confident that He’ll guide, comfort, and help you live a great, and beautiful life. Hallelujah!

## Prayer

Dear Holy Spirit, thank you for living in me. Thank you for teaching, guiding, and helping me every day. As I listen to you, I live in joy, victory, and success always, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.
