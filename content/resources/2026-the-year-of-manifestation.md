## New Year’s Eve Service 2025



## Rev Chris Oyakhilome DSC DSC DD

Prophecy The word of the Lord that He gave was tested and tried. The word of the Lord came to pass, the word of the Lord stood tests, the word of the Lord was victorious. But the word was gracious and a great blessing to many. The word was sent to all who would receive it. Throughout the world, the word sounded strong and powerful knocking at the heart of men to receive and its light did shine; its voice was loud. And as many as received him, to them He gave power to become sons of God, even to those who believe on His name... And even today, His voice stands strong to the ends of the earth: His angels are out reaching out to many, that they may come into the kingdom while it is yet time and while it is yet today. His life flows out of love to all, for God loves all. He despises no one, not even the worst of sinners. For Jesus died to save sinners. His blood was divine, shed for many for the remission of sins and all those who call on him are saved. So says the Spirit of God.

## Message For 2026

As we entered into 2025, I shared with you a message for 2025 with a mandate: and that mandate was we would complete the preaching of the gospel of Jesus Christ to every nation on earth. And I want to congratulate all of God's children all over the world. Because there are so many denominations in Christianity, many think that those subtle divisions really do divide us, no, not near as much as you think; there is more unity among us Christians than the world may assume. We recognize the love of Christ far more across the board than many may realize and our love for one another irrespective of our denominations is what has sustained the church till this time, whether Catholic, Baptist, Anglican, Methodist, etc. We do agree who Jesus is to us and to God; we do agree on his work, His mission on earth and the love He put in our hearts for one another. We have continued the preaching of the gospel to all the world and most didn't know how far we had gone because they had many assumptions. Tonight, you would be amazed at where many are gathered like this all over the world. In India we had the largest gathering of Christian leaders so far. It wasn't a conference of so many speakers, they came knowing they will be listening to only one speaker; that was the love of God and Unity in manifestation. And that unity will not leave them: they will grow from strength to strength and glory to glory. This year we will see leaders of the churches of Christ around the world come together and unify, speak with one voice like never before. Jesus said by this shall all men know you are my disciples when you love one another. I give you one commandment: love one another as I have loved you. That love is a sign to the world that He sent us; but that love is shed abroad in our hearts by the Holy Ghost. I did say to you that once we are done by 31st of December 2025, we would move into extra time. Extra time is a very touching thing and moving hour that each one of us must be conscious of. Romans 11:25 KJV, NIV Until the full number of the gentiles has come in. In the teaching of the word of God, He lets us know that there is a definite number that He is taking from across the gentile nations. According to God's divine plan, salvation was to go from Israel to the rest of the world. We are in the extra time. What is the extra time? It is the race for the last man. Who is the last man or woman to come in? How do we know? This year 2026, we are going to be reaching out; maybe the person you are leading to Christ may be the last one. The day of the revelation of the Christ to Israel is at hand. The day of His appearing is at hand. Now that we have taken the message to all the nations, it's person to person now. Maybe that man or woman is in your yard, family, street, someone who works with you. Who is that last person? The extra time is the race to find that last lost soul. When you reach out to others, let them know how important this is. Nothing has been as accurate as the prophetic word through the spirit of God and this has been since bible days, even our world as it is today was prophesied long ago. I did tell you that the globalist agenda was under suspension. It is still under suspension in 2026 because it was full of fallacies and deceptions. This year 2026 is the year of Manifestation. First is the manifestation of the truth. Phanerosis (Greek) 2 Corinthians 12:7, 2 Corinthians 4:2 It is not given but first acquired and lived. Many experience the manifestation of the spirit in the gifts of the spirit listed in the Bible; but not many have experienced what is called the manifestation of the truth. It's the demonstration of the realities of God. They must become revelations to your spirit; they must live in you. Ephesians 1:17 The word Revelation there is also translated Manifestation. As God's people have learned the demonstration of the gifts of the spirit, they will come into the reality of the demonstration of the truth. This is a higher level of one's walk with God. This higher level is what the Lord is calling you up to, He says Come up Hither. The second thing, Apokalypsis, which means revelation. Romans 8:19 NIV. The creation waits eagerly for the sons (Huios) of God to be revealed. These are mature sons of God who have learnt to walk with God and have left the babyhood stage. Enoch walked with God and God took him. The sons of God are going to walk with God and are going to be manifested in this 2026 in a way that the world has never known before. It is called THE DISCLOSURE. That's another word for Apokalypsis John the Baptist was called to DISCLOSE to Israel who Jesus was; that was his calling in life. That's why I am telling you this through the prophetic voice because it's not the kind of things the world knows. You only get to know things like this through the spirit of God. You will witness the manifestation of the sons of God and you will be a part of it. When you look at the Bible, something often follows the manifestation of the sons of God. In the Old Testament, they were representatives of the kingdom and bearers of God's message. Like the prophets. Abraham, Noah, Moses; when they were manifested or angels were manifested, something followed. They often came for a sign for an era that was about to close and what followed was Judgement. Our world is in a state where the evil thereof is so much, life in so many quarters has become so cheap. So, they do population management what you would call in reality mass murder, and they do everything to ensure that both males and females can no longer have children. A lot of food and simple edible commodities that a lot of people will have, have become poisons today. Side talk: A lot of the sparkling water that many of you drink contains fluoride; you have to actually be sure that it doesn't. Sometimes it is listed on the content that is attached to the label but often it isn't listed. They were poisoning people knowingly. The reason there is so much cancer today is because many are taking poisonous substances without their knowledge. You need to be very careful of what you're consuming. When the Lord said it's the year of Manifestation; as simple as that came... I observed something. Douay Bible (Catholic Bible): 1 Corinthians 1:4-7 Vs 7: So that nothing is wanting to you in any grace waiting for the manifestation of our Lord Jesus Christ. The coming of Jesus Christ is called the manifestation of Jesus Christ. The word "Coming" was translated from the word apokalypsis. That's a code..what that might signify, I leave to you, only God knows but I have shared it with you. You will only know that it didn't exactly mean that after 2026, As long as 2026 is on, please keep this before your eyes. He gives us codes, and when he gives us codes, we receive them and we walk by them. I don't think this has happened anytime in history that you know. I'm not sure you ever had anybody relate this with you as a Christian, that means it's significant, so take this as an important warning in your life. There are too many things that we coming together to give us an understanding such that we should not ignore this. Ignore it if you know better than the scriptures, but I warn you not to ignore it. Keep it in your heart and live by that warning through the year. The Nations are expecting and they are looking up; their answers will not come from the works of the flesh but their answers will come from the guidance of the spirit: the manifestation of the sons of God. This is the unveiling and revelation. This year, you will see that you are the answer to the cries of many and you will provide the answer because the answer is in you. You will be empowered to help the world and help the Nations because the nations are waiting for you. The whole creation is groaning in travail, waiting for the manifestation of the sons of God. May the spirit of God guide you in these days to fulfill His will; may God guide you to the perfection of His will in your life.
